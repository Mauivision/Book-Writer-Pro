## 📐 **Technical Specifications**

### **Required Sizes**
- **Amazon KDP**: 2560 x 4096 pixels (2:3 ratio)
- **Kobo**: 1600 x 2400 pixels (2:3 ratio)
- **Apple Books**: 1400 x 1873 pixels (3:4 ratio)

- **Web/Thumbnail**: 400 x 600 pixels

### **File Formats**
- **High-res**: PNG or TIFF (300 DPI)
- **Web**: JPEG (72 DPI)
- **Print**: PDF or TIFF (300 DPI)

### **Color Requirements**
- **RGB**: For digital/online use
- **CMYK**: For print (if doing print version)
- **Color Profile**: sRGB for digital

## 🎨 **Design Elements Guide**

### **Background (Deep Space)**
- **Colors**: Deep blues (#0B1426, #1A2332), purples (#2D1B69, #4A148C)
- **Elements**: Stars, nebula clouds, cosmic dust
- **Mood**: Mysterious, vast, inviting

### **Jump Gate (Central Element)**
- **Colors**: Bright amber (#FFB000), gold (#FFD700), white (#FFFFFF)
- **Style**: Geometric, otherworldly, glowing
- **Position**: Center or slightly off-center
- **Effect**: Glowing, pulsing light

### **Stellar Wind (Foreground)**
- **Colors**: Metallic silver (#C0C0C0), white accents
- **Style**: Realistic spaceship, approaching the gate
- **Position**: Lower third, approaching the gate
- **Scale**: Small enough to show vastness of space

### **Typography**
- **Title Font**: Modern, clean, sci-fi appropriate
  - Examples: Orbitron, Exo, Rajdhani, Space Grotesk
- **Author Font**: Elegant, readable serif
  - Examples: Playfair Display, Crimson Text, Libre Baskerville
- **Colors**: White or light colors for contrast
- **Effects**: Subtle glow or shadow for readability

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Design Process**

### **Step 1: Setup (30 minutes)**
1. **Choose your tool** (Canva recommended for beginners)
2. **Create new project** with book cover template
3. **Set dimensions** to 2560 x 4096 pixels
4. **Import your chosen images**

### **Step 2: Background (45 minutes)**
1. **Add space background** image
2. **Adjust colors** to deep blues/purples
3. **Add nebula effects** or clouds
4. **Ensure good contrast** for text

### **Step 3: Central Elements (60 minutes)**
1. **Add jump gate** image or create geometric shape
2. **Apply glow effects** (amber/gold colors)
3. **Add Stellar Wind** spaceship image
4. **Position elements** for good composition

### **Step 4: Typography (45 minutes)**
1. **Add title text** "Remembrance of the Moon"
2. **Choose sci-fi font** and adjust size
3. **Add author name** with elegant font
4. **Apply text effects** (glow, shadow, outline)

### **Step 5: Final Touches (30 minutes)**
1. **Adjust overall contrast** and brightness
2. **Ensure text readability** against background
3. **Check composition** and balance
4. **Export in required formats**

## 🎯 **Design Tips for Success**

### **Composition Rules**
- **Rule of Thirds**: Place key elements on intersection points
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Title should be most prominent
- **Balance**: Distribute visual weight evenly
- **Contrast**: Ensure text is readable against background

### **Color Psychology**
- **Blue**: Trust, stability, technology
- **Purple**: Mystery, spirituality, transformation
- **Gold/Amber**: Wisdom, enlightenment, discovery
- **White**: Purity, clarity, hope

### **Typography Tips**
- **Readability**: Test at thumbnail size
- **Hierarchy**: Title > Subtitle > Author
- **Spacing**: Adequate space between elements
- **Effects**: Subtle effects enhance, don't overwhelm

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid**
- ❌ Too many elements competing for attention
- ❌ Text that's hard to read
- ❌ Poor contrast between elements
- ❌ Inconsistent font choices
- ❌ Overly complex design
