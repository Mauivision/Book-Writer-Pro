## 🎯 **Challenge Rules & Guidelines**

### **Core Rules**
1. **Write Every Day** - No exceptions, even if it's just 100 words
2. **Track Everything** - Log daily progress and insights
3. **Stay Focused** - Work on one project during the challenge
4. **Celebrate Wins** - Acknowledge every achievement, big or small
5. **Learn & Adapt** - Adjust your approach based on what works

### **Flexibility Guidelines**
- **Minimum Daily Words**: 100 words (on tough days)
- **Ideal Daily Words**: [DAILY_WORD_GOAL] words
- **Maximum Daily Words**: 3,000 words (to avoid burnout)
- **Rest Days**: 1-2 per week (if needed)
- **Catch-up Days**: Use weekends for extra writing

- **Primary Goal**: Write consistently for 30 days
- **Secondary Goal**: Complete [TARGET_WORD_COUNT] words
