## 🚨 **Critical Success Tips**

### **Cover Art Selection**
- **Look for sci-fi experience** - Don't hire a romance cover designer
- **Check portfolio quality** - Make sure their style matches your vision
- **Ask for revisions** - Most designers include 2-3 rounds of revisions
- **Get all formats** - High-res, web, thumbnail versions

### **Editor Selection**
- **Genre experience matters** - Sci-fi has specific conventions
- **Check references** - Ask for client testimonials
- **Timeline is crucial** - Book early, good editors fill up
- **Sample edit** - Many editors offer sample edits for $50-100

### **Communication**
- **Be specific** - Clear briefs get better results
- **Ask questions** - Don't assume anything
- **Set expectations** - Timeline, budget, deliverables
- **Get contracts** - Protect both parties
