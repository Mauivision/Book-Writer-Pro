- **Format**: PNG or JPEG

### **Other Platforms**
- **Kobo**: 1600 x 2400 pixels

- **Print**: 300 DPI resolution

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Process (30 Minutes)**

### **Step 1: Setup (5 minutes)**
1. **Go to Canva.com** (or your chosen tool)
2. **Create account** (free)
3. **Search "book cover"** in templates
4. **Choose "Science Fiction"** category
5. **Select blank template** or closest match

### **Step 2: Background (10 minutes)**
1. **Upload space background** image
2. **Resize to fit** template dimensions
3. **Adjust colors** to deep blues/purples
4. **Add overlay** if needed for text readability

### **Step 3: Add Elements (10 minutes)**
1. **Add jump gate** (geometric shape or image)
2. **Apply glow effect** (amber/gold color)
3. **Add spaceship** image (small, approaching gate)
4. **Position elements** using rule of thirds

### **Step 4: Typography (5 minutes)**
1. **Add title text** "Remembrance of the Moon"
2. **Choose Orbitron font** (or similar sci-fi font)
3. **Add author name** with Playfair Display
4. **Apply white color** with subtle glow/shadow

## 🎯 **Quick Design Tips**

### **Composition**
- **Place jump gate** in center or slightly off-center
- **Position spaceship** in lower third
- **Keep title** in upper third
- **Use rule of thirds** for balance

### **Colors**
- **Background**: Dark blues/purples
- **Accent**: Bright amber/gold
- **Text**: White with subtle glow
- **Contrast**: High contrast for readability

### **Typography**
- **Title**: Large, bold, sci-fi font
- **Author**: Smaller, elegant serif
- **Spacing**: Adequate space between elements
- **Readability**: Test at thumbnail size
