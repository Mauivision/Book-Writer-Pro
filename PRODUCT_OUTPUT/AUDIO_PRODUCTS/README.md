## 📋 Package Contents

Each audio package includes:

### 🎧 Audio Files
- ✅ **High-Quality Audio** (WAV, FLAC for master)
- ✅ **Distribution Formats** (MP3, M4B, AAC)
- ✅ **Multiple Bitrates** (128kbps, 256kbps, 320kbps)
- ✅ **Chapter Markers** (for easy navigation)

### 📝 Scripts & Documentation
- ✅ **Narration Scripts** (formatted for voice actors)
- ✅ **Character Voice Profiles** (voice direction)
- ✅ **Sound Effects Cue Sheet** (timing and placement)
- ✅ **Production Notes** (technical specifications)

### 🎨 Marketing Assets
- ✅ **Audio Clips** (30s, 60s, 2min samples)
- ✅ **Cover Art** (audio-specific designs)
- ✅ **Promotional Copy** (descriptions and blurbs)
- ✅ **Social Media Assets** (waveform graphics, etc.)

### 📊 Quality Assurance
- ✅ **Audio Quality Reports** (technical analysis)
- ✅ **Narration Reviews** (performance feedback)
- ✅ **Final Approval Checklist** (production standards)

## 🔧 Technical Specifications

### Audio Standards
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit or 24-bit
- **Channels**: Mono or Stereo (as appropriate)
- **Format**: WAV (master), MP3 (distribution)

### Quality Requirements
- **Noise Floor**: -60dB or better
- **Dynamic Range**: Appropriate for content type
- **Consistent Levels**: -3dB peak, -12dB RMS average
- **No Clipping**: Clean audio without distortion
