# SHADOW REALMS - Production Requirements & Timeline

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Project:** Shadow Realms Feature Film
**Genre:** Dark Fantasy Epic
**Runtime:** 120-140 minutes
**Budget Range:** $40-60M (Mid-budget fantasy)
**Production Strategy:** Practical effects priority with selective CGI enhancement

## PRE-PRODUCTION PHASE (12-16 WEEKS)

### Month 1-2: Development & Planning

**Week 1-4: Script Finalization**
- Complete screenplay polish and dialogue adjustments
- Legal review and clearance research
- Budget breakdown by scene and department

**Week 5-8: Casting & Creative Team Assembly**
- Principal cast auditions and negotiations
- Director, cinematographer, and key creative hires
- Production designer and concept artist briefings

**Week 9-12: Pre-Visualization**
- Storyboard creation for all major sequences
- Pre-vis animation for complex action scenes
- Location scouting and technical reconnaissance

**Week 13-16: Technical Preparation**
- Special effects testing and practical creature design
- Virtual production setup and testing
- Costume and makeup concept finalization

### Key Milestones:
- Script lock by end of week 4
- Principal cast attached by end of week 8
- 50% of storyboards complete by end of week 12
- Technical proofs of concept approved by end of week 16

---

## PRODUCTION PHASE (20-24 WEEKS)

### Principal Photography Structure

**Unit 1: Light Realm & Village Sequences (4 weeks)**
- Location: Rural exteriors, practical village sets
- Focus: Character introduction, attack sequence
- Cast: Elara, villagers, initial shadow creatures

**Unit 2: Shadow Realm Transition (3 weeks)**
- Location: Forest/practical shadow realm sets
- Focus: Realm crossing, Thorne introduction
- Cast: Elara, Thorne, shadow creatures

**Unit 3: Rebel Encampment (6 weeks)**
- Location: Purpose-built shadow encampment set
- Focus: Character development, training sequences
- Cast: All principals, rebel community

**Unit 4: Action & Climax (5 weeks)**
- Location: Veil breach practical set with extensions
- Focus: Final confrontations and resolution
- Cast: All principals, enhanced creatures

**Unit 5: Pickups & Inserts (2 weeks)**
- Location: Various as needed
- Focus: Close-ups, magical details, establishing shots

### Production Logistics:
- **Shooting Schedule:** 5-day weeks with 12-hour days
- **Weather Contingencies:** Indoor alternatives for exterior-dependent scenes
- **Second Unit:** Action sequences, creature work, visual effects plates
- **Rest Periods:** Built-in downtime for cast and crew recovery

---

## POST-PRODUCTION PHASE (16-20 WEEKS)

### Editorial (8-10 weeks)
- **Assembly:** Rough cut completion within 4 weeks of wrap
- **Fine Cut:** Director's cut locked by week 8
- **Picture Lock:** Final approved cut by week 10

### Visual Effects (12-16 weeks parallel to editorial)
- **CGI Enhancement:** Shadow creatures, constellation effects, magical elements
- **Compositing:** Realm transitions, practical effect augmentations
- **Motion Capture:** Shadow Councilor performance integration

### Sound Design (8-10 weeks)
- **Sound Edit:** Dialogue, effects, atmospheres
- **Music Composition:** Original score development
- **Final Mix:** 5.1 surround sound optimization

### Color Grading & Finalization (4 weeks)
- **DI Process:** Color timing for realm consistency
- **HDR Mastering:** Enhanced dynamic range for streaming
- **Quality Control:** Technical standards verification

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### Camera & Lighting Package
- **Primary Cameras:** ARRI Alexa 65 for main unit, RED Komodo for second unit
- **Lenses:** Anamorphic primes for epic scope, spherical zooms for action
- **Lighting:** LED practicals mixed with traditional tungsten/HMI
- **Grip Equipment:** Extensive for shadow manipulation effects

### Special Effects Requirements
- **Practical Creatures:** Puppet/makeup combinations for close interaction
- **Pyrotechnics:** Magical energy bursts, safe shadow effects
- **Atmospherics:** Mist machines, wind effects, particle generators
- **Rigging:** For shadow architecture movement

### Visual Effects Pipeline
- **Software:** Maya, Houdini, Nuke for primary VFX work
- **Motion Capture:** Stage setup for Shadow Councilor
- **Scanning:** LIDAR for location digitization
- **Render Farm:** Cloud-based for efficiency

### Sound Requirements
- **Recording:** Double-system sound with booms and lavaliers
- **Foley:** Custom shadow movement sounds, magical effects
- **Music:** 80-piece orchestra for epic score
- **Mixing:** Dolby Atmos capable facility

## RISK ASSESSMENT & CONTINGENCIES

### Primary Risks
1. **Weather Delays:** Exterior-dependent shadow realm sequences
2. **Technical Challenges:** Practical shadow effects complexity
3. **Cast Availability:** Principal actor scheduling conflicts
4. **Budget Creep:** VFX scope expansion

### Mitigation Strategies
1. **Indoor Alternatives:** Shadow realm stages for weather protection
2. **Extensive Testing:** Pre-production effects validation
3. **Flexible Scheduling:** Built-in buffer days for technical issues
4. **VFX Supervision:** Dedicated oversight to control scope
