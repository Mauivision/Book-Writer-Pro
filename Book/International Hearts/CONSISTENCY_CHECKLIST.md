# ✅ INTERNATIONAL HEARTS - Consistency Checklist

## 👥 **CHARACTER CONSISTENCY**

### **David Thompson**
- ✅ Name: Always "David Thompson" (never "Dave" or "David T.")
- ✅ Age: 35 at story start
- ✅ Occupation: Senior Architect, Competition Judge
- ✅ Physical: 6'1", dark brown hair, blue eyes, scar above left eyebrow
- ✅ Background: Widower (Sarah died 3 years ago), from Seattle
- ✅ Personality: Professional, emotionally guarded, passionate about architecture

### **Aiko Sato**
- ✅ Name: Always "Aiko Sato" (with honorifics: "Aiko-san" when appropriate)
- ✅ Age: 28 at story start
- ✅ Occupation: Graphic Designer (NOT architect - she's a designer entering architecture competition)
- ✅ Physical: 5'3", black hair, dark brown eyes, petite
- ✅ Background: Tokyo native, traditional family, pressure to marry
- ✅ Personality: Creative, determined, culturally aware, brave

### **Supporting Characters**
- ✅ Hiroshi: Driver, polite, helpful
- ✅ Mr. Sato: Aiko's father, traditional, stern but caring
- ✅ Mrs. Sato: Aiko's mother, traditional, concerned
- ✅ Professor Nakamura: David's mentor (deceased)
