# Plot Outline - Lumina-Umbra

## Structure: 18 Chapters

## Act I - The Divided City (Ch. 1-6)

### Chapter 1: The Divide of Light
- Liora discovers a discrepancy in a civic memory ledger.
- Cael steals a damaged memory plate tied to the same record.
- Their collision at the border checkpoint ends in forced cooperation.

### Chapter 2: The Smuggler's Concord
- Cael proves the plate contains overwritten testimony.
- Liora realizes her mother's legal archive trail was altered.
- They form a temporary pact: decode one full chain before dawn cycle reset.

### Chapter 3: Clarity Ceremony
- Public ritual updates official memory indexes.
- Liora watches a witness account be "harmonized" in real time.
- Cael identifies Curator Serev's signature encryption.

### Chapter 4: Below the Umbra
- Liora enters Umbra for the first time.
- Nima Ro introduces shadow theater records as uneditable memory.
- First emotional rupture: Liora sees children reciting erased family names.

### Chapter 5: Border Fracture
- Military patrol seizes a courier cell.
- Cael's old mentor is captured and disappears into Lumen.
- Liora and Cael argue over strategy: leak now or collect proof.

### Chapter 6: The Third Archive
- Hidden vault under the border contains pre-division city maps.
- Reveal: Vesperline was one district before the Engine partition.
- End beat: both are marked as coherence threats.

## Act II - Memory War (Ch. 7-13)

### Chapter 7: Directive of Silence
- Curator Serev launches a disinformation sweep.
- False confession of Cael airs in Lumen channels.
- Liora burns her public credentials to stay off-grid.

### Chapter 8: The Glass Choir
- The duo infiltrates a memory lab where child recollections are filtered.
- Cael finds evidence he once ran those filters.
- Liora chooses not to abandon him after the confession.

### Chapter 9: Testimony Market
- Umbra factions demand payment for hidden witness records.
- Nima brokers a difficult alliance.
- Thread planted: a corrupted plate references "Helio Key Prime."

### Chapter 10: The False Sun Protocol
- Serev accelerates daylight intensity to suppress border unrest.
- Heat surges cause casualties in low-shield zones.
- Liora shifts from evidence gathering to resistance logistics.

### Chapter 11: The Engine Vault
- Cael and Liora breach an engine maintenance chamber.
- They uncover a deliberate design feature: cyclical fear conditioning.
- Emotional midpoint: they choose each other over mission purity.

### Chapter 12: The Public Lie
- Council releases forged records painting Umbra as saboteurs.
- Nima stages a covert truth performance in daylight districts.
- Citizens begin to question archive authority.

### Chapter 13: Severance Night
- Serev orders a full border blackout.
- Mass panic across both districts.
- Liora is captured while transmitting raw witness files.

## Act III - The Shared Dawn (Ch. 14-18)

### Chapter 14: Interrogation of Memory
- Liora endures coherence interrogation and memory prompts.
- She notices the interrogators fear one missing file more than anything.
- Cael mounts a rescue through old utility tunnels.

### Chapter 15: Helio Key Prime
- The missing file reveals a failsafe to merge day/night systems.
- Cost: system merge permanently exposes all state edits.
- Stakes escalate from survival to societal collapse risk.

### Chapter 16: The Theater of Names
- Nima leads a citywide spoken-name action.
- Thousands publicly recite erased lineages.
- Collective memory begins overpowering official narrative feed.

### Chapter 17: Collapse of the Archive
- Serev triggers emergency lockdown and tries to destroy primary ledgers.
- Liora broadcasts unedited records through the Engine relay.
- Cael prevents a catastrophic overload but is severely injured.

### Chapter 18: A Shared Dawn
- Engine merge restores a natural sky cycle over Vesperline.
- Truth is incomplete but irreversible.
- Liora and Cael commit to building a civic memory commons.

## Sequel Hooks (Optional)

1. Neighboring city-states request access to Vesperline records.
2. Surviving council loyalists build a covert revision network.
3. The Engine logs imply earlier civilizations ran the same experiment.
