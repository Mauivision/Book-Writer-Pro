# Character Profiles - Lumina-Umbra

## 1) Liora Kes (POV A)

- **Role:** Junior State Archivist, Lumen District
- **Age:** 27
- **Public Identity:** Idealist clerk rising through the Light Council system
- **Private Reality:** Daughter of a "reclassified dissident" erased from official lineage

### Core Traits

- Precise, disciplined, socially fluent
- Emotionally guarded, intellectually fearless
- Trained to believe records over memory

### Internal Conflict

Liora built her life on archival certainty; discovering systemic edits means admitting she helped preserve lies.

### External Stakes

If she exposes forged ledgers, she loses status, safety, and legal identity.

### Arc

From obedient custodian of state truth to active architect of plural truth.

---

## 2) Cael Vey (POV B)

- **Role:** Memory-forger and courier, Umbra Undercity
- **Age:** 29
- **Public Identity:** Black-market technician, suspected border smuggler
- **Private Reality:** Former apprentice in a sanctioned memory lab

### Core Traits

- Improvisational, dry-humored, deeply observant
- Habitually distrustful of institutions
- Protective toward vulnerable communities in Umbra

### Internal Conflict

Cael profits from altered memory systems while despising what they do to people.

### External Stakes

If he is captured with original memory plates, execution is legal under "coherence law."

### Arc

From survival-first operator to principled witness willing to be seen.

---

## 3) High Curator Serev

- **Role:** Public face of the Light Council archive authority
- **Function in Story:** Antagonist with ideological conviction

### Profile

Serev genuinely believes social peace requires curated history. He is not cruel for spectacle; he is dangerous because he thinks erasure is mercy.

---

## 4) Nima Ro

- **Role:** Shadow-theater director in Umbra
- **Function in Story:** Mentor bridge between personal testimony and collective action

### Profile

Nima collects spoken memories in performance form so they cannot be cleanly edited by state systems. She pushes both leads toward public truth, not private revenge.

---

## 5) The Helio Engine (System Character)

- **Role:** City-scale day/night control lattice
- **Function in Story:** Environmental pressure, mystery source, eventual reveal

### Narrative Use

Though not sentient in a conventional sense, the engine behaves like a character: it responds to policy shifts, holds historical imprints, and can be weaponized.
