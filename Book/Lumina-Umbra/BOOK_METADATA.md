# Book Metadata - Lumina-Umbra

## Publishing Profile

- **Title:** Lumina-Umbra  
- **Series:** Standalone (expandable universe)
- **Genre:** Science-fantasy, political thriller, mystery
- **Target Audience:** Adult crossover (16+)
- **Estimated Length:** 95,000-110,000 words
- **POV Style:** Dual first-person, alternating chapters
- **Primary Setting:** The city-state of Vesperline

## Core Promise

Two people raised to distrust each other are forced to decode a hidden history before the machinery that controls day and night consumes their city.

## One-Line Pitch

When an archivist from eternal daylight and a forger from engineered darkness discover their memories are being edited by the same regime, they must cross a forbidden border and decide whether truth is worth civil collapse.

## Reader Experience Goals

1. Tightly plotted chapter endings that create immediate forward pull
2. Distinct voice contrast between both POV leads
3. A growing emotional bond embedded inside political stakes
4. Clear, visual world rules with escalating moral complexity

## Thematic Stack

- Manufactured truth vs lived memory
- Order vs autonomy
- Inherited fear and social engineering
- The cost of illumination
- What love can survive when history is falsified

## Act Architecture

- **Act I:** Discovery and coercive alliance
- **Act II:** Infiltration, betrayal, memory collapse
- **Act III:** Public truth event and systemic rupture

## Signature Story Devices

- Memory ledgers that can be altered at high social cost
- A literal line dividing light and dark sectors
- Public "clarity ceremonies" where official history is rewritten
- Illegal shadow theaters that preserve unedited testimonies
