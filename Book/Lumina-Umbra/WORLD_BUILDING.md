# World Building - Lumina-Umbra

## Vesperline

A city-state split by the Divide, a charged rail and sensor spine that runs Meridian Span. Official doctrine says the split is natural: light-born people thrive under engineered sun; night-born people require managed dark. In practice the split is policy, enforced by the Helio Engine.

## The Helio Engine

City-scale lattice that locks Lumen in perpetual day and Umbra in engineered night. It also hosts the civic memory feed. Policy shifts change light intensity, border permeability, and which records are "harmonized" into public indexes.

Known public claim: the Engine prevents riot-era chaos from returning.

Hidden function (do not name in-text before Chapter 9): cyclical fear conditioning. A later failsafe, Helio Key Prime, can merge day/night systems and permanently expose state edits.

## Light Council Doctrine

Core slogans:

- Precision protects peace.
- Memory without harmony is violence.
- The archive is the city.

Governing body: the Light Council. Archive authority sits with the Curatorial Tier, currently headed by High Curator Serev.

**Coherence Law:** possessing unharmonized original plates is a capital offense. The legal theory is that raw memory can re-ignite civil fracture.

**Clarity Ceremonies:** public rituals at first bell. Selected excerpts are broadcast citywide and written into official indexes. Once a ceremony locks a version, later contradiction is treated as sabotage.

**Harmonization:** the technical name for rewriting testimony until it matches Council narrative. Serev's office signs high-tier edits as `SEREV.COHERENCE.14`.

## Lumen District

Vertical glass, mirrored facades, vault lamps that never dim. Housing, work, and legal identity require a clean archive score. People here are trained from age nine to trust records over recollection.

Key sites:

- Archive Hall Three
- Clarity Plaza
- Checkpoint Six on Meridian Span
- Civil Mediation Node 4 (historical capture origin for the Vael riot plate)

## Umbra Undercity

Layered terraces under the span: filament lanterns, phosphor murals, suspended markets, utility tunnels. Order is improvised and relational, not doctrinal.

Key sites:

- Concord House - decoding room, three keys, six rumors
- Night market at Meridian Span
- Shadow Theater (Nima Ro) - spoken testimony stored as performance, hard to edit
- West tiers - first patrol sweep zone after checkpoint incidents
- Lower arteries - print shops, signal laundries, repair stalls

## Factions (Act I)

- **Curatorial Tier / Patrol:** official memory and border control
- **Courier cells:** move plates, people, and unedited captures
- **Shadow theater circle:** Nima's witnesses; public truth without files
- **Quiet leakers inside the vaults:** unnamed; one of them got the Vael original to Cael
