# Ascent of the Eternal Spark

## Overview
A literary, cosmic-spiritual novel following Elias, a 38-year-old architect, on a soul’s ascent from material entanglement to transcendence. The narrative blends intimate, third-person-limited scenes with omniscient cosmic passages, using motifs of stars (origin), wind (guidance), light (essence), water (renewal), and shadows (unintegrated regret).

Target length: ~80,000–100,000 words
Structure: 5 phases, 25 chapters + Epilogue

## Phases
1. The Material Anchor (Ch. 1–5)
2. The Awakening of Regrets (Ch. 6–10)
3. The Pursuit of Knowledge (Ch. 11–15)
4. The Balance and Surrender (Ch. 16–20)
5. The Cosmic Transcendence (Ch. 21–25) + Epilogue

## Supporting Cast
- Mira: former lover; grace and grounded love
- Dr. Lena Voss: elder astronomer; wonder and wisdom
- Street Artist: intuitive nebula murals; symbol of heart-knowledge
- Young Activist: eco-conscience; planetary bond
- Father (estranged, deceased): severity of nature; survival and wound

## Files
- OUTLINES/master-outline.md — detailed phase and chapter breakdown
- CHAPTERS/ — chapter manuscripts
- PUBLICATION/ — exports (PDF/EPUB), front matter, and notes
