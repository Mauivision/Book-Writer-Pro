# Chapter 22 — Comet Tails

The regrets arrive as comet tails—long streams of light that trail behind him as he moves through the cosmic space of expanded consciousness. They're not heavy anymore. They're not painful anymore. They're just light. Just memory. Just lessons learned, wounds healed, transformations complete.

He watches them trail behind him, each one a story, each one a moment, each one a choice that led him here. The shame of cheating on the test becomes a tail of light that teaches about integrity. The harsh words to his father become a tail of light that teaches about communication. The way he treated Mira becomes a tail of light that teaches about love. The buildings that scarred the land become a tail of light that teaches about responsibility.

They're not erased. They're not forgotten. They're transformed. They're alchemized. They're turned from lead into light, from weight into wisdom, from regret into teaching.

The purification stages continue. Each regret is examined, understood, integrated, released. The process is not violent. It's gentle. It's not forceful. It's natural. It's not ending. It's becoming.

He watches the comet tails burn away—not with destruction, but with transformation. They become light. They become energy. They become part of the cosmic background, part of the universal hum, part of the everything that is.

The purification is complete. The regrets are released. The weight is lifted. The light is pure. The consciousness is clear. The transcendence deepens.

He moves through the cosmic space, trailing light, becoming light, being light. The comet tails fade. The regrets dissolve. The lessons remain. The wisdom stays. The light expands.

The purification is not an ending. It's a beginning. It's not a loss. It's a gain. It's not a death. It's a rebirth.

He's ready. The purification is complete. The transcendence deepens. The cosmic unity approaches.
