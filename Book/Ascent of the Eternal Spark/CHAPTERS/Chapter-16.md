# Chapter 16 — Color Ledger

He stands at the bridge with his eyes closed and recounts the hues he has carried. Rage was red with a noise like a bent fork against porcelain. He holds it in his hands now and it is lighter than he feared; it has edges but no teeth. He thanks it and sets it down. Gold enters his chest as the warm afterimage of Mira’s kindness and he releases it not because it burns, but because it belongs on everything.

Blue—curiosity—arrives on the wind like an old friend finally forgiven for leaving him alone when he needed answers. “You were only asking me to look up,” he tells it, and the sky listens. The city behind him breathes. Below, the river tenses and relaxes and the sound is close to prayer.

Surrender is not a word he makes himself say. It is the way his fingers release the rail without instruction, the way his breath falls into a rhythm he does not conduct, the way he understands that balance is not a scale but a bow over a string drawing a single note long enough for love to find it.
