# Editorial Guidelines — Shadow Realms Reborn

## Voice & Tone
- Character-driven, immersive, cinematic
- Balance wonder and menace; avoid grimdark nihilism
- Keep POV tight to Elara (limited third) unless scene demands otherwise

## World & Magic Consistency
- Shadow magic is creative, fluid, temperature-lowering; light magic is restorative, structured, warming
- Balanced magic appears iridescent; requires emotional equilibrium
- Day/Night cycles actively affect environment and magic potency

## Style
- Show first, explain later; embed exposition in conflict or action
- Vary atmospheric descriptors; avoid repeating "metallic tang"/"humming magic" too frequently
- Keep chapters 2.5–4k words in final draft; end on forward motion

## Character Arcs
- Elara: fear → curiosity → mastery (chooses balance over power)
- Thorne: penance → protection → partnership (curse → choice)
- Lira: pragmatism → mentorship → statesmanship

## Scene Checklist
- Clear goal, obstacle, stakes
- Sensory grounding in the dual-realm world
- Character beat or relationship movement
- Micro-turn (reversal, reveal, decision)
- Exit with hook or unresolved tension

## Continuity Rules
- The Veil weakens during transitions; false dawns occur in Shadow Realms
- Constellation appears only for true weavers; avoid overuse
- Corruption tempts via shortcuts; refusal strengthens balanced magic access

## Prohibited
- Deus ex machina resolutions
- Info-dumps > 250 words without conflict
- Breaking magical rules for convenience

## Revision Pass Order
1) Structure and arcs
2) Scene goals/stakes
3) Prose and imagery
4) Continuity and rule-check
5) Typos/formatting
