# Magic and World — Shadow Realms Reborn

## The Dual Realms
- Light Realms: ordered, sunstone cities, restorative magic, Council governance
- Shadow Realms: shifting geographies, living architecture, shadow weaving, clan/rebel networks
- The Veil: semi-permeable barrier; weakest at transitions; now a potential bridge

## Cycles and Transitions
- Light cycle (LR): 16h day / 8h twilight; magic strongest under sun
- Shadow cycle (SR): 8h dim / 16h deep night; magic strongest under deep night
- False dawns in SR during transition; highly unstable terrain and magic

## Magic Systems
### Light Magic (Binding)
- Source: solar/life energies
- Uses: healing, protection, growth
- Costs: exhaustion; reduced potency in deep night; can scorch if overchanneled

### Shadow Magic (Weaving)
- Source: void/lunar/affective energies
- Uses: constructs, stealth, scrying, living architecture
- Costs: cold drain; corruption risk if used for domination/shortcuts

### Balanced Magic (Rare)
- Source: harmonized light+shadow
- Uses: Veil modulation, high-level integration, stabilization
- Costs: requires emotional equilibrium; extreme fatigue

### Cursed Magic (Backlash)
- Manifestation of imbalance; grants power with pain/limitations
- Example: Thorne’s curse; heightened senses/strength with persistent cost

## Artifacts & Lore
- Veil Keys (3): stabilize/destabilize the Veil; distributed across factions
- Shadow Grimoire: pre-Veil compendium; enables balanced techniques
- Voss Amulet: bloodline amplifier; corruption guardrail

## Societies & Factions
- Mage Council (LR): preservationist, control-focused, splintering
- Shadow Weavers (SR): preservation of old knowledge; rebels and clans
- The Shadow Lord (Antagonist): corrupted guardian seeking unilateral shadow order

## Continuity Anchors
- Constellation appears to true weavers; plot signal, not a gimmick
- Transitions drive geography and danger; use for set-pieces
- Corruption tempts through shortcuts; refusal unlocks balance access
