## Chapter 06 — The Veil Key Problem

The Radiant Seal Gate glowed like a smug judge.

Haruto stood in front of it with his hands on his hips, trying very hard to look like someone who hadn’t been isekai’d forty minutes ago.

The seal pulsed once, as if impatient.

Sunny—the tutorial sprite—hovered at eye level and bobbed in place like a balloon tied to the concept of suffering.

“To proceed,” Sunny said brightly, “you need the matching Veil curse-key!”

Seraphina’s jaw tightened. “The Radiant Dominion does not ‘match’ with the Veil.”

Sunny rotated toward her. “Incorrect!”

Seraphina’s eye twitched.

Luminara leaned closer to Haruto, voice soft with amusement. “Heaven help you if you start arguing with a tutorial.”

Haruto whispered back, “It started it.”

Velvet’s gaze stayed on the gate with the calm focus of a surgeon. “Where is the key kept?” she asked.

Sunny chimed. “Great question! The key is located in a nearby room guarded by enemies and a puzzle designed to promote cooperation!”

Mira clapped like this was a tour. “Fun!”

Haruto stared down the corridor Sunny indicated.

“We don’t have a Veil teammate,” Haruto pointed out. “How do we solve a cooperation puzzle that requires Shadow?”

Sunny bobbed. “Another great question!”

Haruto waited.

Sunny’s face stayed cheerful.

Haruto’s patience meter—an invisible stat he was certain existed—dropped.

“Answer?” he prompted.

Sunny chirped, “You figure it out!”

Haruto’s UI chimed immediately.

> **SIDE QUEST ACCEPTED:** Emotional endurance  
> **Reward:** none  
> **Failure condition:** tears (optional)

Haruto pinched the bridge of his nose. “Okay. Fine. We go get the key.”

Seraphina stepped forward, already in motion. “Stay behind me.”

Mira grabbed Haruto’s sleeve.

Haruto froze.

Mira blinked, innocent. “Life-or-death emergency?”

Haruto hesitated.

The corridor ahead felt… hungry.

Haruto sighed. “Fine. Life-or-death adjacency is permitted.”

Mira smiled like she’d won a trial.

Seraphina made a strangled sound of restrained outrage.

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (minor)  
> **NOTE:** You just wrote your own loophole.

Haruto glared at the air. “Stop narrating my mistakes!”

> **TIP:** No.

They followed Sunny down the corridor, away from the gate.

The path dipped slightly, the floor tiles changing from pale gold inlay to something older—stone etched with patterns that didn’t feel Radiant.

The light dimmed.

The air chilled.

Haruto’s cuff warmed against his wrist as if reacting to the shift.

Velvet noticed instantly. “It’s responding,” she murmured.

“To what?” Haruto whispered.

Luminara’s flame-hair flickered lower. “To balance,” she said. “The dungeon is sliding toward Shadow.”

Seraphina scowled. “Then we cleanse it.”

Sunny drifted ahead, cheerful as ever. “Great attitude! You will fail!”

Haruto hissed. “Why do you exist?”

Sunny replied brightly, “To help!”

They reached a door—smaller than the gate, but still carved with Radiant symbols.

This one wasn’t sealed by a glowing circle.

It was sealed by a *lock*.

A lock shaped like a sun.

Sunny bobbed. “Welcome to **Puzzle Room #2: Cooperation!**”

The door clicked open on its own, like it wanted to be caught.

Inside, the room was split cleanly down the middle.

Left side: bright marble, warm light, Radiant runes.

Right side: darker stone, cool mist, patterns that looked like they’d been etched by someone who enjoyed secrets.

Between the halves ran a thin black line in the floor, perfectly straight, like a boundary.

Haruto stepped inside and felt the pressure change.

Not physically.

Socially.

Like the room had opinions.

The System chimed.

> **PUZZLE ROOM #2:** Cooperative Lock  
> **Requirement:** Light + Shadow activation within 10 seconds  
> **Failure:** hostile spawn + embarrassment

Haruto’s throat went dry. “We can do Light. But Shadow—”

A pedestal stood on the Radiant half, topped with a sun-shaped plate.

On the Veil half, a matching pedestal waited—its plate shaped like a crescent, dark and glossy like obsidian.

Above both pedestals floated a single instruction window.

> **STEP 1:** Place a Radiant hand on the Sun Plate.  
> **STEP 2:** Place a Veil hand on the Moon Plate.  
> **STEP 3:** Try not to argue.

Seraphina strode to the Sun Plate immediately and slammed her palm on it like she was executing a criminal.

The plate lit up, runes flaring.

“Done,” she said. “Now where is the Veil hand?”

Everyone’s gaze drifted to the Moon Plate.

It sat there patiently.

Waiting.

Luminara’s smile turned thoughtful. “You have Party Leader Authority,” she said, eyes on Haruto. “The System acknowledged it.”

Haruto swallowed. “That doesn’t mean I can—”

Sunny chimed. “Actually, it might!”

Haruto glared at Sunny. “Oh now you’re helpful.”

Sunny replied, “I help when it’s funniest.”

Velvet’s eyes narrowed. “Haruto,” she said gently, “the dungeon is built on roles. If you are the Player, you may have permission to imitate missing roles.”

Haruto stared at the Moon Plate.

It looked harmless.

It also looked like stepping into the Veil half would cause a dramatic music cue.

Mira whispered, “If you go over there, do you become edgy?”

Haruto whispered back, “I’m already suffering. That’s edgy enough.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Edginess increases critical hit rate.  
> **TIP:** Also increases emotional damage.

Haruto took a breath.

He stepped over the black line.

The moment his foot crossed, a chill ran up his leg like the room had licked him.

He resisted the urge to yelp.

His UI updated.

> **ZONE EFFECT:** Veil Influence (minor)  
> **Status:** “Stylish gloom” applied  
> **Note:** Your posture is now 12% more tragic.

Haruto’s shoulders slumped automatically.

“No,” Haruto whispered. “No. Don’t you do this to me.”

Luminara’s lips twitched. “It’s doing it.”

Seraphina snapped, “Stand properly!”

Haruto straightened out of spite.

His cuff warmed again, as if approving his rebellion.

Haruto approached the Moon Plate.

The closer he got, the more the Veil half of the room seemed to breathe—mist curling along the floor, runes faintly shifting like they were alive.

He raised his hand.

Velvet’s voice softened. “If it hurts, move away.”

Mira whispered, “If you die, I’ll drag you back and scold you.”

Haruto swallowed. “Comforting.”

He placed his palm on the Moon Plate.

For one heartbeat, nothing happened.

Then the Moon Plate flared with dark light—not evil, not violent, just… cold.

A second line of text appeared above Haruto’s HUD.

> **SKILL TRIGGER:** Party Leader Authority — *Co-op Override*  
> **Effect:** Temporary “Veil Hand” permission granted

Haruto’s eyes widened. “It worked.”

Sunny chimed so loud it felt personal.

“CONGRATULATIONS!” it sang. “You have learned cooperation! With yourself!”

Haruto hissed, “That’s not cooperation. That’s… loneliness.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Loneliness is also an emotion.

The plates pulsed in sync.

The room trembled.

And the far wall slid open, revealing a small recess where something rested on velvet-black cloth.

A key.

It was long and thin, carved like a thorn. Its metal looked like blackened silver with faint neon lines running through it.

It was beautiful in the way a dangerous thing was beautiful.

Sunny bobbed proudly. “Behold! The Veil curse-key!”

Haruto lifted his hand from the Moon Plate and stepped back over the black line like he was escaping a bad haircut.

Instantly, the chill vanished.

His posture returned to normal.

His UI updated:

> **ZONE EFFECT ENDED:** Stylish gloom removed  
> **Note:** Tragic posture reduction applied

Haruto exhaled in relief.

Seraphina still had her palm on the Sun Plate, unwavering.

She looked at Haruto like he’d done something both impressive and suspicious.

“How,” she demanded.

Haruto shrugged, trying to look casual while his heart hammered. “I’m built different.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** He is not.

Velvet’s mouth curved. “Well done,” she said quietly.

Haruto felt warmth crawl up his neck.

He forced himself to breathe.

Calm.

Calm.

No emotion spikes.

Mira leaned close, eyes shining. “That was so cool. You looked a little… dark and handsome for a second.”

Haruto’s calm shattered.

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (minor)  
> **NOTE:** Nice try.

Seraphina’s face colored instantly. “Lady Mirabelle—”

Velvet stepped smoothly between them like a healer intercepting damage. “Mira,” she said, sweet as poison, “please refrain from complimenting him in trap rooms.”

Mira pouted. “We’re not in a trap room.”

Sunny chimed from above. “Everything is a trap room!”

Haruto glared at Sunny. “Stop that.”

Sunny replied, “No!”

Haruto walked to the recess and stared at the key.

He didn’t pick it up immediately.

He didn’t trust anything that sat on velvet cloth in a dungeon.

He waited for the System to say “Binds on pickup” again.

It didn’t.

Instead, a new window appeared.

> **ITEM:** Veil Curse-Key  
> **Function:** Opens Radiant Seal Gates when paired with Light activation  
> **Warning:** Holding this key may attract Veil attention

Haruto swallowed. “Attract Veil attention.”

Luminara’s smile turned sharp. “We already promised them attention.”

Seraphina’s voice was tight. “We promised nothing.”

Velvet’s gaze stayed calm, but her tone carried weight. “We promised a commander.”

Mira clasped her hands. “And I promised him a lap pillow if he survives.”

Haruto stared at her. “That was not—”

The System chimed.

> **CONTRACT UPDATE:** “Lap pillow” clause pending verification

Haruto’s face went red.

Seraphina made a strangled noise.

Luminara laughed softly.

Velvet’s smile stayed serene, which meant she was filing this away as ammunition.

Haruto grabbed the key before the dungeon could add more clauses.

The moment his fingers closed around it, the key pulsed—once, like a heartbeat.

His UI flashed.

> **ITEM ACQUIRED:** Veil Curse-Key  
> **NOTE:** Your inventory now contains “Shadow.”

Haruto stared at the word Shadow in his HUD like it was a living animal.

Sunny bobbed. “Great! Now return to the Radiant gate! Try not to die on the way back!”

Haruto’s stomach sank. “On the way back?”

As if responding to his dread, the room’s lights dimmed.

The pedestals faded into the floor.

The door they’d entered through—back toward the Radiant Seal Gate—slid shut with a soft *thunk.*

Seraphina’s head snapped around. “It closed.”

Sunny chimed brightly. “Yes! You have unlocked **Lesson 3: Detours!**”

Haruto’s eye twitched. “There’s a detour?”

The far wall—the one behind the recess—opened with a slow grind, revealing a darker corridor that breathed out cold mist.

Velvet’s expression didn’t change, but her hand lifted slightly, ready. “Of course there is.”

Mira whispered, thrilled, “Secret route.”

Luminara’s flames flickered lower. “Shadow route.”

Seraphina lifted her sword. “Stay close. No hero falls behind.”

Haruto clenched the key in his hand and felt it hum faintly, like it was pleased.

Then a final window appeared.

Not from the System.

Not from Sunny.

This one was darker—text etched in sharp lines, letters like bone.

> **MESSAGE RECEIVED (VEIL):**  
> *Good. Keep it safe.*  
> *You owe me, Player.*  
> — **Bone Legion Commander**

Haruto’s throat went dry.

Seraphina leaned in, eyes narrowing as she read it. “He is watching you.”

Luminara smiled like she’d just been served dessert. “Told you.”

Velvet’s voice was quiet. “And now the dungeon will test whether you keep your word.”

Mira squeezed Haruto’s arm—briefly, just enough to be grounding. “We’ve got you,” she whispered.

Haruto took a breath.

The corridor ahead exhaled cold mist like laughter.

He tightened his grip on the Veil key and stepped forward.

“Okay,” Haruto said, more to himself than anyone. “Detour it is.”
