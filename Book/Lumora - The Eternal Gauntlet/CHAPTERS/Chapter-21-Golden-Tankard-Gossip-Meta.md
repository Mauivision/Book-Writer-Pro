## Chapter 21 — Golden Tankard Gossip Meta

Rumors in Lumora moved faster than mobs.

That was Haruto’s first thought when he woke up and found his own face staring at him from the Golden Tankard’s front chalkboard.

It wasn’t a drawing.

Not a caricature.

Not the kind of cute little doodle taverns put up to advertise “Hero’s Special: half price if you’re bleeding.”

It was a *diagram*.

Mira had drawn it in white chalk with the precision of a woman who had learned how to weaponize a smile.

**HARUTO (THE PLAYER)** sat in the center like a boss node.

From it, lines branched outward to names and symbols:

- **Seraphina** — Radiant Paladin — (HEART?)  
- **Luminara** — Solar Sorceress — (HEART??)  
- **Velvet** — High Priestess — (HEART???)  
- **Mira** — Tavern Mistress — (???)  
- **Bone Legion Commander** — Veil — (SKULL)  
- **Chiller Executioner** — Veil — (AXE)  
- **Ecto** — (HUGS)
- **Elara** — (LEAF???) — (???)

Haruto stared at it, half asleep and fully offended.

Then he saw the second chalkboard.

The second chalkboard wasn’t cute.

It listed *versions* of him, each with a different title, each with a different arrow pointing to “likely faction outcome.”

**HARUTO: RADIANT SAINT (LIKELY)**  
**HARUTO: VEIL TRAITOR (LIKELY)**  
**HARUTO: HAREM WARLORD (UNFORTUNATELY LIKELY)**  
**HARUTO: ANOMALY (CONFIRMED)**

Haruto blinked hard.

The System chimed softly, like it was trying to sound helpful in a room full of panic.

> **NOTICE:** Reputation systems active.  
> **Tip:** Rumors modify outcomes.  
> **Tip:** Outcomes modify rumors.

Haruto rubbed his face.

“So it’s a feedback loop,” he muttered.

> **TIP:** Yes.

“Great. Love that.”

Mira popped up from behind the bar like she had been waiting specifically for him to notice.

“Morning, hero!” she chirped. “Try not to look directly at your own legend, it’s bad for the spine.”

Haruto pointed at the chalkboard. “Why is there a flowchart of my love life?”

Mira’s smile was dazzling. “It’s not your love life.”

She leaned in conspiratorially.

“It’s your *survival plan.*”

Haruto’s stomach tightened.

Behind him, Seraphina entered the main room like she was ready to fight sunlight itself.

She saw the chalkboard.

She froze.

Her cheeks turned red in a way Haruto had begun to associate with “the world is about to spawn something.”

Luminara drifted in right after, hair glowing faintly like a sunrise that had learned sarcasm.

She read the board.

Her smile widened.

Velvet followed more quietly, already in healer mode, gaze scanning for damage that couldn’t be bandaged.

Bone Legion Commander arrived with the patience of a man who had already predicted this disaster.

Chiller Executioner arrived, yawned, and said, “Is that me?”

Ecto arrived and clapped.

Elara arrived last.

She didn’t react outwardly.

But her eyes flicked over the leaf symbol and the question marks beside her name, and Haruto saw something like irritated acceptance settle in her gaze.

Mira turned and beamed at the room.

“Okay!” she announced brightly. “We have a situation.”

Seraphina marched up to the board like it had personally insulted Radiant doctrine.

“Remove it,” she ordered.

Mira didn’t even blink. “No.”

Seraphina’s eyes narrowed. “Mira.”

Mira met her glare with a bartender’s calm.

“You think I put this up because I’m bored?” Mira asked sweetly.

Seraphina looked like she was about to say yes.

Mira continued before she could.

“The city already has this board,” Mira said, tapping the chalk with her nail. “Not on chalk. In mouths. In eyes. In ‘oh my god did you hear’ whispers.”

Haruto’s HUD chimed, as if eager to join.

> **NEW STATUS:** “Talk of the Town”  
> **Effect:** +Attention  
> **Side effect:** +Micro-incident frequency

Haruto stared at the words until his soul tried to uninstall.

“Why is attention a stat,” he whispered.

Velvet answered without looking away from Haruto. “Because it always was.”

Seraphina’s jaw tightened. “This is slander.”

Luminara laughed softly. “It’s marketing.”

Bone Legion Commander clicked his jaw. “It’s targeting.”

Elara’s voice was quiet. “It’s pressure.”

Chiller Executioner scratched his head. “Is there breakfast?”

Ecto bounced. “Group breakfast!”

Mira slapped two plates down on the counter. “Yes. Eat. Stress makes rumors stick.”

Haruto blinked. “That’s not real.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Stress makes rumors stick.

Haruto closed his eyes for one full second.

When he opened them, the room hadn’t changed, which was disappointing.

Seraphina stood rigidly, staring at the board like if she glared hard enough she could smite chalk.

Velvet placed a gentle hand on Seraphina’s arm.

Seraphina flinched.

Not away.

Just… like she didn’t want the touch to be seen.

Velvet’s voice was calm. “Seraphina. It’s not you they’re hurting.”

Seraphina’s breath hitched. “It is.”

Haruto turned toward her.

He saw it then.

Not jealousy.

Not rivalry.

Shame.

Because the rumors weren’t just about Haruto.

They were about what Seraphina “was” in the System’s story.

A route.

A flag.

A prize in a competition she never agreed to join.

Haruto’s chest tightened with something angry and protective and too human to be a mechanic.

His HUD chimed.

> **WARNING:** Bond event conditions detected.

Haruto stared at the warning and felt his face go hot with rage.

“No,” he said out loud. “Not everything is a bond event.”

The System chimed again, offended.

> **NOTICE:** Everything is a bond event.

“Shut up,” Haruto snapped at the air.

The room went quiet.

Even Chiller paused mid-bite.

Mira’s eyes glittered, not amused—impressed.

Because Haruto had just done something dangerous.

He had argued with the System in public.

Haruto swallowed.

He forced his voice to stay steady.

“We’re not doing this,” he said, looking at Seraphina. “You’re not a route. You’re not a prize. You’re a person.”

Seraphina’s throat bobbed. Her eyes went wide, then narrowed like she was trying not to let the words in.

Velvet’s hand tightened on her arm, grounding.

Luminara’s smile softened by a fraction.

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked once, quieter than usual.

Elara watched Haruto with something like cautious respect.

Mira, sensing the moment, deliberately looked away—like she was giving it privacy by refusing to witness it as entertainment.

The System chimed anyway.

> **BOND EVENT DETECTED:** “Public Validation”  
> **Effect:** Unity +1  
> **Side effect:** Rumor volatility increased

Haruto’s stomach dropped.

“Rumor volatility,” he repeated flatly.

Mira turned back with a grim smile. “Yeah. That one’s new.”

As if summoned by the word, the tavern door swung open.

A trio of patrons stumbled in laughing, cheeks flushed, already halfway through a story they couldn’t wait to finish.

“Did you hear?” one of them said, loud. “The Player refused BOTH factions!”

“No,” another cackled, “he refused the factions because he has *seven girlfriends* and they’d fight the council!”

The third one slapped the table nearest them. “I heard he’s a saint. Radiant blessed. Chose purity.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned slowly.

Seraphina’s cheeks went red again.

Luminara’s eyes glittered.

Velvet’s calm turned colder.

Ecto waved at the patrons enthusiastically. “Hi!”

The patrons froze when they saw the mixed table.

Their laughter died in their throats like they’d swallowed a debuff.

One of them whispered, “That’s… the skeleton.”

Bone Legion Commander nodded once, polite as a guillotine. “Correct.”

The patrons backed up.

Mira stepped in smoothly, smile bright.

“Welcome!” she sang. “Come in, come in! Try the recovery drinks! They’ll help you survive telling that story without dying of embarrassment.”

The patrons hesitated.

Mira’s smile didn’t falter.

She was controlling the story.

Haruto watched her work and realized, with a sick twist in his stomach, that this was combat.

No swords.

No spells.

Just perception.

Mira leaned in to the patrons and murmured something too quiet for Haruto to hear.

The patrons’ expressions shifted.

One of them laughed nervously.

Another nodded quickly.

Then all three turned, loudly, and announced to the room:

“NO, NO, it’s not like that. The Player’s party is… uh… diplomatically diverse!”

Someone at the bar snorted.

Mira beamed. “Yes! Exactly! Diverse!”

The System chimed.

> **MICRO-INCIDENT AVOIDED:** “Public Panic”  
> **Cause:** proactive narrative management  
> **Reward:** +Rumor control (Mira)

Haruto stared.

“Rumor control,” he whispered.

Velvet exhaled slowly. “It’s becoming a skill.”

Mira winked. “It’s always been a skill.”

Seraphina looked sick.

Haruto turned toward her.

“Seraphina,” he said quietly.

Seraphina’s jaw clenched. “I don’t like being laughed at,” she admitted, voice tight. “I don’t like being… reduced.”

Haruto’s chest tightened.

He wanted to say a thousand things.

He chose the smallest honest one.

“Me neither,” he said.

Seraphina looked at him like she didn’t know what to do with honesty that wasn’t a vow.

Luminara leaned back in her chair, flames dim.

“You can’t stop them from talking,” she said softly. “But you can decide what you give them.”

Elara’s gaze slid to the window, to the street outside.

“And you can decide where you stand when the talk becomes a weapon,” she added.

Bone Legion Commander clicked his jaw once. “Radiant will spin you as betrayal. Veil will spin you as opportunity.”

Mira smiled, sharp. “And I will spin them back.”

Haruto swallowed.

He looked at the chalkboard again.

At his face.

At the branching lines.

At the hearts.

At the skull.

At the leaf and question marks.

He hated it.

He also understood it now.

This wasn’t gossip.

This was the city running a simulation and waiting to see which version of him stabilized.

His HUD chimed again—gentle, deadly.

> **NOTICE:** Public perception forming.  
> **Tip:** You cannot opt out.

Haruto exhaled slowly.

“Okay,” he said.

Mira perked up. “Okay!”

Velvet watched him carefully.

Seraphina stiffened.

Bone Legion Commander’s shadows tightened.

Elara’s eyes narrowed slightly.

Luminara smiled.

Chiller ate.

Ecto hugged a chair.

Haruto pointed at the board.

“If we can’t opt out,” Haruto said, “then we choose the version of the story that hurts us the least.”

Mira’s grin widened. “Now you’re speaking my language.”

Haruto looked at Seraphina.

“And we don’t let them turn you into a joke,” he added.

Seraphina’s throat bobbed.

She nodded once, very small.

The System chimed.

> **RUMOR VOLATILITY:** rising  
> **Tip:** This is your fault.

Haruto glared at the air.

“Yeah,” he muttered. “Put it on my tab.”

As if the System had been waiting for that exact phrase, Mira lifted a cloth and wiped the chalkboard clean with one dramatic sweep.

The tavern gasped.

Haruto blinked. “Mira—”

Mira didn’t look at him.

She wrote three new words in huge chalk letters:

**WE WORK TOGETHER.**

Then, underneath it, smaller:

**ASK US WHY.**

The room went quiet again—not because people were afraid, but because people didn’t know how to respond to a story that refused to be funny on command.

Haruto’s HUD chimed, almost reluctant.

> **NOTICE:** Narrative anchor placed.  
> **Effect:** reduces rumor drift (local)

Velvet exhaled softly. “Mira.”

Mira smiled like nothing in the world could hurt her.

“I told you,” she said. “We control the story.”

Then the door opened again.

Not patrons this time.

A man stepped in carrying a wooden plank.

A *board.*

He placed it near the bar, propped it up, and began hammering a fresh sheet of parchment to it.

Haruto watched, confused, until he saw the ink.

Names.

Numbers.

Marks that looked uncomfortably like odds.

At the top, in cheerful handwriting:

**BETTING BOARD — WHO CLAIMS THE PLAYER?**

Haruto’s stomach dropped.

His HUD chimed with the exhausted delight of a system that loved escalation.

> **NEW EVENT:** Wagering  
> **Tip:** Congratulations. You are now entertainment.

Haruto stared at the board.

Then he looked at his party.

Then he looked at Mira.

Mira’s eyes glittered.

“Okay,” she whispered, delighted in the way only dangerous people were delighted. “Now it’s a game.”
