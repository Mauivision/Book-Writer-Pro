## Chapter 25 — Bond Quest: Veil (Humanity)

The bond quest notification didn't pop up in gold.

It arrived in fog-gray, Veil-branded, tucked into Haruto's quest log like something that didn't want applause.

> **BOND QUEST (Veil):** Supply Run — Crypt District  
> **Objective:** escort medicine + food to fog-garden families  
> **Reward:** cross-faction trust modifier  
> **Warning:** Radiant optics may interpret as betrayal

Haruto read the warning twice.

Bone Legion Commander materialized beside him at the Veil cart staging point like he'd been waiting for the System to finally assign something sensible.

"You received the quest," the Commander said.

"I received the warning label," Haruto replied. "Same thing, different font."

Chiller Executioner was already loading crates onto a creaking cart, humming a tune that sounded like a dirge played on a broken arcade cabinet. Ecto flowed around the wheels, stabilizing axles with enthusiastic blob-hugs.

"Families!" Ecto chirped. "Little ghoul kids! They like hugs if you ask first!"

"Ask first," Bone Legion Commander repeated, flat. "Write that on the crate."

Haruto grabbed a harness strap. "Why me? You've got Veil personnel."

The Commander's skull turned toward him. "Because the families asked for the Player who escorted civilians without demanding a speech. Because Radiant hardliners will call it collusion if you go. Because Radiant hardliners will call it cowardice if you refuse." A pause. "We don't get saints. We get outcomes."

Haruto should have argued.

Instead he checked the route map—fog gardens, back alleys, a shrine checkpoint Gravetender Odo had marked as respawn-safe.

Velvet found him before they rolled out.

She didn't block the cart.

She adjusted Haruto's collar like that fixed anything.

"If Halvion's patrol sees you," Velvet said quietly, "do not perform guilt. Perform competence."

"That advice works for everything here."

"Yes." Velvet's gaze held his. "Also—come back. I have triage supplies if someone throws a sanctified rock."

Haruto nodded.

Seraphina watched from the tavern doorway, arms crossed, jaw tight—not stopping him, not blessing him. Choosing trust over theater. That hurt worse than a debuff.

The cart moved.

---

Fog gardens lived in the seam between Castle Lumora's polished surface and the Veil Crypts' neon underworld—green-black hedges, grave-soil planters, lanterns that burned cold. Families here weren't raid props. They were people who respawned too often and still made dinner.

A ghoul mother met the cart at the first gate, three small figures peeking from behind her legs.

"Player," she said, voice rough with relief. "You came."

Haruto handed down medicine crates. "Directions said you needed antitoxin."

"Directions said we needed someone who wouldn't sell our address to Radiant auditors." She took the crate, then softer: "Thank you."

Ecto distributed food packets with ceremony—bowing, asking permission, hugging only when invited. One small ghoul laughed. Ecto's blob-body rippled with joy so sincere it made the fog feel warmer.

Chiller stacked firewood with excessive force, then apologized to a tombstone he scratched.

Bone Legion Commander handled logistics—head counts, ration math, which shrine paths stayed clear of patrol overlap. No drama. No declarations. Just work.

Haruto moved crates until his shoulders burned and his reputation bar did things he didn't want to inspect.

> **REPUTATION:** Veil Syndicate +3  
> **REPUTATION:** Radiant Dominion -2 (pending confirmation)  
> **Tip:** optics are also a weapon.

At the third house, Haruto saw what the quest was really teaching.

Not that Veil citizens were secretly noble.

That they were *ordinary*—worried about rent-shaped equivalents, school schedules for kids who phased through walls, whether the next Patch Note would nerf fog-garden respawn rates. A grandmother ghoul offered him tea that tasted like mint and static. A teenage skeleton practiced axe forms in the alley, embarrassed when caught.

"You're staring," the teenager said.

"Sorry." Haruto set down a sack of grain. "You look like my friend back home. Different bones."

The teenager's eye sockets brightened. "Tell your friend skeletons can be cool."

"He'd believe you."

Bone Legion Commander watched Haruto interact and said, quietly, "This is why Unity works. Not because factions merge. Because people stop being categories when you carry their groceries."

Haruto exhaled. "That's almost sentimental."

"Report it and I deny it."

---

On the return route, Ecto froze mid-hug.

"Someone's watching," Ecto whispered.

Haruto followed the blob's gaze.

A Radiant observer in white cloak stood at the fog-garden edge—too far for intervention, close enough for testimony. Note tablet out. Expression professionally scandalized.

Haruto's HUD chimed:

> **WITNESS EVENT:** Player escort (Veil civilians)  
> **Likely spin:** betrayal / collusion / romance (pick two)

Chiller raised his axe.

"Don't," Haruto said.

" They're going to lie about us."

"Yes." Haruto kept walking, hands visible, crates empty, conscience inconveniently full. "Let them lie. We did the job."

The observer wrote anyway.

Bone Legion Commander's shadows curled tight—not threatening the observer, shielding the cart. "Outcome recorded. Optics will follow."

Haruto's bond quest window updated:

> **BOND QUEST COMPLETE:** Veil Humanity  
> **Reward:** cross-faction trust +2 (party-wide)  
> **Consequence:** rumor seed planted (Radiant hardliners)

As they rolled back toward the Golden Tankard, a new line appeared on the betting board Haruto couldn't see yet—already forming in chalk somewhere ahead:

*Haruto Takahashi seen escorting Veil civilians through restricted routes.*

Betrayal or breakfast.

In Lumora, the difference was mostly timing.

Haruto tightened the harness and kept walking.

He had chosen help over optics.

The System had counted it.

So would the hardliners.
