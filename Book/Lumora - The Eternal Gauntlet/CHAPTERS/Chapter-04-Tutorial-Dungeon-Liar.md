## Chapter 04 — Tutorial Dungeon (Liar)

The archway didn’t open like a door.

It opened like a **menu**.

Light folded inward, lines of glowing text scrolling along the marble frame as if the dungeon were booting up.

Haruto stepped forward and felt the air change.

Outside, Castle Lumora had been warm—sunlight and incense and the faint smell of virtue. Here, on the threshold of Sky Dungeon #7, the air was cooler. Thinner. It tasted like stone dust and ozone, the way a room smelled after you unplugged something expensive.

The world shimmered.

And then it clicked into place.

Haruto’s vision snapped sharp as if someone had adjusted resolution settings.

The floating island beyond the gate looked *too* perfect: pale stone paths, white ruin-walls draped in glittering vines, and columns that stood at heroic angles like they’d been carefully posed for promotional art.

Even the clouds below seemed arranged to flatter the scenery.

“It’s pretty,” Haruto said honestly.

“So is poison,” Velvet replied, serene as always.

Luminara drifted a half-step closer to the threshold, her hair-flame flickering in anticipation. “Sky Dungeon #7,” she murmured. “The dungeon that smiles before it bites.”

Seraphina lifted her greatsword and rested it against her shoulder with practiced ease. “Stay close. Keep formation.”

Mira bounced on the balls of her feet like she was about to enter a festival. “I love dungeons,” she announced. “They’re like taverns, but with more screaming.”

Haruto’s UI chimed—bright, eager, and entirely untrustworthy.

> **ENTERING: SKY DUNGEON #7**  
> **Difficulty:** Beginner (Friendly)  
> **Recommended Level:** 1  
> **Recommended Mood:** Calm  
> **Warning:** Moods may not remain calm.

Haruto stared at “Friendly.”

“It lied already,” he muttered.

> **TIP:** Dungeons cannot lie.  
> **TIP:** Dungeons can only be misunderstood.

Haruto pointed at the word “Friendly.” “This is a lie.”

The window blinked.

> **TIP:** Friendly means “you will learn a lot.”

Velvet’s eyes softened with pity. “You will,” she agreed.

The party crossed the threshold together.

For a heartbeat, the light bridge behind them vanished.

Haruto’s stomach dropped.

Then it reappeared with a quiet shimmer, as if the dungeon wanted him to know it could take the exit away whenever it felt like it.

Seraphina didn’t even flinch. “Forward,” she said.

Haruto followed.

The first corridor was wide and clean, lined with pale stone. Faint gold runes glowed along the floor, pulsing in slow, steady rhythms like a heartbeat.

At the end of the corridor stood a sign.

Not carved into stone.

Not painted.

A floating, cheerful sign, like a theme park.

> **WELCOME, BEGINNER HEROES!**  
> **THIS WAY TO THE TUTORIAL**

Mira clasped her hands. “Aw, it’s welcoming us!”

Velvet’s voice remained gentle. “It’s bait.”

Luminara smiled. “It’s adorable.”

Seraphina narrowed her eyes. “It’s indecent.”

Haruto blinked at her. “Indecent?”

Seraphina flushed. “Everything in this world is indecent.”

Haruto couldn’t argue.

They reached the end of the corridor and the dungeon opened into a circular room.

The ceiling was high, exposing open sky above like the island had been hollowed into a shrine.

In the center of the room sat a pedestal.

On the pedestal was a glowing crystal.

And above the crystal floated another window—bigger, bolder, with cheerful icons and a font designed to make danger look cute.

> **TUTORIAL ROOM #1 — MOVEMENT!**  
> Step on the highlighted tiles to proceed.  
> **Reward:** 1 treasure chest (guaranteed)

Haruto’s heart lifted despite himself.

Guaranteed treasure.

That sounded like a tutorial dungeon.

Then the System chimed.

> **TIP:** Guaranteed means “guaranteed to contain something.”

Haruto’s hope died quietly.

The floor tiles ahead lit up in a neat path of pale gold squares leading toward the pedestal.

Seraphina moved first, as if instinctively assuming the role of “Tank Walks Into Obvious Trap.”

Haruto opened his mouth to stop her, but she was already stepping onto the first glowing tile.

Nothing happened.

She stepped onto the second tile.

Still nothing.

She glanced back, chin lifted. “See? The dungeon tests resolve, not fear.”

Haruto pointed at the window. “It’s literally called a tutorial room.”

Seraphina ignored that and stepped onto the third tile.

Still nothing.

Mira clapped. “Yay! It’s safe!”

Velvet’s gaze stayed on the floor with clinical attention. “Not yet,” she said.

Luminara’s smile didn’t move, but her eyes gleamed with curiosity. “It’s waiting.”

Seraphina reached the fourth tile.

The moment her boot landed—

*CLICK.*

Haruto felt it through the stone, a mechanical sound that didn’t belong in a fantasy ruin.

Seraphina froze.

Haruto froze.

Even Mira stopped bouncing, which was the first true sign of disaster.

The tutorial window chimed again, cheerfully.

> **CONGRATULATIONS!**  
> You have learned Movement.  
> **NEW LESSON:** Consequences!

The glowing path vanished.

The tile under Seraphina dropped half an inch with a grim little *thunk*.

And all around the room, thin slits opened in the walls.

Haruto’s UI chimed a combat alert.

> **TRAP TRIGGERED:** Sunlance Volley  
> **Targets:** all entities with “high emotion”

Haruto’s brain caught on one phrase. “High emotion?”

Mira pointed at herself proudly. “That’s me!”

Velvet’s calm voice slid in like a blade. “Mira, do not be proud of that.”

Luminara’s eyes lit with amusement. “Oh, this is new.”

Seraphina snapped, “Everyone behind me!”

But the trap didn’t fire at her.

It fired at Haruto.

Because Haruto’s heart had spiked in panic.

And because, apparently, panic counted as “high emotion.”

Thin beams of concentrated light shot from the wall slits like divine lasers.

Seraphina lunged, slamming her sword into the floor.

Radiant runes flared along the blade, and a shield of light sprang up—an arcing barrier that caught the first volley with a shower of sparks.

Haruto blinked at her back.

She’d moved without thinking.

Without posturing.

Just… protected him.

The second volley hit.

Velvet raised her hand and a cool, watery glow wrapped around the barrier, reinforcing it like a second skin.

Luminara lifted two fingers and snapped them closed.

A sliver of sunlight dropped from nowhere and struck one of the wall slits, melting it shut with a hiss.

Mira, as always, did not react like a normal person.

She grabbed Haruto’s sleeve and pressed her chest against his arm like she was hiding behind him.

“Save me,” she whispered dramatically.

Haruto choked. “I’m not the tank!”

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased  
> Trigger: “pressed close”  
> **Trap upgrade:** additional volley unlocked

Haruto’s voice cracked. “WHY DOES IT UPGRADE THE TRAP?!”

Seraphina’s head whipped around, glare scorching. “Lady Mirabelle!”

Mira smiled sweetly. “Yes?”

Seraphina’s face went red with fury. “Stop—stop *using your chest as a weapon!*”

Mira’s eyes widened, delighted. “Oh my—Seraphina, I didn’t know you noticed.”

Luminara giggled.

The System chimed again.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (again)  
> **Note:** Your party is teaching the dungeon faster than it is teaching you.

Velvet stepped forward, calm still intact, and placed her hand lightly on Mira’s shoulder.

“Mira,” Velvet said softly, “if you flirt during trap mechanics, you will kill him.”

Mira blinked.

Then—miraculously—she let go of Haruto’s sleeve.

“Okay,” Mira said, suddenly serious. “No flirting during traps.”

The room felt quieter.

Haruto’s pulse eased.

The slits in the walls clicked closed, as if the dungeon was disappointed.

Seraphina exhaled, lowering the shield.

The tutorial window popped up again, cheerful as ever.

> **LESSON COMPLETE:** Consequences  
> **BONUS TIP:** Calmness reduces trap frequency.  
> **BONUS TIP:** Calmness is unattractive.  
> **Reward:** chest (guaranteed)

Haruto stared at “Calmness is unattractive.”

“This dungeon is bullying me,” he whispered.

Velvet’s mouth curved. “Yes.”

The pedestal crystal pulsed.

The chest appeared with a shimmer—golden, ornate, almost smug.

Haruto stepped forward carefully, avoiding every tile that looked even slightly excited.

He opened the chest.

Inside was… a small band of metal.

A ring?

No—too thick.

A cuff.

It was silver-white, etched with radiant filigree, glowing faintly as if it was happy to exist.

Haruto lifted it.

The moment his fingers closed around it, the System chimed triumphantly.

> **LOOT ACQUIRED:** **Radiant Party Cuff**  
> **Rarity:** Rare  
> **Effect:** +1 Party Morale, +1 Reputation (Radiant)  
> **Special:** **Binds to Player on pickup**

Haruto went still.

“Binds?”

The cuff snapped around his wrist.

Haruto yelped and tried to pull it off.

It didn’t budge.

Seraphina leaned in, eyes narrowing. “A binding accessory? Already?”

Luminara’s smile sharpened. “How forward.”

Mira leaned in from the other side, breath warm against Haruto’s ear. “It looks like a wedding thing.”

Haruto’s face caught fire. “It does not—!”

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased  
> **NOTE:** Binding loot increases drama.

Velvet took Haruto’s wrist gently—just enough to be intimate, not enough to be obvious—and examined the cuff.

Her thumb brushed his skin once, slow.

Haruto’s brain blanked.

Seraphina stiffened.

Luminara’s flame-hair crackled.

Mira made a satisfied little hum.

And the dungeon responded.

The runes on the floor pulsed faster.

The air grew heavy.

Somewhere deeper in the island, something *woke up*.

The System’s cheerful font flickered for the first time, as if even it had felt a chill.

> **NOTICE:** Dungeon difficulty recalculating…  
> **CAUSE:** “Party Morale dangerously high”  
> **CAUSE:** “Binding loot acquired”  
> **CAUSE:** “Multiple romance flags in proximity”

Haruto swallowed.

“Okay,” he said hoarsely, “new rule.”

Everyone looked at him.

Haruto raised his cuffed wrist like it was both proof and warning.

“No touching me,” Haruto said, voice trembling with sincerity and desperation, “unless it’s a life-or-death emergency.”

Mira pouted. “But everything is life-or-death in a dungeon.”

Velvet smiled serenely. “A wise boundary.”

Luminara’s eyes gleamed. “For now.”

Seraphina nodded sharply, as if relieved to have something she could call “discipline.”

“Agreed,” she said. “We proceed.”

The tutorial window popped one last message, almost smug.

> **LESSON 5 (SECRET):** Boundaries  
> **TIP:** Boundaries will be tested.

Haruto stared at it.

“I hate this place,” he whispered.

The dungeon’s gate ahead opened with a soft, eager click.

Like it had been waiting for him to say that.
