## Chapter 13 — Rivalry Mechanic Unlocked

The deeper they went, the more Sky Dungeon #7 started acting like it had opinions about their party composition.

Haruto didn’t mean that metaphorically.

He meant it literally, because the moment Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander walked side by side without trying to kill each other, the floor runes dimmed like they were disappointed.

Then, the moment Seraphina glanced at the commander with open suspicion, the runes brightened again—pleased, hungry, ready to monetize tension.

Haruto’s UI chimed as if reporting weather.

> **NOTICE:** Social volatility detected  
> **Dungeon preference:** “Rivalry”  
> **Tip:** Please argue.

Haruto muttered, “No.”

Sunny chirped, “Yes!”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull angled toward the orb. “I retract my earlier statement.”

Sunny chirped, delighted. “You can’t!”

“Noted,” Bone Legion Commander said. “I will still resent you.”

Seraphina’s voice cut in, sharp. “Do not waste attention on it.”

Luminara drifted behind Haruto with a smile that promised chaos and delivered it on schedule. “It is very hard not to waste attention on something that wants it.”

Velvet’s presence stayed calm at Haruto’s back, like a steady hand on the spine of a book trying not to fall apart.

Mira hummed cheerfully, because apparently she derived power from atmospheres that would make normal people cry.

The corridor widened into a room that looked less like a dungeon chamber and more like an arena designed by a game designer who had recently discovered the concept of “relationship mechanics.”

Two statues stood at opposite ends.

Left statue: a Radiant paladin with wings carved behind her helm, sword raised toward the ceiling.

Right statue: a skeletal commander with a cloak carved from stone, one hand extended like he was inviting someone to kneel.

Between them, the floor was tiled in a long strip of alternating gold and violet squares—like a board.

And in the center of that strip floated a window so cheerful Haruto wanted to throw up.

> **ROOM TYPE:** Rivalry Trial  
> **Objective:** Cross the board  
> **Mechanic:** “Competing Leaders”  
> **Bonus:** Don’t die (optional)

Haruto whispered, “Everything is optional.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** And yet you keep doing it.

Seraphina stared at the Radiant statue, then at the skeletal one. “This is insulting.”

Bone Legion Commander stared at the skeletal statue, then at the Radiant one. “This is accurate.”

Seraphina snapped, “Accurate?”

Bone Legion Commander replied, flat, “You are dramatic. I am disciplined. The dungeon is using the truth.”

Seraphina’s cheeks flushed. “I am disciplined!”

The floor tiles pulsed.

Haruto’s UI flashed red.

> **MECHANIC ACTIVATED:** Rivalry Meter  
> **Current:** 1/5  
> **Effect:** enemy spawn rate increases per argument

Haruto stepped between them reflexively.

“Stop,” Haruto said, voice tight. “It’s literally a mechanic.”

Sunny chirped, “Yay! A new meter!”

Haruto glared at Sunny. “This is your fault.”

Sunny chirped, “No it isn’t!”

Seraphina exhaled sharply, forcing her anger into a sheath. “Fine. We cross.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked once, approval or annoyance, impossible to tell. “Proceed.”

They stepped onto the first row of tiles.

Nothing happened.

Haruto’s shoulders loosened—just slightly.

Then the room spoke.

Not in a voice.

In UI.

Two windows appeared at once, one gold, one violet, floating above the tiled strip like opposing banners.

> **RADIANT CHALLENGE:**  
> **Declare your strategy.**  
> Reward: +1 morale  
> Failure: “cowardice” debuff

> **VEIL CHALLENGE:**  
> **Insult your strategy.**  
> Reward: +1 damage  
> Failure: “soft-hearted” debuff

Haruto stared.

“…It wants Light to declare and Shadow to insult,” Haruto said slowly.

Luminara’s smile turned wicked. “Perfect.”

Seraphina’s eye twitched. “Absolutely not.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull tilted. “Absolutely yes.”

The Rivalry Meter ticked up.

> **RIVALRY:** 2/5

Haruto inhaled hard. “No. We’re not doing that.”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm. “Haruto. The room is built to force the pattern. We must reframe it.”

Haruto swallowed, eyes flicking between the two prompts.

Declare strategy.
Insult strategy.

If they refused, they’d take debuffs.
If they obeyed, they’d feed the Rivalry Meter.

Haruto’s brain shifted again into “game logic” mode.

*Okay. If it wants a declaration and an insult… give it both without real hostility.*

Haruto raised his voice, addressing the Radiant window like it was a person.

“Our strategy,” Haruto declared, “is to clear this room with teamwork and survive.”

The gold window chimed happily.

> **RADIANT SUCCESS:** Morale +1

Then Haruto turned to the violet window and, with all the seriousness he could fake, said:

“Our strategy is also… extremely stupid.”

Seraphina’s head whipped toward him. “Player!”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked, amused.

The violet window chimed.

> **VEIL SUCCESS:** Damage +1

The floor tiles pulsed—then stayed steady.

Haruto’s UI updated.

> **RIVALRY:** unchanged  
> **Note:** Insult redirected to “plan,” not “person.”

Haruto exhaled in relief.

Sunny chirped, disappointed. “Aw, that was healthy.”

Haruto snapped, “Yes. It was.”

The room responded anyway, because it was petty.

The tiles ahead lit up in alternating gold and violet.

And from the shadows at the edges of the chamber, enemies crawled out like the dungeon had been waiting for an excuse.

Not husks.
Not skitters.

These were taller, more structured—armored shapes made of stone and mist, carrying blunt weapons carved from the same material.

Above their heads, labels appeared.

> **Duel Warden** — Lv. 3  
> **Trait:** targets the “loudest leader”

Seraphina’s lips parted in outrage.

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned slightly, as if offended by the idea he could be “loud.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** They will hit whoever tries to lead hardest.

Haruto’s stomach tightened.

“So… if Seraphina and the Commander compete, we die,” Haruto said.

Velvet’s voice stayed gentle. “Yes.”

Mira whispered, delighted, “This is like a relationship test.”

Haruto whispered back, horrified, “Why do you keep calling combat a relationship test?”

Mira smiled sweetly. “Because it is.”

Seraphina stepped forward, sword raised. “I will—”

Bone Legion Commander stepped forward at the same time. “I will—”

The Rivalry Meter flashed.

> **RIVALRY:** 3/5

Haruto threw both hands up like a referee.

“STOP,” Haruto shouted. “I’m leading this room.”

They both froze.

The Duel Wardens’ heads snapped toward Haruto like he’d just taunted them.

Haruto’s UI flashed.

> **AGGRO SHIFT:** Player has become “loudest leader”

Haruto’s stomach dropped.

Sunny chirped, delighted. “Oops!”

Haruto hissed, “Not oops!”

The first Duel Warden charged.

Its weapon swung in a heavy arc toward Haruto’s chest.

Seraphina moved instantly, intercepting the blow with her greatsword.

The impact rang through the room like a bell.

Bone Legion Commander stepped in a half-beat later, not to block—he flicked his hand and violet mist wrapped around the Warden’s legs, slowing its movement as if shadow itself had grabbed its ankles.

Velvet’s aura surged around Haruto’s feet, giving him a cool anchor.

Luminara’s heat-line snapped across the Warden’s shoulder joint, weakening the stone.

Mira—learning from earlier—bonked a smaller Warden quietly with her skillet.

Soft violence.

Effective.

Haruto’s heart hammered.

But he didn’t panic.

He watched the pattern.

These Wardens weren’t random.

They moved like chess pieces.

One advanced straight, heavy and slow—rook-like.

Another cut diagonally—bishop-like.

A third lurched in an L-shape, oddly fast—knight-like.

Haruto’s UI obligingly overlaid faint movement indicators.

> **OBSERVATION:** Enemy movement mimics “class roles”  
> **Tip:** Treat this like a board.

Haruto swallowed.

“Okay,” Haruto said, voice steady. “We beat the mechanic.”

Seraphina grunted, holding the main Warden back. “How?”

Haruto looked at Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander.

The room wanted them to compete.

So they needed to cooperate in a way that didn’t look like cooperation.

They needed to “compete” against the enemy instead.

Haruto pointed at Seraphina. “You call the next move.”

Seraphina blinked. “Me?”

Haruto nodded. “Short. Tactical. No speech.”

Seraphina’s jaw clenched.

Then she said, crisp and fast, “Left flank. Two tiles.”

The moment she gave the order, one Duel Warden shifted toward her like it had detected “leadership.”

Before it could reach her, Bone Legion Commander said, equally crisp, “Counter.”

He snapped his hand, and the Warden’s shadow stretched like a leash, yanking it off balance.

Seraphina’s sword flashed and shattered its arm at the joint.

The Warden crumbled into mist.

The System chimed.

> **COMBO SUCCESS:** Declared + Countered  
> **RIVALRY:** decreased (forced cooperation detected)

Haruto’s eyes widened.

They’d tricked the room.

Seraphina didn’t “lose” to the commander.
The commander didn’t “submit” to Seraphina.

They competed against the enemy together.

Luminara’s smile returned, sharp. “Oh. That’s good.”

Velvet murmured, pleased, “Yes.”

Mira whispered, delighted, “They’re co-parenting the violence.”

Haruto didn’t have time to be horrified by that phrasing.

“Again,” Haruto said quickly. “Seraphina calls. Commander counters. Luminara breaks joints. Velvet keeps us stable. Mira… softly bonk.”

Mira saluted with her skillet. “Soft bonk ready.”

Sunny chirped, “This is teamwork!”

Bone Legion Commander said, “Be silent.”

Sunny chirped, “No!”

The fight became a rhythm.

Seraphina: short commands, clean movement, no drama.

Bone Legion Commander: counters and debuffs, efficient and ruthless.

Luminara: heat-line precision, cracking weak points without feeding Radiant immunity.

Velvet: calm stabilization, healing timed like a metronome, no lingering touches—mostly.

Mira: quiet chaos, item support, and cookware violence delivered at indoor voice volume.

Haruto stood in the center and did the one thing he was starting to learn mattered most:

He kept them aligned.

Not by forcing them to like each other.

By making them useful to each other.

The last Duel Warden shattered into mist with a final combined strike—Seraphina’s blade cleaving, Bone Legion Commander’s shadow anchoring, Luminara’s heat cracking, Velvet’s aura sealing the splash, Mira’s skillet finishing like punctuation.

The room’s windows flickered.

The Rivalry Meter dropped.

> **RIVALRY:** 0/5  
> **Reward:** passage unlocked  
> **Note:** The dungeon is furious.

Haruto exhaled shakily. “Good.”

Sunny chirped, aghast. “You fixed it! That wasn’t supposed to happen!”

Haruto stared at the orb. “So you admit you’re evil.”

Sunny chirped, “I’m helpful!”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned. “I hate it.”

Seraphina wiped her blade with a practiced motion and, without looking at the commander, said, stiffly, “Your counters were… effective.”

Bone Legion Commander paused, then replied, equally stiff. “Your calls were acceptable.”

Mira whispered to Haruto, “That’s basically marriage.”

Haruto whispered back, “Stop saying that.”

Velvet’s voice was soft. “It’s progress.”

Luminara’s eyes glittered. “And entertainment.”

The door at the far end of the room unlocked with a clean click, the statues’ eyes dimming as if disappointed they hadn’t gotten their drama.

Haruto stepped forward, heart still racing.

He didn’t feel safe.

But he felt… capable.

And that scared him almost as much as the dungeon did.

Because if he could do this—

—then the gods would notice.

And the gods, he suspected, didn’t like players who learned too quickly.

As he crossed the threshold, the polished edge of the Radiant statue’s blade caught the light—just long enough to throw a reflection across the floor.

For the briefest flicker, Haruto saw someone who wasn’t in the room.

Not a party member. Not an enemy. Not a mirror-copy.

A slim silhouette in a hooded cloak the color of deep forest, standing above them like she’d been watching from a balcony that shouldn’t exist.

Two green points of light—eyes—met his for half a heartbeat.

Then the reflection slipped away, replaced by ordinary stone and the sound of their own breathing.
