## Chapter 26 — Jealousy Peaks (Town Becomes Dungeon)

The rumor reached Castle Lumora before Haruto did.

That was the problem with living in a world that treated gossip like a damage-over-time spell: the debuff applied whether you were in the room or not.

By the time Haruto stepped off the Veil supply cart with Ecto's arm still looped around his shoulder in a celebratory hug, the Golden Tankard's betting board had already updated.

> **RUMOR:** Player Betrayal (Radiant)  
> **Source:** "reliable observer"  
> **Effect:** faction volatility +12%

Mira read the board aloud like she was hosting a game show.

"'Haruto Takahashi seen escorting Veil civilians through restricted routes,'" she recited, chalk squeaking. "'Possible collusion. Possible romance. Possible *both.*'"

Seraphina's gauntlet creaked. "That is slander."

"It is *marketing,*" Mira corrected cheerfully.

Haruto's HUD chimed before he could argue.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** elevated  
> **Town modifier:** Social hazards enabled  
> **Tip:** Try not to exist.

Sunny chirped from inside the crate Mira kept under the bar like a pet that needed licensing. "Try harder!"

The first hazard arrived before Haruto finished reading the tip.

It wasn't a monster.

It was worse.

It was a *door.*

The tavern's back storage door, which had been perfectly normal wood five seconds ago, now displayed a floating label in cheerful gold script:

> **OBJECT:** Jealousy Trap (Minor)  
> **Trigger:** unresolved romantic tension within 3 meters  
> **Effect:** embarrassing spawn

Velvet, who had been wiping down a table with the calm of someone who had seen worse centuries, paused.

"Haruto," she said quietly. "Do not move."

Haruto had already moved.

The door burst open.

Not inward.

*Outward*—like the dungeon had decided architecture was optional.

Three small Radiant training dummies tumbled into the tavern, wobbling on weighted bases, swords raised in poses that suggested someone had taught chivalry to furniture and regretted it.

One dummy's painted face bore a crude likeness of Haruto.

The other two bore likenesses of Seraphina and Luminara, which was somehow more insulting to everyone involved.

Mira clapped. "Oh, I *love* when the town gets spicy."

Seraphina drew her blade in one smooth motion. "I will destroy those."

"Don't," Haruto said quickly. "That's—look at the UI—"

> **WARNING:** Destroying jealousy proxies increases spawn tier.

Bone Legion Commander's skull tilted toward the dummies with professional assessment. "Acceptable targets."

"Not acceptable targets," Haruto snapped. "They're *bait.*"

Chiller Executioner raised his axe. "I can bait them back."

"Nobody is baiting anything," Velvet said, and her voice carried the kind of authority that made even Chiller hesitate.

The dummies advanced anyway, because Lumora did not respect authority unless the authority came with a health bar.

Haruto stepped between Seraphina and the Haruto-dummy, which felt like the universe was making a point.

"Everyone breathe," he said. "We're in town. This is supposed to be safe."

The floor answered by flashing a new overlay across the tavern tiles—gold and violet squares, like the rivalry board from Sky Dungeon #7 had decided to retire in a pub.

> **ZONE CONVERSION:** Golden Tankard → Social Dungeon (Temporary)  
> **Mechanic:** Jealousy Aggro scaling  
> **Current Tier:** 2/5

Elara, who had been standing near the hearth with her cloak damp from the fog route, spoke for the first time since they'd entered.

"Correction pressure building," she said.

Not loudly.

Not dramatically.

Just true, the way weather reports were true.

Haruto's skin prickled. "How can you tell?"

Elara's emerald eyes flicked to his wrist, then to the party, then to the dummies still wobbling toward them like passive-aggressive parade floats.

"Because the world is *listening* to you argue," she said. "And it is getting bored of waiting."

That landed like a cold coin dropped down his spine.

Because she was right.

Seraphina and Luminara had been orbiting each other all morning with the brittle politeness of two suns sharing an orbit. Velvet's checkup from two days ago still hung in the air like perfume Haruto couldn't wash off. Mira's chalkboard had turned their private choices into public spectacle. And Bone Legion Commander—who Haruto had started trusting in the quiet, ugly way trust happened during supply runs—stood beside Seraphina like a challenge carved in bone.

Every glance was a mechanic.

Every pause was a spawn trigger.

Haruto felt it in his Party Leader Authority like a second heartbeat: the social layer wasn't flavor. It was *damage intake.*

The Haruto-dummy swung.

Seraphina intercepted, shield ringing.

The impact wasn't hard.

The *UI* was.

> **DISUNITY SPIKE:** +1  
> **Party Morale:** unstable  
> **Note:** "Protective" actions fueled by rivalry still count.

Haruto swore.

"Okay," he said, voice tight. "New rule. Nobody blocks for me unless I ask. Nobody declares for me unless I ask. Nobody *flirts* for me unless—" He stopped. "Actually that last one is always. Stop flirting as combat."

Luminara's smile was thin. "That is a large request."

"It's a *survival* request."

Ecto oozed forward, transparent body shimmering. "What if I hug the dummies?"

Haruto stared. "...What?"

"Hugs reduce hostility," Ecto said earnestly. "I read it on a loading screen."

Sunny chirped, muffled: "That was a joke!"

Ecto hugged the Seraphina-dummy.

The dummy cracked down the middle like a piñata filled with regret.

Confetti exploded—tiny paper hearts and faction emblems that dissolved into ash before they hit the floor.

The tavern laughed.

Then the laugh curdled.

Because outside, in the castle square, something heavy shifted.

Haruto felt it through the soles of his boots: a pulse, deep and formal, like the System clearing its throat.

The betting board erased itself mid-sentence.

Mira's chalk snapped.

Velvet's hand closed on Haruto's sleeve—not possessive, *anchoring.*

Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander both went still, instinct aligning across faction lines for the first time all day.

Elara's voice was barely above a whisper.

"There," she said. "That's the threshold."

Haruto's HUD stopped being cute.

The font changed.

The colors cooled from gold to slate gray.

And a window appeared in the center of the tavern, visible to everyone, spoken in a register Sunny had never used:

> **FORMAL SYSTEM WARNING**  
> **Morale exceeds safe operating thresholds.**  
> **Social cohesion anomaly detected.**  
> **Stand by for corrective event scheduling.**

The dummies froze mid-wobble.

The floor tiles stopped pulsing.

For one breath, the Golden Tankard was just a tavern again.

Then the window added a single line, colder than the rest:

> **Recommendation:** reduce attachment density or evacuate zone.

Mira whispered, "Well. That's rude."

Haruto looked at his party—Radiant and Veil and Verdant interrupt and one college kid who had never asked to be a raid lead—and understood, with sick clarity, that the town wasn't safe anymore.

It was a dungeon that had learned their names.
