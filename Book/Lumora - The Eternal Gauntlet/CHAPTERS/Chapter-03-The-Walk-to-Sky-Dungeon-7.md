## Chapter 03 — The Walk to Sky Dungeon #7

If Haruto had to describe Castle Lumora in one word, it would be:

**Extra.**

Everything about the Radiant capital was designed like it expected a camera to be filming at all times.

The corridors were wide enough for parade formations. The walls were marble so white they made Haruto’s eyes water. Sunlight poured in from skylights that absolutely did not need to be that large, and yet were, as if the building itself feared shadows more than taxes.

Even the air felt curated.

It smelled faintly of warm stone, polished metal, and some kind of holy incense that screamed, *We are morally superior and we would like you to notice.*

Haruto walked at the center of a formation he did not ask for and could not control.

Seraphina marched on his left, greatsword on her back, posture so perfect it looked painful.

Luminara floated a half-step on his right, robes fluttering in an indoor breeze that still refused to explain itself.

Velvet followed just behind him like an elegant shadow, hands folded, eyes half-lidded with calm authority.

And Mira—

Mira walked directly in front of Haruto, backwards, like the idea of personal space had never been patched into her character.

“So,” she said brightly, keeping pace with no effort at all, “first day in Lumora. How are we feeling, hero?”

Haruto tried not to look at the way her hair caught the light. Or the way her smile was clearly a debuff. Or the fact that every time she leaned closer, Seraphina’s armor made a faint, angry *creak.*

“I feel like I’m being escorted to my own trial,” Haruto said.

“Escorted?” Mira gasped. “This is a date. A heroic date.”

Seraphina’s voice snapped like a banner in the wind. “It is not a date.”

Luminara’s lips curved. “It could be.”

Haruto’s UI chimed softly, as if clearing its throat before delivering emotional harm.

> **STATUS:** Party travel initiated  
> **BUFF:** Radiant Parade (minor morale increase)  
> **DEBUFF:** Unwanted Attention (NPCs stare + gossip)  
> **NOTE:** You are very stare-able.

Haruto rubbed his forehead. “Why is the System like this?”

Velvet’s voice was gentle. “Because the gods made it.”

“The gods,” Haruto repeated. “Right. The bored ones.”

Luminara’s hair flickered. “You get used to it. Or you don’t, and you scream a lot.”

“Screaming lowers morale,” Mira reminded him with a wink.

“Panic lowers morale,” the System corrected immediately.

> **TIP:** Screaming is fine if it is funny.

Haruto stared at the air.

Seraphina leaned in slightly, lowering her voice as if sharing a sacred secret. “Player. Focus. Sky Dungeon #7 is not far, but it is dangerous.”

“It’s a starter dungeon,” Haruto said. “Isn’t it supposed to be… tutorial-level?”

Seraphina’s mouth tightened.

Luminara’s smile turned a little too sweet. “Oh, it’s a tutorial.”

Velvet nodded serenely. “It will teach you fear.”

Mira clasped her hands. “And teamwork!”

The System chimed with cheerful malice.

> **SKY DUNGEON #7:** Tutorial Dungeon  
> **Lesson 1:** Movement  
> **Lesson 2:** Traps  
> **Lesson 3:** Cooperation  
> **Lesson 4:** Romance Flags (Advanced)

Haruto stopped walking.

Everyone stopped with him, like the party’s AI had been tied to his anxiety.

“Why is romance lesson four,” Haruto said, voice hollow, “in a dungeon tutorial?”

Luminara shrugged as if the answer was obvious. “Because you’re here.”

Seraphina cleared her throat harshly. “Because the System is indecent.”

Mira beamed. “Because the System understands what the people want.”

Velvet’s smile stayed gentle. “Because the gods are cruel.”

Haruto resumed walking.

“Great,” he muttered. “I’m the patch note.”

They passed through a set of doors that opened on their own with the sigh of expensive hinges.

And the castle—impossibly—became even more.

Outside, the Radiant capital spread across a series of terraces and floating platforms, all connected by bridges of white stone and gleaming gold.

Above them, islands drifted in the sky like continents that had decided gravity was a suggestion.

Light flowed across everything, warm and constant, as if the sun had signed a long-term contract with the Dominion.

Haruto stood at the edge of a balcony and stared.

It was beautiful.

It was also the kind of beautiful that made you suspicious.

Because if the world looked like this on the surface… what did it look like when it wanted to kill you?

NPCs—no, people, he reminded himself; living beings with health bars—moved through the streets in orderly patterns. Radiant knights patrolled in pairs. Clerics stood near shrines, offering blessings and extremely polite judgment.

And everywhere Haruto looked, he saw the same thing:

Floating bars above heads.

Names.

Levels.

Titles.

Haruto spotted one noblewoman gliding by in a gown that sparkled like weapon-grade luxury.

Above her head:

> **Lady Sera Vantrell** — Lv. 12  
> **Title:** Patron of Charity  
> **Mood:** mildly scandalized

She glanced at Haruto’s group—at Mira leaning in too close, at Velvet’s calm smile, at Luminara’s hair literally on fire, at Seraphina’s simmering rage—

—and her “mildly scandalized” mood flickered to:

> **Mood:** extremely scandalized

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **GOSSIP DETECTED**  
> Local rumor seeds planted: 3  
> Estimated spread time: 12 minutes

Haruto whispered, “I hate it here.”

Mira’s grin widened. “No you don’t.”

Before Haruto could argue, a set of stone steps led them down into a broad plaza where a shrine stood in the center—an arch of marble wrapped in gold filigree, with a shallow pool of glowing water at its base.

The water hummed.

It felt like a checkpoint.

Seraphina stepped forward first and placed her hand over the pool, eyes closing in reverence.

Light flared.

The shrine chimed like a bell.

> **SHRINE ACTIVATED**  
> **Respawn Point Updated**  
> **Tip:** Don’t die. It wastes everyone’s time.

Haruto’s stomach dropped. “That’s a respawn point?”

Velvet nodded. “One of many. But yes. If you fall—”

“When you fall,” the System corrected.

Velvet continued smoothly, unfazed by the interruption, “you will return here.”

Mira’s fingers tapped Haruto’s shoulder. “See? Safe. Now you can be brave.”

Haruto exhaled. “That’s not how bravery works.”

“It is in games,” Luminara said.

The plaza opened into a bridge—one of the famous sky-bridges Haruto had seen from the balcony. Except this one wasn’t stone.

This one was light.

A ribbon of radiant energy stretched between platforms, shimmering as if it were woven from sunlight and stubborn faith.

Haruto stepped up to it and hesitated.

The bridge looked solid.

It also looked like it could vanish the second he trusted it.

Seraphina stepped onto it without pause. Her boots made no sound. The light held her weight like it wanted to be worthy of her.

“It’s stable,” she said, as if stability was her birthright.

Haruto put one foot on the bridge.

Warmth rose through his shoe, like stepping onto sun-heated stone.

The light didn’t bend.

It didn’t wobble.

It simply… accepted him.

Haruto released a breath he hadn’t realized he’d been holding.

The System chimed.

> **MOVEMENT TUTORIAL:** Light Bridges  
> **Rule:** Radiant bridges support those with “permission.”  
> **Rule:** Permission is a social mechanic.  
> **Rule:** Social mechanics are the real boss.

Haruto glanced at Seraphina. “Permission?”

Seraphina didn’t look back. “You are under my protection. The bridge recognizes that.”

Luminara drifted across as if she could float over nothing regardless. “The bridge recognizes drama,” she said pleasantly. “It loves drama.”

Mira skipped onto the bridge like she was walking into a tavern. “If the bridge is social, tell it I’m charming.”

The System chimed.

> **MIRABELLE:** Charm check successful.  
> **BRIDGE MOOD:** delighted

Haruto stared. “The bridge has a mood.”

Velvet stepped onto the light and walked as if it were a marble hallway. “Everything in Lumora has a mood,” she said. “It’s exhausting.”

Halfway across the bridge, Haruto felt it.

A prickle at the back of his neck.

That sensation of being watched.

Not by people.

By something that was evaluating.

He glanced down.

Far below, clouds drifted.

And in the space beneath the bridge, faint shadows moved in patterns that didn’t match the sunlight.

The System chimed, softer now.

> **WARNING:** Enemy presence detected  
> **Suggested action:** don’t fall

Haruto’s heart kicked.

“We’re being followed,” Haruto said quietly.

Seraphina’s head snapped up. “Where?”

Luminara’s gaze drifted lazily toward the shadows under the bridge. “Mm. Yes.”

Mira leaned in close to Haruto, whispering like she was sharing gossip. “Is this the part where you protect us?”

Haruto hissed, “I’m the one who needs protecting!”

Velvet’s eyes narrowed, calm sharpening into readiness. “Seraphina,” she said softly. “Shield.”

Seraphina didn’t hesitate.

She turned, drew her greatsword in one smooth motion, and planted it point-down into the light bridge.

Radiant runes flared along the blade.

The bridge brightened.

The air shifted.

Haruto’s UI chimed hard.

> **COMBAT ENCOUNTER:** Ambush (Minor)  
> **Enemies:** Sky Motes x6, Shade Skitters x3  
> **Modifier:** Jealousy Aggro (active)

Haruto blinked. “Jealousy aggro is active? We’re just walking!”

The System beeped.

> **TIP:** You are doing it wrong.  
> **TIP:** You are always doing it wrong.

The first enemy rose from below like a bubble of shadow.

It looked like a spider made of smoke and glass, legs too thin, eyes glowing faintly.

It skittered up the underside of the light bridge and then—

—phased through the light like it didn’t care about permission.

It landed on the bridge with a hiss.

Seraphina swung.

The greatsword cut through the air with holy certainty, and the creature shattered into drifting black sparks.

Haruto’s UI popped up a tiny message.

> **KILL CONFIRMED:** Seraphina  
> **Loot:** none  
> **Note:** dramatic swing bonus applied

“Dramatic swing bonus?” Haruto repeated.

Seraphina didn’t have time to be offended. Two more Shade Skitters climbed up.

Luminara lifted her hand, fingers graceful.

A thin ribbon of sunlight snapped out like a whip.

It struck the bridge just in front of the enemies and erupted into a flare that made Haruto’s eyes water.

The Shade Skitters shrieked, their bodies blistering under the light.

“Stay behind Seraphina,” Velvet said, voice calm. She raised her hand and a cool glow spilled out, wrapping Haruto in a faint veil of water-scented light.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **BUFF:** Ward (1)  
> **Applied by:** Velvet Dawn  
> **Note:** lingering touch bonus enabled

Haruto flinched. “Why is it called lingering touch bonus—”

Mira grabbed his wrist and yanked him sideways.

“Because she likes you,” Mira said brightly, and then hurled a small bottle at an enemy like she was throwing a grenade in a cooking show.

The bottle shattered.

A burst of glittering liquid exploded across the bridge.

The Shade Skitter hit it and—

—slipped.

Its legs scrambled.

It slid backward off the edge with a shocked, offended hiss, falling into the clouds like it had been betrayed by physics.

Haruto stared at Mira. “What was that?”

Mira winked. “Recovery drink.”

Haruto’s UI chimed, delighted.

> **ITEM USED:** Mirabelle’s “Recovery Drink”  
> **Actual effect:** Slippery (area), +1 morale, -1 dignity

Haruto opened his mouth to protest the dignity loss, but a new enemy appeared—this one a floating cluster of glowing motes, like a swarm of angry fireflies made of gold.

They swirled toward Haruto’s face.

Velvet’s hand flicked.

A wave of cool light pushed outward, washing over the motes.

They dimmed, sputtered, and dispersed like candles snuffed by tide.

“They’re attracted to emotional spikes,” Velvet said calmly.

Haruto blinked. “Emotional spikes?”

Velvet’s gaze flicked to Mira, then to Luminara, then—very briefly—to Seraphina.

Seraphina’s cheeks were pink with anger.

Luminara looked amused.

Mira looked delighted.

Haruto felt his own face warm.

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased  
> Trigger detected: “blushing”  
> **Reminder:** Your face is a crime scene.

Haruto choked. “I can’t control my face!”

“Yes you can,” Seraphina snapped, cleaving another creature in half. “Focus!”

Luminara giggled softly. “Oh, Seraphina. He’s adorable when he panics.”

Seraphina’s grip tightened.

Haruto’s UI chimed again.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (again)  
> Cause: “Ara ara incident”

Haruto shouted, “Stop saying ara ara! It’s summoning monsters!”

Luminara’s smile widened. “Ara ara~”

Three more Sky Motes spawned instantly, like the dungeon itself had been waiting.

Haruto made a sound of despair.

“I hate this world!” he yelled.

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Hate is also an emotion.

Seraphina’s sword flashed.

Luminara’s sunlight cracked like a whip.

Velvet’s aura held firm around Haruto, keeping the worst of the motes from biting into his HP.

Mira threw another bottle, then another, then—when she ran out—smacked a mote out of the air with a ladle she produced from nowhere.

Haruto didn’t ask where she’d been carrying the ladle.

He didn’t want to know.

The enemies fell quickly, dissolving into light and shadow particles that drifted away like confetti from a battle-themed festival.

Silence returned to the bridge.

Haruto’s heartbeat slowly stopped trying to escape.

The System chimed a neat summary.

> **ENCOUNTER COMPLETE**  
> **Damage taken:** minimal (thanks to Velvet)  
> **Most dramatic kill:** Seraphina  
> **Most unnecessary spawn trigger:** Luminara  
> **Most illegal item use:** Mirabelle  
> **Player contribution:** emotional instability

Haruto stared at the last line.

“Hey,” he said, offended. “I contributed by… being present.”

> **TIP:** Exactly.

Seraphina turned to him, breathing steady, but eyes sharp. “Now you understand. The dungeon does not simply test strength.”

Luminara’s voice was light. “It tests your ability to behave.”

Mira leaned in, grin wicked. “You’re doomed.”

Velvet’s gaze softened, and she stepped closer, adjusting his collar with a calm intimacy that made Haruto’s brain short-circuit again.

“You’re learning,” Velvet murmured. “That’s all that matters.”

Haruto’s face warmed.

The System chimed instantly.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (minor)  
> **NOTE:** You are not learning fast enough.

Haruto made a strangled noise.

Seraphina pointed forward with her sword.

The bridge ended at a platform where a towering arch of marble and gold rose like a doorframe to the sky itself.

Beyond it, Haruto could see a floating island suspended in the sunlight, its surface covered in pale stone and glittering vegetation, with ruins that looked *too* inviting.

And above the arch, in glowing letters that hovered in midair like a sign made by a sadistic developer, the dungeon’s name appeared.

> **SKY DUNGEON #7**  
> **WELCOME, BEGINNER!**  
> **WE WILL NOW EVALUATE YOU.**

Haruto swallowed.

“Okay,” he whispered, stepping toward the gate. “I’m ready.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** You’re not.

And the arch opened.
