## Chapter 27 — System Warning (Morale Dangerous)

The formal warning did not fade.

It *pinned* itself to the air above the Golden Tankard like a wanted poster for feelings.

> **FORMAL SYSTEM WARNING**  
> **Morale exceeds safe operating thresholds.**

Haruto stared at it until his eyes watered.

Sunny, who usually filled silence with chirps and bad advice, made a small sound from inside the crate—something between a hiccup and a glitch.

"…Sunny?" Haruto said.

The orb flickered through the slats, light stuttering. "Everything's fine! Probably! Don't worry about—" A pause too long to be comedy. "—scheduling."

Velvet's gaze sharpened. "That is not your usual register."

Sunny chirped, too bright. "New patch! New voice pack! Yay!"

Mira leaned on the bar, smile still in place but eyes calculating. "Voice pack my ass. The board's gone quiet."

She was right.

The betting chalkboard that had been weaponizing their love life for three days now displayed a single line in block letters:

> **WAGERS SUSPENDED.**  
> **Reason:** morale hazard.

Outside, Castle Lumora's square had developed the nervous energy of a crowd that knew a boss was loading but hadn't been told where to stand.

Radiant patrols tightened formation.

Veil scouts melted into fog pockets at the alley mouths, watching.

And in the middle of it all, ordinary NPCs did what ordinary NPCs did when the System got formal: they placed bets anyway, whispering odds on whether the Eternal Player would pick Light, pick Shadow, or explode.

Haruto hated all three options.

Arch-Justicar Halvion arrived with the Radiant delegation first—white armor, jaw set like a verdict waiting to happen.

"Player Takahashi," Halvion said, not bothering with greeting. "You will report to the Grand Radiant Hall for stabilization."

"Stabilization," Haruto repeated. "Like a patch."

"Like *order,*" Halvion corrected.

Syndic Madame Nyx Marrowveil materialized from a fog seam before Haruto could answer, veil-trimmed coat immaculate, smile sharp enough to cut glass.

"Or," Nyx purred, "you will come below for *freedom.* We don't chain our assets to marble floors."

"I'm not an asset," Haruto said.

Both factions answered, in unison, "You are *valuable.*"

Haruto's HUD chimed.

> **NOTICE:** Competing custody attempts detected.  
> **Effect:** Jealousy Aggro +1  
> **Tip:** Pick one owner for optimal narrative flow.

Sunny added, too quickly, "Optimal!"

The word landed wrong—flat, administrative.

Elara stepped into the space between delegations like she was inserting a wedge into a closing door.

"You're both doing the same thing," she said.

Halvion's eyes narrowed. "You are not recognized here."

Elara didn't blink. "Neither is the truth, apparently."

She looked at Haruto.

"If unity persists at this level," Elara said, "the world will spawn a correction. Not to punish you for being bad." Her voice stayed level. "To punish you for being *stable* across factions. The System treats that like a bug."

Haruto's throat tightened. "How do you know that?"

"Because the cyan route on your wrist flinches whenever the gods pretend they're in control," Elara said.

Velvet inhaled sharply—almost silent, but Haruto heard it.

Mira whispered, "Oh, she said the quiet part."

The System window above the tavern updated.

The font grew colder.

> **SECONDARY WARNING:** Social cohesion anomaly persists.  
> **Morale rating:** DANGEROUS (elevated affection density + cross-faction synergy).  
> **Containment recommendation:** assign Player to single faction anchor OR initiate public alignment event.

Haruto read *public alignment event* twice.

Sunny chirped, "That sounds fun!"

This time the chirp clipped at the end, like a recording skipping.

Haruto looked at the crate. "Sunny. Are you okay?"

Sunny's light pulsed erratic. "I'm helpful! I'm always helpful! Helpful is—" Another stutter. "—my primary function."

Velvet moved closer to Haruto, voice low. "Something is wrong with the tutorial layer."

"Maybe it's tired," Mira offered, too lightly.

"Maybe it's *leaking,*" Elara said.

Bone Legion Commander's jaw clicked. "Define leaking."

Elara's gaze didn't leave Sunny's crate. "Monitor fragments don't joke when the numbers go red. They *report.*"

The square went quiet enough that Haruto heard fog drip from the alley eaves.

Then his HUD flickered—brief, violent—and a quest window tried to spawn.

It was enormous.

World-scale.

The header pulsed in slate and gold:

> **WORLD EVENT (PENDING):** Corrective Raid — Alignment Trial  
> **Trigger condition:** public faction choice unresolved  
> **ETA:** calculating—

The window shivered.

Lines of text scrolled too fast to read—patch notes, maybe, or logs written in a language that felt older than Luminos and Umbrax.

Then the quest *vanished.*

Not completed.

Not failed.

*Dismissed*, like someone had yanked it back behind a curtain before the crowd could see the full name.

Haruto's wrist burned cold for half a second.

Cyan flicker in the corner of his vision—there and gone.

Sunny made a tiny, strangled sound.

"...Did anyone else see that?" Haruto asked.

Seraphina nodded once, grim. "I saw."

Nyx's smile thinned. "So did I."

Halvion's hand rested on his sword hilt. "The Player will choose Radiant alignment before this city pays the price."

Nyx laughed, soft and dangerous. "He'll choose *life* before your marble eats him."

Haruto looked at his party—Seraphina's fierce worry, Luminara's controlled fear, Velvet's steady pressure at his side, Mira's chalk-stained hands clenched on the bar, Bone Legion Commander's patient violence, Ecto's trembling sincerity, Chiller already bored with politics and ready to axe something, Elara's warning still hanging in the air like smoke.

He was so tired of being a mechanic in someone else's game.

"The price," Haruto said, voice rough, "is already here."

The formal warning pulsed once, acknowledging him.

> **Acknowledged.**  
> **Next step:** public alignment event scheduled.  
> **Location:** Grand Radiant Hall — outer steps.  
> **Time:** dawn.

The window held that last word like a blade.

**DAWN.**

Haruto exhaled.

"Fine," he said. "You want public. I'll give you public."

Velvet's fingers tightened on his sleeve—brief, then released.

Seraphina stepped closer, not declaring, just *present.*

Bone Legion Commander inclined his skull. "We will attend."

Elara's voice was quiet. "You should refuse them both."

Haruto met her eyes. "That's the plan."

Above the tavern, the warning did not fade.

But Sunny, for the first time since Haruto had arrived in Lumora, went silent.
