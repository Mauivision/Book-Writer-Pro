## Chapter 29 — Raid Prep (Forced Mixed Party)

The square didn't empty.

It *reconfigured*—marble folding into staging lines, fog pooling into role markers, civilians herded toward evacuation corridors by Radiant ushers who suddenly remembered they had jobs besides judging Haruto.

The World Boss notification hung above the Grand Radiant Hall like a second sun made of red text:

> **RAID ALERT:** Correction Warden — Phase Raid  
> **Requirement:** mixed-faction party (mandatory)  
> **Composition lock:** ENABLED  
> **Start:** when roster valid

Haruto stood at the center of the loading zone with Sunny's crate under one arm and his party scattered across faction lines like a diagram the gods hadn't approved.

His HUD opened a roster panel he couldn't close.

Nine slots blinked into place—each locked, each faction-tagged:

> **SLOT 1:** Party Leader — Eternal Player (locked)

> **SLOT 2:** Radiant Vanguard — Seraphina (locked)

> **SLOT 3:** Veil Vanguard — Bone Legion Commander (locked)

> **SLOT 4–5:** Support — Velvet Dawn / Ectoplasm Elemental (locked)

> **SLOT 6–7:** Flex DPS — Luminara / Chiller Executioner (locked)

> **SLOT 8:** Verdant Observer — Elara Sylvandrel (locked)

> **SLOT 9:** Logistics — Lady Mirabelle Heartflame (locked)

Then the panel flashed red:

> **Error:** roster exceeds standard raid size  
> **Override:** Party Leader Authority accepted

Nine slots.

One impossible team.

Mira whooped. "We're a bundle deal!"

Arch-Justicar Halvion stepped into the staging line with two Radiant knights flanking him. "This composition violates sanctified matchmaking. Replace Veil assets."

Nyx Marrowveil answered from the fog line before Haruto could. "Replace your assets, Halvion. The System locked the roster. Even your bureaucracy can't argue with a mandatory event."

"The Player chose heresy," Halvion snapped.

"The Player chose *people,*" Velvet said, voice calm enough to be a weapon. "Stand down or stand aside."

Halvion's knights didn't stand down.

They moved—subtle, professional—toward the roster edge where Ecto bubbled cheerfully unaware.

Bone Legion Commander's shadows snapped up a wall. "Attempt detected."

> **SABOTAGE:** faction hardliner interference  
> **Effect:** composition timer paused until resolved

Haruto's chest went cold. "They're trying to break the lock."

"Of course they are," Elara said beside him. She hadn't drawn her blade. She didn't need to. "Unity threatens their ownership models. Halvion wants you Radiant. Nyx wants you Veil. Neither wants you *yours.*"

Haruto stepped forward, Party Leader Authority humming in his bones—quiet command, not shout.

"New rule," Haruto said, voice carrying across the square. "No sabotage. No poaching slots. No 'accidental' friendly fire in prep. We survive this raid or the zone rolls back and *everyone* loses reputation. That includes you, Halvion. That includes you, Nyx."

Nyx smiled. "I prefer winning."

"Then win with us in the roster."

Silence.

Halvion's jaw worked. He waved his knights back—one inch, not surrender.

> **COMPOSITION TIMER:** resumed  
> **Time to gate:** 4:00

Prep chaos erupted the way only Lumora could manage—lethal stakes with tavern energy.

Mira opened her vendor tray and became logistics incarnate. "Potions! Bandages! Liquid Courage! Stop Being Weird! Limited stock because I am not a charity!"

"Buy everything," Haruto said.

Mira grinned. "Music to my ears."

Velvet moved down the line with triage efficiency—splint check on Seraphina's still-sore wrist from training, mana baseline on Luminara, shadow reserves on Bone Legion Commander, Ecto's hug-related overstimulation risk.

"You're all functional," Velvet reported. "Which is not a compliment. It's a minimum."

Seraphina raised her shield for inspection. "I can rally."

"Rally later," Haruto said. "Phase one punishes disunity. No declarations at the boss unless the UI asks. Argue with your feet, not your manifestos."

Bone Legion Commander unfolded a tactical overlay only he could see. "Phase assumptions: mirror mechanics, jealousy bait, authority severance on leader. Assign lanes now."

They ran assignments—Seraphina right vanguard, Commander left, Luminara and Chiller flex on caller rotation, Velvet mid-line triage, Ecto utility and morale, Mira rear logistics with strict orders not to hero.

Elara stayed near Haruto, eyes on the sky seam. "Correction Warden adapts to harmony. When you feel Unity spike, don't hoard it. Spend it before the boss learns the pattern."

"You say that like you've fought one."

"I've fought the *idea* of one." Elara's voice dropped. "The immune response you triggered isn't unique. It's a template. Templates get patched."

Sunny chirped from the crate—too bright. "Templates are fun!"

Velvet's glance at the crate could have sterilized instruments.

Luminara rolled her shoulders, heat shimmering. "Fear into focus. Tell me when."

"When the gate opens," Haruto said.

Chiller tested his axe edge on nothing, deadpan. "I will hit the boss until numbers go down or my arm falls off."

"Prefer numbers," Haruto said.

"Noted."

Hardliner sabotage didn't stop—it got petty.

A Radiant aide "accidentally" spilled sanctified oil across the Veil staging marker. Chiller slipped; Ecto caught him; Mira charged the aide triple for "hazard generation."

A Veil scout mimicked Halvion's voice ordering Seraphina to withdraw. Seraphina almost obeyed on reflex; Haruto's Authority ping cut through the fake command like static.

> **SABOTAGE:** voice spoof blocked  
> **Cost:** Party Leader Authority -3% (prep drain)

Haruto gritted his teeth. "Four minutes becomes three if we keep playing their game."

He looked at his party—Radiant and Veil and tavern and elf—and made the call that mattered more than any slot lock.

"No ownership," Haruto said. "Not Radiant's. Not Veil's. Not mine as symbol. We're a raid team because the world is trying to delete us for cooperating. That's the only allegiance that counts for the next ten minutes."

Seraphina met his eyes. Nodded once.

Bone Legion Commander inclined his skull. "Accepted."

Velvet's hand brushed Haruto's shoulder—brief, grounding. "Always."

Mira slapped a potion into his free hand. "Drink. You're doing leader face again."

The composition timer hit zero.

The sky seam above the Grand Radiant Hall widened.

The ground shuddered—not earthquake, *loading*—a deep rhythmic grind like the world turning gears behind the scenery.

> **ROSTER:** valid  
> **RAID GATE:** opening  
> **Tip:** good luck (sincerity not guaranteed)

The square's center dropped away.

Haruto grabbed Sunny's crate, looked at his locked, mixed, impossible party, and stepped forward as the gate pulled them down.

The sound was teeth on metal.

The raid began.
