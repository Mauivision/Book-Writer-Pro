## Chapter 01 — Loading Screen: Ordinary Guy Meets Truck-kun… Again?

Haruto Takahashi had a strict personal policy:

1) **Never** do anything that could be described as “a fate encounter.”  
2) **Never** trust any pop-up that appeared without being clicked.  
3) And above all, **never** stand too close to traffic when you were sleep-deprived, caffeine-powered, and holding a convenience store bag like it contained your last remaining will to live.

It was 11:48 p.m., which was basically morning for a college student who had just spent six straight hours pretending a group project was “almost done.”

The city was damp with early summer humidity. Streetlights made the wet pavement shine like a game map rendered on ultra settings—pretty, but suspicious. Haruto trudged along the sidewalk, hoodie half-zipped, earbuds in, and his thoughts drifting in that familiar, exhausted loop:

*I should’ve cooked.*  
*I didn’t cook.*  
*Now I’m eating convenience store karaage and calling it character development.*  

His phone buzzed with a notification.

**[Raid Invite: “Finals Week (Hard Mode)”]**  
**From: Kenji**  
**Message: bro you alive**  

Haruto stared at it. Considered replying with a dramatic monologue about suffering, the fragility of youth, and the inevitability of deadlines.

He typed: **“no”**

Then, because the universe had an impeccable sense of comedic timing, something huge and bright blurred into his peripheral vision.

Haruto’s brain—trained by years of gaming—didn’t register it as a vehicle at first.

It registered it as a **boss telegraph**.

*Big hit incoming.*

He turned his head.

A truck’s headlights filled his world like the world’s worst jump scare.

He didn’t have time to think, *Truck-kun?* like a normal isekai victim.

He only had time to think, *Of course it’s a truck. The genre has standards.*

Then—

Impact didn’t come.

Instead, everything cut to black like someone had slammed the power button on reality.

No pain.

No dramatic slow-motion.

No final words.

Just darkness.

And then a sound.

**DING!**

It was crisp. Cheerful. The kind of noise that belonged to a phone unlocking, or a toaster being proud of itself.

A rectangle of light flickered into existence inches from Haruto’s face.

> **WELCOME, ETERNAL PLAYER!**  
> **Initializing Summon Sequence…**  
> **Loading World: LUMORA**  
> **Difficulty:** *WHY IS THIS SET TO HARD?!*

Haruto blinked.

The window stayed there, perfectly centered like it had auto-snapped to his field of view.

He reached out reflexively—because of course he did. He’d been conditioned his whole life to poke glowing rectangles.

His fingers passed through it like it was made of glass, arrogance, and corporate policy.

“Okay,” Haruto whispered into the void. His voice sounded too close, like it had nowhere to go. “Either I finally died of caffeine… or my brain installed a game.”

The window chimed again, offended at his skepticism.

> **TIP:** Don’t panic! Panic lowers your **Morale** stat.  
> **TIP:** Screaming is permitted, but may attract **mobs**.

Haruto stared.

“…Mobs,” he repeated flatly.

**DING!**

> **TIP:** Mobs are groups of hostile entities.  
> **TIP:** You are also a hostile entity to someone, somewhere.  
> **Have a nice day!**

“That’s not—” Haruto began, but the darkness tore open before he could finish complaining.

Light poured in like a god had turned the brightness slider up and then snapped it off at maximum.

The world hit him all at once.

Cold marble slammed into his face.

Haruto made a noise that was not dignified. It was a sound of a human being reminded, very suddenly, that gravity existed and held grudges.

He rolled onto his side, blinking, and pushed himself up on shaking arms.

His palms met polished stone so smooth it felt unreal.

Haruto looked around.

He was in a hall so radiant it hurt to look at. Pillars of white marble rose into a domed ceiling painted with golden murals of winged warriors smiting things that probably deserved it. The air was full of drifting motes of light, like glitter that had developed self-esteem and learned to float.

Rows of armored figures stood in formation, perfectly symmetrical. Their armor was so clean and reflective it looked like the entire hall had been buffed by a dedicated janitorial religion.

At the far end, a dais glowed with warm gold.

And above him—because the universe was feeling theatrical tonight—a choir sang one sustained note, long and holy, like the world itself was holding its breath for him to say something protagonist-worthy.

Haruto stared up at all of it.

Then he opened his mouth and said the most honest thing he could think of.

“Is there a skip button?”

The choir note wobbled.

One of the knights in the formation coughed. It sounded like someone trying not to laugh in church.

Another window appeared in Haruto’s face, larger than the first.

> **QUEST ACCEPTED:** *The Eternal Gauntlet*  
> **Objective:** Survive your introduction.  
> **Reward:** Basic clothing (equipped automatically).  
> **Penalty for failure:** Embarrassment (permanent).

Haruto looked down.

He was wearing a “starter outfit” that could only be described as: *Default Protagonist Casual.*

It fit perfectly.

Of course it did.

He swallowed, because suddenly he realized something horrifying.

He wasn’t dreaming.

Dreams didn’t have UI.

Dreams didn’t have **penalties**.

And dreams definitely didn’t auto-equip your clothes.

A sharp *CLANG* rang out across the hall.

Someone had struck the marble floor with the pommel of a sword.

The sound echoed like punctuation.

A figure stepped forward from the radiant formation.

She was tall. Statuesque. Silver hair cascaded behind a winged helm like moonlight spilling out of a crown. Her armor gleamed white and gold with runes that pulsed softly, as if they were breathing.

She planted a greatsword in the floor with effortless strength and lifted her chin like she was about to deliver a season finale speech.

“Hero from another world!” she declared, voice ringing with conviction. “By the blessing of the Radiant Dominion, you have been summoned to Castle Lumora. I, Seraphina—”

She paused, narrowing her eyes as if the universe itself had annoyed her.

“—of the Radiant Dominion,” she continued, somehow even more dramatic, “shall protect you with my blade and my—”

Her gaze dipped.

Just for half a second.

The hall’s light reflected off her chest plate at an angle that made the metal shine with cruel honesty.

A faint flush rose to her cheeks.

“…pure heart,” she finished, just a little too quickly.

Haruto’s UI chimed again, as if delighted by the timing.

> **ROMANCE FLAG DETECTED:** *Radiant Paladin (Tsundere Variant)*  
> **Warning:** Jealousy-based aggro probability: **HIGH**

Haruto stared at the message.

Then he stared at Seraphina.

Then, because he had the survival instincts of a man who had once played dating sims as a joke and paid for it emotionally, he whispered, “I didn’t do anything.”

Seraphina’s eye twitched.

The choir note adjusted itself into something like “background tension.”

Haruto swallowed again, because her stare felt like a debuff.

Before Seraphina could launch into another vow, the ceiling above them brightened.

Not naturally.

Not the way light did when someone opened a window.

It brightened like the world itself had decided to show off.

A beam of sunlight speared down into the hall with the audacity of a special effect.

Within it, a second figure floated down as if gravity had been nerfed for her convenience.

She had hair like living flame—golden, shifting, and somehow perfectly styled. Her robes were white and amber silk, layered and flowing, caught in a wind that did not exist indoors.

She landed with a softness that made the marble seem clumsy.

Her smile was warm.

Which, judging by the sudden temperature spike, was not just a metaphor.

“Fear not, dear Player~” she purred, voice sweet like honey poured over a threat. “My solar magic will warm you in ways no mere knight ever could.”

She winked.

Seraphina’s grip on her sword tightened.

Haruto’s UI chimed again, practically giggling.

> **ROMANCE FLAG DETECTED:** *Solar Sorceress (Teasing Overlord)*  
> **Note:** This route contains dangerous dialogue options.

Haruto exhaled slowly through his nose.

Two flags.

Two gorgeous women.

Two entirely different varieties of “I am about to be emotionally bullied.”

He was beginning to understand what the real penalty of this world was.

The sorceress tilted her head, golden eyes shining with amusement. “You seem quiet. Are you overwhelmed?”

Haruto wanted to say, *No, I’m fine, I’m a functional adult.*  

Instead, his mouth said, “I’m… loading.”

Luminara’s smile widened. “Ara ara~”

Seraphina took a step forward, armor practically radiating indignation. “Enough! Player, you are under the protection of—”

The floor cracked.

Haruto felt it through the marble, a vibration like a bass drop.

The choir note went sharp.

A jagged line split the radiant floor with a thunderous *KRRAK* and then—

The world’s foundation exploded upward.

Stone fragments flew.

Dust and glittering light particles mixed in the air like the universe couldn’t decide if it was holy or just messy.

Three figures surged up through the broken marble like they were crashing a party they had not been invited to.

The first was a skeleton in a warlord’s mantle, bones polished like ivory and decorated with dark metal clasps. His skull turned side to side, jaw clacking with disgust.

Behind him strode a tall man in a hood, dragging an axe so large it scraped the stone with a screaming metallic shriek. He looked like he’d been born in a shadowy corner and raised by questionable life choices.

And between them bounced a wobbling mass of glowing ectoplasm—a blob that sparkled like liquid moonlight and moved with the energy of an over-caffeinated jelly.

The skeleton pointed at Haruto with military precision.

“The Veil Syndicate demands equal recruitment rights,” he announced, voice somehow both hollow and authoritative. “This Player is ours too.”

Seraphina’s eyes widened. “Undead filth—!”

Luminara’s smile didn’t change, but the air around her shimmered with heat. “Oh? How bold.”

Haruto’s quest window updated itself like it was taking notes.

> **OBJECTIVE UPDATE:** Survive your introduction.  
> **NEW CONDITION:** Also survive *everyone else’s* introduction.

The hooded axe man swung his weapon absentmindedly, as if testing the weight.

The blade arced too close to the ceiling.

There was a soft *tink*.

Then a chandelier—made of crystal and aggressively sacred aesthetics—split neatly in half.

It fell.

Time slowed.

Haruto watched, fascinated, as glittering shards of light rained down like the world’s most expensive mistake.

The axe man blinked.

“…Oops,” he said.

Somewhere, the UI chimed, delighted.

> **ENVIRONMENTAL OBJECT DESTROYED!**  
> **Respawn in:** 3… 2…

The shattered chandelier flickered, dissolved into light particles, and reformed instantly back on the ceiling as if nothing had happened.

Haruto’s eye twitched.

“Okay,” he muttered. “So the chandelier can respawn too. Great. I’m definitely not in a nightmare. This is totally normal.”

The ectoplasm blob bounced closer, tendrils forming a heart shape midair.

“Group hug?” it asked in a voice like a happy bubble. “Group hug!!”

Seraphina recoiled as if the blob had suggested a war crime. “Absolutely not!”

The skeleton warlord—Bone Legion Commander, Haruto assumed, because he looked like he’d file reports about everything—clacked his jaw once in annoyance.

“Maintain formation,” the skeleton snapped at his own team, though there was no formation. “We are not here to fraternize. We are here to secure the asset.”

Haruto flinched at the word “asset.”

“I’m right here,” he said. “And I’m not—”

The side doors of the Grand Radiant Hall burst open with the force of someone who believed the plot should revolve around them.

A woman with bright red hair stormed in like a living exclamation point.

She wore a tavern mistress apron over a low-cut outfit that looked suspiciously like “holy barmaid cosplay,” and she moved with the confidence of someone who had never been told “no” in her entire career.

She spotted Haruto and her face lit up like she’d just found a rare drop.

“New hero!” she chirped, already closing the distance. “You look exhausted from all that summoning.”

Before Haruto could protest, she threw her arms around his shoulders and pressed close, warm and unapologetically affectionate.

Haruto’s brain short-circuited.

His UI chimed in betrayal.

> **BONUS NPC ENCOUNTER!**  
> **Warning:** Morale may exceed safe limits.

The red-haired woman laughed softly and leaned in, voice dropping into the kind of tone that belonged in a tavern at midnight.

“Come to the Golden Tankard,” she whispered. “I’ll give you my special recovery drink… and maybe a lap pillow if you’re good~”

Seraphina made a sound that could only be described as *holy outrage.*

Luminara’s eyebrows lifted in amused approval. “Mm. Aggressive.”

The skeleton warlord’s skull turned, eye sockets narrowing like he could glare through absence. “Who is this?”

“Lady Mirabelle Heartflame,” the woman announced brightly without letting go of Haruto. “But everyone calls me Mira.”

Haruto attempted to inhale oxygen and dignity at the same time.

Then his other side went warm.

Not sunlight warm.

Soft, gentle warm—like hands that knew exactly what they were doing.

Another woman had appeared beside him without making a sound.

Lavender hair flowed over her shoulders in perfect waves. Her figure was curvaceous beneath elegant cleric robes, and the calm on her face suggested she could handle a crisis, a war, and a gossip storm without smudging her makeup.

She placed a glowing hand flat against Haruto’s chest.

“For healing purposes,” she said, voice quiet and intimate.

Haruto froze.

His starter outfit did not come with “how to respond when a beautiful healer touches your sternum with divine intent.”

The cleric smiled, eyes half-lidded like she was evaluating him.

“Don’t listen to the tavern wench, my light,” she murmured. “My chambers have much softer beds… and divine massages that last all night.”

Haruto’s soul left his body and requested a refund.

The UI chimed again.

> **ROMANCE FLAGS TRIGGERED:** 2 new entries detected.  
> **Warning:** Conflicting routes may cause **Jealousy Aggro**.

Seraphina’s eye twitched violently.

“Unhand the Player at once,” she hissed, voice trembling with righteous fury, “you… you flirtatious heathens!”

Mira blinked innocently. “I’m not a heathen, sweetie. I’m hospitality.”

The cleric’s smile didn’t move. “High Priestess Velvet Dawn,” she introduced herself with the calm confidence of someone who had never lost an argument. “And I am simply providing care.”

“You’re providing—” Seraphina choked on her own outrage. “That’s not—”

Luminara stepped closer, solar flames flickering along her hair in tiny, adorable sparks that looked harmless until you remembered she controlled the sun.

“Ara ara~” she purred, gaze drifting between Mira and Velvet with the delighted cruelty of a cat observing two birds. “Looks like we have competition.”

The skeleton warlord took a sharp step forward, cloak flaring dramatically despite the lack of wind. “This is irrelevant. The Veil Syndicate—”

“Irrelevant?” Mira repeated, affronted. “Honey, I run the Golden Tankard. Nothing involving a cute new hero is irrelevant.”

Velvet’s hand remained against Haruto’s chest. “His heart rate is elevated,” she noted serenely. “He requires—”

“Space,” Haruto croaked, finally finding his voice. “I require space.”

The hall went quiet.

For one miraculous second, everyone’s attention focused on him.

Haruto took the chance to breathe.

Then a tutorial window appeared directly in front of his nose.

> **TUTORIAL:** Social Encounters  
> **Tip:** Choosing dialogue options carefully can prevent combat.  
> **Tip:** Failure may result in **Jealousy Aggro**.  
> **Tip:** Jealousy Aggro is your fault.

Haruto stared at the last line.

“…Excuse me?”

The window beeped.

> **Tip:** Don’t argue with the System.

Seraphina raised her sword with a dramatic flourish that was ninety percent heroic and ten percent “if I don’t hit something I will explode.”

“Enough!” she declared. “Player, you will stand behind me. I shall—”

Bone Legion Commander stepped forward at the same time, spine straight as a spear.

“The Player will stand with us,” he countered. “The Veil Syndicate claims equal—”

Chiller Executioner rested his axe on his shoulder, unimpressed. “Can we just… pick already? This hall is too bright.”

Ectoplasm Elemental bounced in place, tendrils shaping into a giant glowing heart that floated above everyone like a banner.

“Group hug!” it insisted. “Group hug solves everything!”

“No it does not!” Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander snapped in perfect unison.

Haruto watched them, stunned.

Their timing was identical.

They sounded exactly like two rival guild leaders who had rehearsed their hatred.

Luminara’s lips curved. “How cute.”

Mira squeezed Haruto’s shoulder cheerfully, ignoring the blade nearby like it was a mild inconvenience. “So,” she said, “what kind of party do you like, hero? A disciplined one? A spicy one? A healing one?”

Velvet leaned in a fraction closer, her voice a whisper that somehow made the holy hall feel like a private room. “You may choose all of us, if you wish.”

Haruto’s face went hot.

He had never been so aware of his own existence.

The choir—apparently desperate to remain relevant—tried to swell into a dramatic harmony.

It was immediately drowned out by another UI chime.

> **WELCOME, ETERNAL PLAYER!**  
> **Choose your starter harem—**  
> **…I mean, party!**  
> **Recommended composition:** Tank / DPS / Support / Emotional Support

Haruto’s eyes widened. “It said—”

Seraphina’s face turned red. “It did not say that!”

Mira’s grin could have powered a small city. “Oh, it absolutely did.”

Velvet’s smile stayed serene. “The System is… very honest.”

Luminara tilted her head, flames flickering in amusement. “And very cruel.”

Bone Legion Commander jabbed a bony finger at the window. “This is propaganda.”

The UI responded instantly, as if listening.

> **NOTICE:** Disrespecting tutorial content lowers **Reputation**.  
> **Bone Legion Commander:** -1 Reputation (Veil) for “public negativity.”  
> **Seraphina:** +1 Reputation (Radiant) for “dramatic leadership.”  
> **Haruto:** +2 Stress (permanent).

Haruto made a strangled sound.

“Excuse me?” he asked the air. “Why do I get permanent stress?”

> **TIP:** Because you are the protagonist.

Haruto shut his eyes.

He wanted to wake up.

He wanted to wake up in his tiny apartment with his overdue assignment and his lukewarm convenience store karaage.

He wanted to be normal again.

Instead, he opened his eyes to a hall full of gorgeous, dangerous people arguing over him like he was a limited edition item.

And the System—this world’s invisible narrator—was watching like it had popcorn.

The starter selection window expanded, glossy and cheerful, listing options with little icons:

**[Seraphina — Radiant Paladin]**  
**[Luminara — Solar Sorceress]**  
**[Mirabelle — Tavern Support (Morale)]**  
**[Velvet — High Priestess (Healing)]**  
**[Bone Legion Commander — Veil Tactician]**  
**[Chiller Executioner — Veil DPS (Axe)]**  
**[Ectoplasm Elemental — ??? (Hugs)]**

Haruto’s mouth opened.

No sound came out.

Because the sheer absurdity of it hit him like a delayed damage tick.

*This is a game.*

*But it’s also real.*

And for some reason, the world had decided that he was the “Player.”

The System chimed one more time, as if cheering him on.

> **PARTY MORALE:** dangerously high.  
> **ROMANCE FLAGS:** triggered.  
> **DIFFICULTY:** increased by **300%** due to jealousy mechanics.

Haruto stared at the words.

Then at the arguing paladin.

Then at the smiling sorceress.

Then at the tavern mistress clinging to him like a buff.

Then at the serene priestess whose hand was still “healing” his chest.

Then at the skeleton warlord who looked ready to draft a contract in blood (or whatever skeletons used).

Then at the axe man who had already murdered a chandelier just to make a point.

Then at the blob that wanted everyone to cuddle.

Haruto swallowed.

“Okay,” he said weakly, because it was the only thing his brain could assemble. “So… what happens if I pick wrong?”

The System window blinked.

> **ANSWER:** You will.

And the Grand Radiant Hall erupted into chaos again.
