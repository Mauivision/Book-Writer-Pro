## Chapter 32 — World Boss Phase 3 (Unity Override)

Phase three didn't announce itself with fanfare.

The arena *changed*—mirrors melting, duel strips collapsing, the Correction Warden shedding its courtroom skin until what remained looked like a judge built from raid mechanics and bad intentions, visor scrolling a single line:

> **PHASE 3:** Harmony Punishment  
> **Rule:** healthy choices become damage sources  
> **Counter:** Party Unity Override (unauthorized)

Haruto felt Authority Severance still clamped on his wrist—the gray debuff from phase two, recruitment and override functions locked, the System insisting he was an anomaly that needed sorting.

The Warden raised both gavel-hands.

Golden light erupted—not at the party, *through* them. Every time Seraphina adjusted her grip to protect someone, her health bar dipped. Every time Velvet moved to heal, the Warden counted it as collusion. Every time Ecto hugged morale back into range, the arena flagged it as exploitative warmth.

> **PARTY DAMAGE:** 8% (support action)  
> **PARTY DAMAGE:** 6% (shield adjustment)  
> **Note:** "being good at teamwork" detected

Luminara swore. "It wants us selfish."

"It wants us alone," Elara said, blade drawn, cyan edge flickering at the Verdant limit. "Override isn't a buff. It's a confession. You tell the world you're choosing each other louder than the rules allow."

Haruto opened his skill panel.

The grayed-out option had been waiting since his first cross-faction recruit—**Party Unity Override**, labeled PROVISIONAL, tagged UNAUTHORIZED, cost unknown.

The Warden turned its visor toward him.

A window opened for the whole arena:

> **DEPLOY OVERRIDE?** Y/N  
> **Warning:** marks Party Leader; escalates Correction tier; gods notified

Seraphina staggered from another harmony-punish pulse. Velvet's hands glowed, then faltered as healing triggered damage. Bone Legion Commander's shadows frayed holding the line.

Haruto didn't look at the gods.

He looked at his party.

"No ownership," he said—not to the boss, to them. "Just us. On purpose."

He selected **Y**.

---

Override didn't feel like power.

It felt like ripping a scab.

Cyan light erupted from Haruto's chest—not Radiant gold, not Veil violet, something the UI didn't have a color swatch for. Party Leader Authority surged past its designed ceiling, linking the roster in visible tether lines: Seraphina's shield, Commander's shadows, Velvet's triage, Luminara's heat, Ecto's sincerity, Chiller's violence, Mira's chaos logistics, Elara's Verdant edge.

> **PARTY UNITY OVERRIDE:** ACTIVE  
> **Effect:** faction/debuff rules suspended (party scope)  
> **Cost:** leader HP + morale tanking  
> **Duration:** until Warden dismisses or leader collapses

Haruto screamed.

Not dramatization—actual pain. Override routed every harmony-punish pulse through him first, converting social damage into something his body could carry so the party could act.

Velvet grabbed his arm. "Too much—"

"Keep working," Haruto gasped. "That's the point."

Seraphina didn't declare at the boss.

She declared at *him*—voice raw, no theater:

"You are not alone."

Bone Legion Commander: "Confirmed."

Luminara, heat white-hot: "We choose the kid with instincts."

Ecto, hugging the tether line instead of a person: "Group hug authorized!"

Chiller: "I hit things for you. That's my love language."

Mira, rear line, throwing potions like grenades: "Drink or I bill you!"

Elara, cyan flaring: "Unity isn't peace. It's coordinated defiance."

Each voice landed like a keystroke in a command the System hadn't approved.

The Warden struck.

Override absorbed, redirected, shared—Haruto's health bar cratered, then stabilized as Found Family logic kicked in, messy and real.

> **HIDDEN STAT:** Unity +5  
> **Boss state:** adaptation failed  
> **Correction response:** logging outcome

The Warden's visor accelerated—ERROR, ERROR, ROLLBACK PENDING, OVERRIDE NOT IN PATCH NOTES—

Haruto forced himself upright, Sunny's crate rattling at his feet, the orb glitching between chirp and flat log voice:

"—unity threshold exceeded—deploying—no wait—"

"Not today," Haruto snarled, and pushed Override one beat longer than his body wanted.

Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander moved in synchronized lanes—not flirting, not posturing, *fighting*—shield and shadow breaking gavel-arms while Luminara melted the visor's scroll speed and Chiller axed through plate labeled JEALOUSY AGGRO.

Velvet healed Haruto through the damage loop—Override making triage legal again.

Elara's blade cut a cyan arc across the Warden's core—not destroying, *dismissal targeting*, the way you closed a ticket the world couldn't resolve.

The Correction Warden froze.

Its visor displayed one final line for everyone to read:

> **OUTCOME:** immune response insufficient  
> **Action:** dismiss raid entity; queue escalation  
> **Note:** Eternal Player flagged for continued monitoring

It didn't die.

It *logged*—armor folding into slate light, body dissolving into UI static, the arena shuddering as the boss entity was dismissed like a bad audit closed without admission of fault.

Silence.

Override cut.

Haruto collapsed to one knee, health at thirty-one percent, wrist burning with cyan afterimage that wouldn't fade.

Velvet caught him before his face hit marble.

"Marked," she whispered—clinical word, scared eyes.

Haruto nodded. He couldn't explain Override without sounding like he'd hacked godhood.

He could explain this:

They were alive.

Together.

On purpose.

The sky seam above began to seal.

Phase three ended not with victory fanfare, but with a single quiet notification only Haruto's HUD showed:

> **Unity Override:** recorded  
> **Escalation:** queued

Somewhere above the raid zone, the Patch Herald cleared its throat for notes that hadn't published yet.

Haruto leaned into Velvet's steadying hand and let his party breathe.

The Warden was gone.

The correction pattern had just begun.
