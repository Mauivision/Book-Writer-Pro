## Chapter 02 — Choose Your Starter Harem— I Mean, Party!

The Grand Radiant Hall was too clean for this.

That was Haruto’s first coherent thought as the argument escalated from “loud” to “boss music.”

Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander squared off like mirrored cutscenes: both rigid posture, both convinced the universe had assigned them the role of *Main Responsible Adult.*

Luminara watched with the faint smile of someone witnessing a predictable tragedy.

Mira hugged Haruto like she’d looted him from a chest.

Velvet’s hand stayed against his chest in the exact spot where his soul was currently trying to flee.

And above all of it, the System hovered—bright, cheerful, and villainous in the way only a friendly UI could be.

> **STARTER PARTY SELECTION**  
> Welcome, Eternal Player!  
> **Recommended:** 3 companions  
> **Bonus Slot:** unlockable via “Teamwork” (or “Drama”)  
> **Warning:** Jealousy Aggro is enabled by default.

“I would like to disable jealousy,” Haruto said immediately.

The window blinked.

> **NOTICE:** This is a story.  
> **NOTICE:** You may not disable plot.

Haruto’s shoulders sagged. “Of course.”

Seraphina snapped her gaze to the floating window like it had personally insulted her honor. “Player, ignore these… these indecent messages. Stand behind me. I shall explain everything.”

Bone Legion Commander pointed at Haruto. “He will stand *with* us. The Veil Syndicate has equal recruitment rights.”

“Recruitment rights?” Seraphina echoed, scandalized. “You cannot simply burst through our sacred floor and—”

Chiller Executioner dragged his axe a half-step, and the sound of metal on marble made several Radiant knights flinch.

“We can and did,” he said, bored. “This floor was asking for it.”

“The floor was not—” Seraphina started.

Ectoplasm Elemental bounced between them, wiggling with joy. “Everybody’s so loud! That means we’re friends!”

“That is not what that means!” Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander snapped again.

Haruto raised both hands. “Okay. Pause. Time-out. I’m the one being… recruited. Can someone tell me where I am, what I am, and why the chandelier respawned?”

Mira’s eyes sparkled. “Oh, honey, you’re in Castle Lumora. And you’re adorable.”

Velvet’s voice was soft as silk, her smile like a blessing with conditions. “You are the Eternal Player. A rare phenomenon. A miracle.”

“A problem,” Bone Legion Commander corrected, which was somehow worse.

Luminara’s hair flickered like a candle flame catching interest. “A variable,” she said, sounding pleased. “Finally.”

Seraphina lifted her sword a fraction, as if the word *variable* was a curse. “You are a hero. Summoned by the Radiant Dominion to protect this world.”

Haruto stared at her. “Protect it from what?”

Seraphina’s jaw tightened.

Bone Legion Commander’s skull angled slightly, eye sockets narrowing.

Mira hummed thoughtfully, still pressed against Haruto’s arm like this was a tavern booth and not an international incident.

Velvet’s hand warmed. “From the Gauntlet,” she said simply.

The System chimed to emphasize her point.

> **MAIN QUEST:** The Eternal Gauntlet  
> **Status:** Active  
> **Current Objective:** Clear **Sky Dungeon #7**  
> **Failure Condition:** World instability (rollback risk)  
> **Bonus Objective:** Try not to start a faction war. (Optional)

Haruto mouthed the words. “Rollback risk.”

Luminara’s eyes softened just a fraction. “The world… resets,” she said. “Not like time travel. Like… the server deciding we were too messy.”

Mira gasped. “Rude.”

Seraphina’s posture stiffened. “The Radiant Council will handle such matters.”

Bone Legion Commander clacked his jaw once. “Your Council handles matters with speeches.”

Seraphina’s eyes flashed. “And your Syndicate handles matters with vandalism.”

Chiller Executioner shrugged. “Efficient.”

Haruto tried to process it. A world that could reset. Factions. Dungeons. Boss events. He wanted to ask about his family, his phone, whether he could go home—

—but the System didn’t wait for existential dread to finish loading.

The starter selection window expanded, glossy and bright, like it had just discovered the joy of ruining a person’s life.

> **SELECT UP TO 3 COMPANIONS**  
> Party composition recommended: **Tank / Damage / Support**  
> **Note:** Emotional support counts as support.  
> **Note:** “Emotional damage” also counts as damage.

Haruto turned his head slowly toward Luminara. “Is it always like this?”

Luminara smiled. “It’s worse during events.”

Seraphina stepped forward, voice swelling like an orchestra behind her. “Player! Choose wisely. The Radiant Dominion offers you honor, safety, and the purest light.”

Mira leaned into Haruto’s ear, her whisper warm and wicked. “And the Radiant Dominion also offers you my tavern. Which is safer than any dungeon. I have pillows.”

Velvet’s fingers pressed gently, just enough to remind Haruto that she could probably stop his heart and restart it if she felt like it. “I offer you healing, guidance, and a sanctified bedchamber for… recovery.”

Haruto’s face went hot again. “Why does everyone keep mentioning beds?”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Beds restore HP.  
> **TIP:** Beds also restore morale.  
> **TIP:** Morale is dangerous.

Haruto exhaled. “This place is cursed.”

Bone Legion Commander stepped in with military crispness, as if presenting a report. “The Veil Syndicate offers practical advantages. Information. Access to underground routes. Curse-tech. And discipline.”

“Discipline,” Mira repeated, grinning. “That’s one word for it.”

Chiller Executioner tapped his axe head against the floor, making a small dent that immediately shimmered and healed. “We also offer… not being blinded by gold paint all day.”

Ectoplasm Elemental bounced higher. “And hugs!”

Seraphina’s eye twitched. “Absolutely no—”

Ecto leaned toward her, tendrils forming a tiny heart. “One hug?”

Seraphina’s jaw clenched so hard Haruto could hear it. “No.”

Ecto deflated slightly. “Never hug?”

“Not—” Seraphina stopped, visibly wrestling her own morality like it was a debuff. “…Not during recruitment negotiations.”

Ecto brightened. “Later hug!”

Bone Legion Commander stared at Ecto with the expression of a man whose army contained a chaos gremlin he could not discharge. “Control yourself.”

Ecto saluted with a tentacle. “Yes, boss!”

Haruto rubbed his temples. “Okay. I’m going to make a team. Like an actual team. With roles.”

The System chimed again, instantly supportive in the way a shark was supportive of blood.

> **LEADERSHIP DETECTED**  
> Unlock condition progress: **Teamwork** +1  
> Also: **Drama** +2

Seraphina straightened, clearly believing this was her moment. “Then you will choose me as your shield.”

Luminara’s hair flickered brighter. “And me as your firepower.”

“And me,” Mira purred, squeezing Haruto’s arm, “as your personal morale booster.”

Velvet’s smile widened by a millimeter, which was somehow more threatening than a full grin. “And me, as your healer.”

Bone Legion Commander clacked his jaw. “You cannot take all four. The System said three.”

Haruto looked at the window helplessly. “Can I negotiate with it?”

The window blinked, as if considering.

> **NEGOTIATION MODE:** Available  
> **Cost:** 1 embarrassment  
> Proceed?

Haruto didn’t even know what “1 embarrassment” meant in a world that measured humiliation like currency.

He chose “Proceed.”

The hall shimmered slightly, like reality had taken a screenshot.

Then the System displayed a cheerful pop-up.

> **NEGOTIATION MINI-GAME**  
> Choose one:  
> **A)** Public declaration  
> **B)** Dramatic pose  
> **C)** Accidental innuendo  

Haruto’s soul left his body again.

Seraphina’s eyes narrowed. “Player?”

Luminara leaned closer. “Ara ara~ What will you choose?”

Mira’s grin was feral. “Pick innuendo.”

Velvet’s tone stayed gentle, which meant she was absolutely serious. “Pick declaration. It will establish authority.”

Bone Legion Commander crossed his arms. “Pick pose. A leader must appear decisive.”

Haruto stared at the options like they were a poison selection puzzle.

He picked **Public declaration** because it sounded the least likely to end with him on a poster.

The System chimed approvingly.

> **DECLARATION SELECTED**  
> Speak your intent.  
> **Reminder:** The System records everything.

Haruto swallowed, then stood up straighter. He was still surrounded by knights, gods’ UI, flirty women, and undead professionals, but he could do one thing.

He could choose to not be pushed around.

Haruto raised his voice. “Okay! Here’s my starter party.”

Silence fell.

Even the choir NPCs seemed to hold their breath.

Haruto pointed at Seraphina. “You. Radiant Paladin. I need a shield that can handle drama and monsters.”

Seraphina’s cheeks colored. She tried to look stoic, failed, and compensated by striking a pose so heroic the choir resumed singing in reflex.

Haruto pointed at Luminara. “You. Solar Sorceress. I need damage, and… uh… someone who can roast the dungeon for me.”

Luminara smiled like she’d just won a prize. “Oh? You want me to warm you?”

Haruto’s face went bright red.

The System chimed loudly.

> **ACCIDENTAL INNUENDO DETECTED**  
> +1 Drama  
> +1 Jealousy Aggro

Seraphina’s eye twitched. “Player—”

Haruto pointed quickly at Velvet. “And you. High Priestess Velvet. Because I don’t want to die on day one.”

Velvet’s hand slid away from his chest at last, slow and deliberate, as if she’d been enjoying the pulse check. “A wise choice,” she murmured. “I will keep you… alive.”

Mira’s mouth fell open.

“Excuse me?” she demanded. “What about morale? What about recovery drinks? What about lap pillows?”

Haruto winced. “Mira, I—”

The System chimed.

> **WARNING:** Support character disappointed.  
> **Potential effects:** gossip debuff, price increase, passive-aggressive side quests.

Haruto flailed at the window with both hands. “No! No price increase! That’s pay-to-cry!”

Mira crossed her arms, chest rising with righteous betrayal. “You picked the priestess over the tavern mistress.”

Velvet’s smile remained serene, which was the problem. “He chose survival. As he should.”

Mira leaned closer to Haruto, whispering like a secret. “I can heal too, you know. Emotionally.”

Haruto’s brain attempted a reboot.

Seraphina cleared her throat with the authority of a thousand dramatic heroines. “This is acceptable. The Player has chosen Radiant companions.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned sharply. “Unacceptable. The Veil Syndicate was excluded.”

Chiller Executioner’s axe slid down his shoulder with a slow scrape. “So we’re just… not invited?”

Ectoplasm Elemental’s tendrils drooped. “No hug?”

Haruto raised both hands again. “I’m not excluding anyone. I’m choosing three for the starter party because the System says so. But I can still—”

He looked at the UI, because apparently it was the only authority here besides gods and hormones.

“—I can still recruit later. Right?”

The System blinked.

> **UNIQUE SKILL DETECTED:** Party Leader Authority  
> **Effect:** Cross-faction recruitment allowed.  
> **Warning:** Cross-faction recruitment increases difficulty and social volatility.

“See?” Haruto said, relief flooding him. “I can recruit later.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clacked. “Prove it.”

Seraphina frowned. “You should not make promises to—”

Luminara’s smile turned sharp. “Let him.”

Velvet’s gaze stayed gentle, but her voice had a warning in it. “Choose your words carefully, my light.”

Haruto took a breath.

He turned toward Bone Legion Commander.

“You want equal recruitment rights?” Haruto said. “Fine. I’m making a deal. You help me clear Sky Dungeon #7—”

Seraphina snapped, “He doesn’t need—”

“—and I’ll recruit one Veil member immediately after,” Haruto finished quickly, louder than the paladin’s interruption.

The hall froze again.

Even Mira stopped looking betrayed long enough to be impressed.

Bone Legion Commander stared at Haruto like he was evaluating a new weapon.

“Which Veil member?” the skeleton asked.

Haruto glanced at the Veil trio.

Chiller Executioner stared back, expression hidden under his hood but posture radiating *I could solve problems.*

Ectoplasm Elemental bounced hopefully.

Bone Legion Commander stood like the concept of a war plan given bones.

Haruto swallowed.

He was trying to be strategic.

He was also trying not to die.

He was also, inconveniently, trying not to get stabbed by romance.

Haruto pointed at Bone Legion Commander. “You. You’re in charge, and you seem like you can… negotiate without breaking chandeliers.”

Chiller Executioner’s voice was flat. “I only broke one.”

The System chimed.

> **RECRUITMENT PROMISE REGISTERED**  
> **Contract:** “Recruit Bone Legion Commander after Sky Dungeon #7”  
> **Penalty for failure:** -5 Reputation (Veil), +3 Embarrassment, “Coward” title unlocked.

Haruto’s face paled. “It can give me a title?”

> **TIP:** Titles are forever.

Haruto’s soul curled into a corner.

Seraphina’s expression was a storm trapped behind a smile. “Player… you are making dangerous commitments.”

Luminara’s eyes glittered, delighted. “He’s learning.”

Velvet stepped closer, her voice low enough that it felt like a secret wrapped around him. “If you insist on this, I will ensure you survive it.”

Mira’s hands landed on Haruto’s shoulders from behind like a sudden buff. “And I will ensure you don’t forget me while you’re out there risking your cute little life.”

Haruto squeaked. “Mira—”

“No,” Mira said, cheerful and firm. “You don’t get to pick the priestess and then ignore the tavern mistress. I’m coming too.”

Seraphina spun. “You cannot simply—”

Mira tapped her apron pocket and produced a small glowing bottle like a magician. “Recovery drink.”

She produced a second. “Stamina drink.”

She produced a third. “Courage drink.”

She produced a fourth. “This one is called ‘Don’t Die, Idiot.’”

Haruto stared. “That’s… an item?”

The System chimed, almost proud.

> **ITEM DISCOVERED:** Don’t Die, Idiot  
> **Rarity:** Uncommon  
> **Effect:** Heal 3 HP and remove “panic” (if present)  
> **Side effect:** user blushes uncontrollably for 10 seconds.

Haruto covered his face. “Why is embarrassment always involved?”

Mira winked. “Because it’s fun.”

Bone Legion Commander clacked his jaw once. “The tavern mistress is… a logistical asset.”

Mira beamed. “I love being called an asset!”

Velvet’s smile didn’t change, but the air chilled by a degree. “Do be careful what you call women, Commander.”

Bone Legion Commander paused, then said with stiff dignity, “Noted.”

Haruto looked between all of them, and for the first time since waking up on the marble floor, he felt something other than panic.

It was… ridiculous.

But it was also a team.

Maybe.

Possibly.

If the gods didn’t patch him into dust first.

The System window compressed into a neat summary like it was pleased with itself.

> **STARTER PARTY CONFIRMED:**  
> **Seraphina** (Tank)  
> **Luminara** (Damage)  
> **Velvet** (Healer)  
> **Add-on:** Mirabelle (Morale / Items) — *unofficial, but persistent*  
> **Contract Pending:** Recruit Bone Legion Commander after Sky Dungeon #7  
> **Difficulty:** still +300% (jealousy)

Haruto looked up at the hall, at the knights, at the broken floor that had already healed itself.

“Okay,” he said, voice steadier than he felt. “Where is Sky Dungeon #7?”

Seraphina lifted her chin. “Above.”

Luminara’s flames brightened. “Far too beautiful to be safe.”

Velvet’s gaze softened. “And close enough to kill you if you don’t listen.”

Mira squeezed his shoulders. “Don’t worry. If you die, I’ll scold you so hard you’ll respawn out of spite.”

Chiller Executioner snorted, which might have been a laugh.

Ectoplasm Elemental bounced in a circle. “Adventure time! Hug time later!”

Bone Legion Commander turned sharply toward Haruto. “Then we move.”

He paused, then added—reluctantly, like it physically pained him—

“Player.”

The System chimed one last time, bright as a bell and twice as threatening.

> **QUEST START:** Clear **Sky Dungeon #7**  
> **Timer:** Active  
> **Tip:** Try not to flirt in the trap rooms.  
> **Tip:** You will fail.

Haruto stared at the final line.

“This world,” he said softly, “is my enemy.”

And then he took his first step as the Eternal Player—into a story that absolutely refused to let him survive quietly.
