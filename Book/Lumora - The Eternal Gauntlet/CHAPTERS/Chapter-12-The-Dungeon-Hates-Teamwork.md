## Chapter 12 — The Dungeon Hates Teamwork

The corridor beyond the Twin Lock sloped downward.

Not by much—just enough that Haruto felt the shift in his stomach, that subtle “you are leaving the safe part of the map” sensation games were so good at weaponizing.

The light faded as they descended.

Gold inlays in the walls grew sparse. Violet lines became more frequent. The air cooled, thickening with mist that clung to the floor like it wanted to trip someone for fun.

Sunny floated at Haruto’s shoulder, humming.

Bone Legion Commander walked with the silent confidence of someone who didn’t need to announce he was competent.

Seraphina walked with the loud confidence of someone who absolutely needed to announce it—except she wasn’t announcing right now, which meant she was thinking.

That was… unsettling.

Haruto’s UI chimed softly.

> **NOTICE:** Party Unity buff active (temporary)  
> **NOTICE:** Dungeon is adapting to Unity  
> **Tip:** Your success is a personal insult.

Haruto muttered, “It’s a dungeon. It shouldn’t have feelings.”

Velvet’s voice was calm behind him. “Everything has feelings here.”

Mira whispered, delighted, “Even the walls.”

Luminara’s tone was light. “Especially the walls.”

The corridor opened into a room that looked like it couldn’t decide what it was.

Half the ceiling was open to the sky—bright, sunlit.

The other half was stone, dark and heavy, dripping mist.

The floor was a mosaic of tiles: gold on one side, violet on the other, meeting in a jagged seam like two territories fighting over the same land.

In the center of the room stood a pillar, carved with both Radiant symbols and Veil runes.

And on the pillar floated a window.

Haruto didn’t like how cheerful it looked.

> **ROOM TYPE:** Teamwork Checkpoint  
> **Objective:** Proceed forward  
> **Bonus:** Keep your party together (optional)

Haruto whispered, “Optional?”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Optional means “it hurts more if you fail.”

Seraphina stepped forward. “We move as one.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull tilted. “We move as efficiently as the room allows.”

Seraphina’s jaw tightened. “That means as one.”

Bone Legion Commander replied, flat. “That means as alive.”

Haruto opened his mouth to intervene—

—and the floor clicked.

Not a trap click.

A mechanism click.

The central pillar flared, gold and violet runes lighting simultaneously.

The room’s seam line glowed bright.

Then the wall behind them slid up from the floor with a brutal, clean speed—stone rising like a guillotine.

Haruto stumbled back.

The wall slammed down between the party in a perfect straight line, splitting them into two groups.

Haruto’s eyes widened.

Seraphina was on the left side—Radiant half.

Bone Legion Commander was on the right side—Veil half.

Haruto himself—

Haruto was on the wrong side of both, stuck in the middle zone where the seam zig-zagged and the wall had cut their formation like a knife through cake.

Mira yelped. “Hey! No! I didn’t consent to being separated from the cute one!”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm, but her hand shot out and caught Haruto’s sleeve, pulling him back from the wall’s edge before he could panic-walk into a hazard tile.

Luminara drifted a half-step upward, eyes narrowed. “The room is splitting roles.”

Sunny chimed brightly, like a child announcing a game.

“SURPRISE!” it sang. “You have unlocked Lesson: **Separation Anxiety!**”

Haruto snapped, “Sunny, I swear—”

Sunny chirped, “You can’t hurt me!”

Seraphina slammed her palm against the wall, armor ringing. “Open!”

The wall did not open.

Bone Legion Commander pressed a bony hand against his side of it, testing.

He looked up at Haruto through the seam gap and said, voice clipped, “Leader.”

Haruto swallowed. “Yes.”

“Stop shouting,” Bone Legion Commander ordered. “It will feed the room.”

Seraphina’s voice came sharp from the other side. “Do not give orders to the Player—”

Bone Legion Commander snapped back, “Then you give orders that keep him alive.”

Haruto’s UI flashed.

> **ROOM MECHANIC:** Unity Punishment  
> **Effect:** raised walls + timed spawns when party argues  
> **Tip:** Please argue.

Haruto stared at the last line. “It wants us to argue.”

Luminara’s voice was soft, amused. “It wants entertainment.”

Velvet’s gaze slid to Haruto, calm and focused. “Haruto. Breathe. Lead.”

Mira whispered, “Lead and also look heroic.”

Haruto ignored the heroic part.

He forced air into his lungs and looked around the room.

The wall had created two corridors to the exit.

Left corridor: Radiant tiles, sunlight, a gold door at the far end.

Right corridor: Veil tiles, mist, a violet door at the far end.

Two doors.

Two routes.

One party.

Haruto’s UI chimed again.

> **OBJECTIVE:** Open both doors within 30 seconds  
> **Failure:** Spawn wave + “Unhelpful encouragement”

Sunny chirped, “That’s me!”

Haruto hissed, “Shut up.”

Sunny chirped, “No!”

Seraphina’s voice snapped, “I will open the Radiant door.”

Bone Legion Commander’s voice cut in, crisp. “I will open the Veil door.”

Haruto’s heart steadied.

Okay.

That’s clear.

Haruto raised his voice just enough to carry without becoming drama-fuel.

“We split,” Haruto said. “Seraphina takes left. Commander takes right. Velvet, Mira, Luminara—stay with me and support both sides.”

Seraphina’s voice was strained. “You will remain behind cover.”

Bone Legion Commander said, “You will remain central. Good.”

Luminara floated closer to Haruto’s shoulder. “You’re learning,” she murmured.

Haruto’s face warmed—only a little.

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (minor)  
> **Note:** Compliments are dangerous.

Haruto exhaled. “Of course.”

They moved.

Seraphina sprinted down the Radiant corridor, armor flashing in sunlight, her greatsword drawn.

Bone Legion Commander moved down the Veil corridor with measured steps, cloak trailing, one hand raised as if feeling for invisible strings.

Haruto stayed in the central seam zone with Velvet, Mira, and Luminara.

The moment they began moving in two directions, the room reacted.

Vents opened along the ceiling.

Not the neat corridor vents from earlier.

These were jagged, like the dungeon had gotten lazy and angry.

Haruto’s UI flashed.

> **SPAWN WAVE:** “Encouragement”  
> **Enemies:** Praise Motes x6, Spite Skitters x3  
> **Tip:** You’re doing great! (derogatory)

Mira gasped. “Praise motes?”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm. “They look harmless. They are not.”

Golden motes drifted down like glitter.

Each one carried a faint, chiming sound—like applause.

Haruto felt it brush his skin like a compliment he didn’t want.

And his mood—his actual mood—lifted.

Just a little.

His heart warmed.

The room brightened.

The System chimed immediately.

> **ROOM REACTION:** “Confidence spike” detected  
> **Result:** additional spawns unlocked

Haruto’s mouth dropped open. “It punished confidence?”

Sunny chirped, “Stay humble!”

Haruto snapped, “You’re literally the problem!”

Sunny chirped, “I’m literally helpful!”

Luminara flicked a finger.

A thin lash of heat snapped through the motes, scattering them into harmless sparks.

But for every mote she destroyed, two more drifted down.

Velvet lifted her hand, and a cool wave rolled out—dampening the motes’ glow, turning applause into quiet.

Mira pulled out her skillet again like it was her emotional support weapon and smacked a Spite Skitter off the wall.

*CLANG.*

Haruto flinched.

The room pulsed violet.

> **ROOM REACTION:** “Loud noise” detected  
> **Result:** Spite Skitters empowered

Mira stared at the wall. “Oh come on.”

Velvet’s gaze narrowed. “Quiet.”

Mira mouthed, silently, “Sorry.”

Haruto’s eyes flicked left, toward Seraphina.

He couldn’t see her corridor clearly through the wall.

But he could hear the ring of her sword and the sharp breathing of effort.

He flicked right, toward the commander.

He saw violet mist swirl.

He heard the clack of bone on stone and a low, cold murmur that sounded like a command given to the dungeon itself.

Haruto’s UI popped a mini-map overlay.

Two timers appeared.

Radiant Door: 15 seconds remaining.

Veil Door: 15 seconds remaining.

Haruto swallowed.

“Velvet,” he said quickly. “Keep us calm. Damp the praise. Mira—no cookware noises. Luminara—control the spawns, don’t chase kills.”

Mira whispered, scandalized, “How do I fight without noise?”

Haruto hissed, “Softly!”

Mira lifted her skillet and… gently bonked a skitter.

The skitter stared at her like it was offended.

Mira bonked it again.

It dissolved.

Mira’s eyes widened. “Oh my god. Soft violence works.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Yes. That is called “discipline.”

Haruto couldn’t help it.

He laughed—just a little.

The room pulsed gold.

> **ROOM REACTION:** “Joy” detected  
> **Result:** Praise Motes multiply

Haruto immediately stopped laughing.

Velvet’s hand pressed briefly to his shoulder—life-or-death touch. “Don’t,” she whispered.

Haruto nodded, face stiff. “I’m not enjoying anything. I’m miserable.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Better.

A scream echoed from the Radiant corridor.

Seraphina’s voice—furious, strained—shouted, “It’s locked by sentiment!”

Haruto’s head snapped left. “Sentiment?”

Sunny chirped brightly. “The Radiant door opens with a heartfelt declaration!”

Seraphina’s voice roared, “I ALREADY DECLARED—”

The room pulsed violet.

Spite Skitters surged.

Haruto shouted, “SERAPHINA! LESS DRAMA!”

Seraphina snapped back, “I AM NOT—”

Haruto cut her off. “Say something short and sincere!”

Silence for half a second.

Then Seraphina’s voice came quieter—almost raw.

“I… will not let him die.”

The Radiant door’s timer blinked.

Then dropped from 10 seconds to unlocked.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **RADIANT DOOR:** Opened  
> **Cause:** “sincere vow”

Haruto exhaled hard, relief slicing through him.

Then the Veil corridor rumbled.

Bone Legion Commander’s voice came through, clipped.

“Veil door requires a counterphrase,” he said. “Player.”

Haruto’s stomach tightened. “Counterphrase?”

Sunny chimed, thrilled. “It opens with a distrustful statement! The Veil likes honesty!”

Velvet’s voice was calm. “Haruto. Speak.”

Haruto swallowed.

What would a Veil door want?

Something edgy.

Something suspicious.

Something that admitted fear without dressing it up.

Haruto raised his voice toward the commander’s corridor.

“I don’t trust this dungeon,” Haruto said clearly. “And I don’t trust the gods. But I trust my party to keep me alive.”

For a heartbeat, the room went still.

As if the dungeon itself had paused to listen.

Then the Veil door clicked.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **VEIL DOOR:** Opened  
> **Cause:** “honest distrust”

Sunny chirped, delighted. “Aww! He trusts you!”

Bone Legion Commander’s voice came flat. “Do not narrate my emotions.”

Sunny chirped, “You have emotions!”

Bone Legion Commander said, cold, “I have objectives.”

The central wall shuddered.

Then, slowly, it sank back into the floor, undoing the split it had created.

Seraphina emerged from the Radiant corridor, breathing hard, cheeks faintly flushed—not from romance, from effort.

Bone Legion Commander stepped out from the Veil corridor, perfectly composed as if he hadn’t been fighting while mist tried to insult him.

The Praise Motes dimmed and drifted away, disappointed.

The Spite Skitters dissolved, bored.

The room’s pillar flashed a final message.

> **BONUS ACHIEVED:** Party stayed functional  
> **REWARD:** minimal humiliation

Haruto slumped. “Minimal humiliation is my favorite reward.”

Velvet’s mouth curved. “You did well.”

Haruto forced his face not to react.

Luminara’s smile returned. “You’re learning to speak the dungeon’s language.”

Seraphina’s gaze flicked to Haruto, then away. “Your call was… correct.”

Haruto’s eyebrows lifted. Praise from Seraphina was rarer than loot.

Bone Legion Commander’s skull angled toward Haruto. “Acceptable leadership.”

Sunny chirped, “He’s so proud of you!”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked. “Sunny.”

Sunny chirped, “Yes?”

Bone Legion Commander said, “Be silent.”

Sunny chirped, “No!”

Haruto laughed—just one breath—

—then stopped, because the room pulsed faintly in response.

The dungeon was still listening.

And somewhere deeper ahead, beyond the newly opened path, Haruto felt a heavier presence waiting.

Not a mini-boss.

Something that didn’t care about their jokes.

Something that would.

And—strangely—beneath the mist and stone and metal, he caught a second scent that didn’t belong in Sky Dungeon #7 at all.

Clean sap.

Crushed leaves after rain.

It vanished as quickly as it came, swallowed by cold air and dungeon breath, but it left his skin prickling like he’d been looked at.

“Okay,” Haruto said, forcing steadiness into his voice. “We move. Before it decides to test our feelings again.”
