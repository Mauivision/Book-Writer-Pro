## Chapter 30 — World Boss Phase 1 (Disunity Punished)

The raid gate didn't open.

It *fell*.

One moment the square was stone and panic; the next, the center dropped away like a trapdoor designed by gods who considered physics a suggestion. Marble folded inward. Fog rushed to fill the gap. And the party—Haruto's impossible mixed roster—was pulled down into an arena that looked less like a battlefield and more like a courtroom built for giants.

> **RAID ZONE:** Correction Warden — Phase 1  
> **Rule:** disunity is damage  
> **Tip:** argue responsibly (you won't)

Haruto landed in a crouch, knees aching, Sunny's crate clutched to his chest like a shield made of bad decisions.

Above them, the Warden descended.

It wasn't a dragon.

It wasn't a lich.

It was worse, in the specific way Lumora was always worse: it was a *mechanic* given flesh.

The Correction Warden stood three stories tall, armor plate layered like patch notes, face obscured by a visor that displayed scrolling text—JEALOUSY AGGRO, FACTION LOCK, ROLLBACK PENDING. Its hands were shaped like gavel heads. When it moved, the air made the sound of a UI window snapping shut.

Seraphina swore, low and Radiant. "By the—"

"Don't declare," Haruto snapped. "Phase one punishes disunity. Read the room."

The room helpfully labeled itself:

> **BOSS MECHANIC:** Fracture Mirror  
> **Effect:** verbal conflict converts to party-wide damage  
> **Mitigation:** synchronized action OR morale tanking

Morale tanking.

Haruto's stomach dropped. "It wants me to eat the feelings again."

Velvet's hand found his shoulder—brief, grounding. "Not alone."

The Warden raised one gavel-hand and the arena responded.

Mirrors slid up from the floor—doz dozen panels angled inward, each reflecting the party but wrong: Seraphina's reflection smirked at Bone Legion Commander's; Luminara's reflection leaned toward an empty space labeled PLAYER SLOT OPEN; Velvet's reflection whispered to a reflection of Haruto that looked relieved to be chosen.

Haruto's HUD screamed:

> **DISUNITY SPIKE:** incoming  
> **Source:** reflected rivalry prompts

"Look away from the mirrors," Elara shouted. "They're bait—"

Seraphina saw her reflection declare something pious and perfect and snapped, "I would *never*—"

The Warden struck the ground.

Damage wasn't physical.

It was *social* made kinetic—a shockwave of embarrassment and anger that hit Haruto like a gut punch. His health bar dipped. So did everyone else's.

> **PARTY DAMAGE:** 12% (argument-triggered)  
> **Note:** "Denial" counts as argument.

Haruto wheezed. "Okay. No denials. No declarations. No—Mira, what are you doing?"

Mira had already opened her vendor tray and was handing out potions labeled LIQUID COURAGE and STOP BEING WEIRD.

"Morale support," Mira said cheerfully.

Chiller drank one and belched violet flame. "Morale improved."

> **PARTY DAMAGE:** 4% (collateral weirdness)

Haruto put his face in his hands.

Bone Legion Commander stepped forward, voice flat. "We require tactics, not beverages."

"Agreed," Haruto said. "Commander—your left. Seraphina—right. Not because factions, because geometry. Luminara, heat the air when the mirrors frost. Velvet, triage on call. Ecto—"

"Hugs?" Ecto asked hopefully.

"Not yet," Haruto said. "Elara, tell me when it's lying."

Elara's eyes tracked the Warden's visor text. "It is always lying."

"Helpful."

The Warden swung.

This time the attack was direct—gavel arc, physical, crushing. Seraphina's shield met it with a sound like a cathedral door slamming. Bone Legion Commander's shadow tethers lashed to her boots, anchoring, distributing weight across the party's shared stance.

> **SYNCHRONIZED BLOCK:** success  
> **Fracture Mirror:** dampened -1

Haruto felt the difference immediately—the social shockwave weaker, the mirrors flickering.

"That's it," he said, breathless. "We don't fight each other. We fight *for* each other. Even when it's annoying."

Luminara's smile was fierce. "Annoying is our specialty."

The Warden's visor scrolled faster.

> **ADAPTATION:** party cohesion rising  
> **Countermeasure:** Jealousy Lances

The mirrors erupted.

Not reflections anymore—projectiles, shards of gold and violet light aimed at the party's weakest bonds. One lance sought the space between Haruto and Velvet. Another hunted Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander like it wanted to monetize their argument forever.

Haruto moved on instinct.

Party Leader Authority flared—warm, sharp, unfair—and he stepped into the lance path between Seraphina and the Commander, arms spread like he could block narrative with posture.

The lance hit him.

It hurt worse than the gavel.

Not flesh—*shame*, hot and specific: the fear of choosing wrong, of being chosen, of being everyone's symbol and nobody's person.

Haruto gritted his teeth.

> **MECHANIC:** Morale Tank active  
> **Damage redirected:** 68%  
> **Cost:** Haruto stability -2

Velvet swore—actual swearing, quiet and Radiant-forbidden—and healed him before the bar could dip into danger.

"Haruto," Velvet said, voice tight. "You cannot absorb everything."

"Watch me," Haruto gasped.

Sunny chirped from the crate, strained: "You're doing great! Don't stop! Definitely don't stop!"

The chirp glitched at the end.

Elara sliced a mirror shard midair, cyan edge flashing once before fading. "Phase threshold approaching. It will escalate when we succeed."

"Of course it will," Haruto said. "Success is a trigger."

Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander exchanged a look—not rivalry, calculation.

"Combined strike," the Commander said.

Seraphina nodded. "On your mark, Player."

Haruto's mark was always now.

They moved—shield bash and shadow lance in the same breath, Radiant gold and Veil violet crossing without colliding. The Warden staggered. Visor text stuttered.

> **PHASE 1 PROGRESS:** 100%  
> **Unity threshold met.**  
> **Phase 2 unlocking:** Declaration Duel

The arena shuddered.

Mirrors sank.

The Warden straightened, gavel hands folding together like a judge preparing for sentencing.

And the air changed—lighter, hotter, charged with the specific drama Lumora loved to weaponize.

> **PHASE TRANSITION**  
> **New rule:** shields require Light + Veil sequence  
> **Warning:** posturing will be monetized

Seraphina's eye twitched.

Bone Legion Commander's jaw clicked.

Haruto exhaled.

"Great," he said. "Act two. Try not to marry each other on accident."

Seraphina sputtered.

The Commander said, deadpan, "No promises."

The Warden raised both gavels.

Phase two began.
