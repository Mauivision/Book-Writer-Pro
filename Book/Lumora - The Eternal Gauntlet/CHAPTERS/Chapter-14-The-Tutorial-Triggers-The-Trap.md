## Chapter 14 — The Tutorial Triggers the Trap

The door at the end of the Rivalry Trial opened with the reluctant click of a dungeon that had just lost an argument.

Haruto stepped through first—because he’d learned a rule about Sky Dungeon #7:

If you hesitated, it made fun of you.

If you acted confidently, it punished you for being “main character.”

So the only winning move was to keep moving and pretend you weren’t aware you were being psychologically bullied by architecture.

The corridor beyond was quieter.

No bright runes.

No glittering “WELCOME, BEGINNER!” signs.

Just stone, shadow, and a faint hum in the floor that felt less like a heartbeat and more like a countdown.

Sunny floated ahead, chirping to itself like it had accomplished something.

“Great job, party!” Sunny sang. “You navigated rivalry, teamwork, and emotional maturity!”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned. “Stop praising them. Praise is a spawn trigger.”

Sunny chirped, “Praise is my love language!”

Seraphina muttered, “Of course it is.”

Mira whispered to Haruto, delighted, “The skeleton hates the fairy.”

Velvet murmured, “He hates the noise.”

Luminara’s smile was soft. “He hates being observed.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked. “I do not hate being observed.”

Everyone looked at him.

Bone Legion Commander paused, then said stiffly, “I hate being narrated.”

Sunny chirped, “Narration is my job!”

Haruto whispered, “We are going to die because of customer service.”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Correct.

They reached a junction where the corridor forked into three paths.

Left: bright, warm light leaking from around the corner.

Right: cool mist, violet glow, the faint sound of dripping water that was probably not water.

Center: a plain passage with no obvious aesthetic, which in Lumora meant it was hiding something.

Sunny stopped in the middle and bobbed like a tour guide about to ruin everyone’s day.

“Ooh!” Sunny chirped. “Choose your route! Left is safe. Right is edgy. Center is… mysterious!”

Haruto stared at Sunny. “Why do I feel like you’re lying?”

Sunny chirped, “I’m helpful!”

Bone Legion Commander stepped forward. “We take center.”

Seraphina snapped, “No. We take left. The Radiant path is safer.”

Luminara murmured, “Right is probably fun.”

Mira raised her hand. “As tavern mistress, I vote wherever the loot is.”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm. “We take the path that advances the objective. Sunny—where does the dungeon want us to go?”

Sunny bobbed, delighted. “Great question!”

The orb rotated slowly toward the center path.

“The dungeon wants you to go there,” Sunny said.

Haruto’s stomach dropped. “Then we should go somewhere else.”

Sunny chirped, “You can’t outsmart a dungeon.”

Haruto said, flat, “Watch me.”

He pointed to the left path. “We go Radiant.”

Seraphina’s shoulders relaxed slightly, relief and pride mixing. “Wise.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull angled toward Haruto. “You are choosing symbolism over information.”

Haruto met his stare. “I’m choosing the option the dungeon didn’t point at.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked. “Acceptable reasoning.”

Sunny chirped, offended. “Hey!”

Haruto ignored it and started down the left path.

Warm light spilled across the corridor, soft and golden. The walls brightened, runes returning—calmer, less jagged.

For a moment, the dungeon almost felt like it was pretending to be kind again.

Haruto’s shoulders loosened.

That was his second mistake.

The floor tile under his boot sank with a soft *click.*

Haruto froze.

Seraphina’s voice snapped instantly. “Don’t move.”

Haruto didn’t.

Sunny chirped, delighted. “Ooh! Trap!”

Haruto whispered, “Sunny.”

Sunny chirped, “Yes?”

Haruto said, very carefully, “If we die, I will haunt you.”

Sunny chirped, “You can’t! You’ll respawn!”

The System chimed.

> **TRAP DETECTED:** Moral Lecture  
> **Effect:** immobilize + forced dialogue  
> **Tip:** You deserve this.

Haruto stared at the air. “Forced dialogue?”

The corridor brightened.

A window the size of a banner unfolded in front of Haruto’s face like a stage curtain.

> **TUTORIAL POP QUIZ — HEROISM**  
> Choose the correct statement:  
> **A)** “I will protect everyone.”  
> **B)** “I will protect the Player.”  
> **C)** “I will protect myself.”  
> **D)** “I will protect my tavern’s profits.”

Mira whispered, “D.”

Seraphina snapped, “A.”

Bone Legion Commander said, cold, “B.”

Luminara murmured, “C is honest.”

Velvet’s voice was gentle. “Haruto. Answer quickly. This trap is not just social.”

Haruto’s UI flashed a timer.

> **TIME REMAINING:** 5… 4…

Haruto’s heart spiked.

He stared at the choices.

If he picked A, it sounded noble—and like a lie, because he didn’t even know these people.

If he picked B, it sounded selfish—and correct, because that was the party’s actual role right now: protect the Player.

If he picked C, it sounded honest—and would absolutely be punished.

If he picked D, Mira would laugh and the dungeon would probably explode.

The timer ticked.

> **TIME REMAINING:** 3… 2…

Haruto chose B.

The banner window chimed.

> **CORRECT!**  
> **Bonus:** “Responsible Leader” +1  
> **Penalty:** “Guilt” +1

Haruto’s face fell. “Guilt?”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Guilt is also an emotion.

The trap released his foot.

Haruto stepped back quickly, but the corridor had already changed.

The warm golden runes dimmed.

The walls shivered like they were disappointed.

And from the ceiling vents, something dropped.

Not skitters.

Not husks.

Something round and floating.

Something shaped like a heart.

Something that made Haruto’s stomach drop on sight.

> **Heart Hazard** — Lv. 3  
> **Trait:** amplifies “guilt” into damage

Mira whispered, impressed, “That’s so mean.”

Velvet’s voice sharpened slightly. “Haruto, do not spiral.”

Haruto didn’t know how to not spiral when the enemy’s trait was literally “makes you feel worse.”

Sunny chirped, delighted. “Wow! An emotional hazard! Those are my favorite!”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned. “Be silent.”

Sunny chirped, “No!”

The Heart Hazard floated forward, pulsing softly.

Each pulse made Haruto’s chest tighten.

A memory flickered behind his eyes—his phone notification, Kenji’s message, his life back home.

*bro you alive*

Haruto swallowed hard.

His HP bar flickered, dropping a fraction as guilt translated into damage.

Seraphina stepped in front of him instantly, sword raised. “I will cut it.”

She swung.

The blade passed through the Heart Hazard like it was fog.

Seraphina’s eyes widened. “It’s intangible.”

Bone Legion Commander lifted his hand, violet mist curling.

Shadow wrapped around the Heart Hazard like a net.

For a heartbeat, it held—

Then the hazard pulsed and the mist shivered away, as if guilt was slicker than shadow.

Luminara’s flame hair dimmed, her expression turning serious. “It’s not a physical target.”

Velvet’s gaze snapped to Haruto. “Haruto. Look at me.”

Haruto did.

Velvet stepped close—close enough to feel like comfort, not like flirting.

Her hand hovered, then pressed gently to the Radiant cuff on his wrist.

Grounding.

“You answered correctly,” Velvet said softly. “Protecting the Player protects everyone. Without you, the party collapses. Without the party, the world resets. Do you understand?”

Haruto’s throat tightened. He nodded.

The guilt in his chest eased.

The Heart Hazard dimmed slightly, as if confused.

The System chimed.

> **MECHANIC COUNTERED:** Guilt reduced by “reframing”

Mira whispered, awed, “She healed his emotions.”

Velvet’s smile stayed calm. “It is medically indicated.”

Seraphina’s cheeks flushed with something complicated.

Luminara’s eyes gleamed.

Bone Legion Commander said, flat, “Effective.”

Sunny chirped, “Aww! Emotional intimacy!”

Haruto shouted, “Sunny!”

The Heart Hazard pulsed again, trying to feed.

But now Haruto didn’t panic.

He did the thing he was learning to do:

He treated it like a mechanic.

“Commander,” Haruto said quickly, “can you anchor it to something real? A shadow, a surface, anything?”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull angled, thinking fast. “Yes.”

He stepped to the side, lifted his hand, and pressed his palm to the stone wall.

Violet lines flared.

A shadow peeled off the wall like a sheet and stretched across the floor, sliding under the Heart Hazard.

The hazard’s pulsing heart-shape wobbled as the shadow grabbed it from below—forcing it to “exist” in relation to something.

“Now,” Bone Legion Commander said.

Seraphina didn’t hesitate.

She drove her blade straight down into the shadow anchor point.

Light met shadow.

The Heart Hazard shrieked—not in sound, but in UI, flashing a warning so bright it hurt.

> **ERROR:** Emotional hazard cannot process “teamwork”

The hazard flickered, then shattered into harmless sparkles that fell like confetti.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **COMBO:** Shadow Anchor + Radiant Strike  
> **Result:** Hazard deleted  
> **Note:** The gods are taking notes.

Haruto exhaled shakily.

Seraphina turned her head away quickly, as if refusing to be seen feeling relief.

Bone Legion Commander looked at his hand, then at Seraphina, then away, as if refusing to be seen acknowledging her.

Mira whispered, delighted, “They’re bonding.”

Seraphina snapped, “We are not.”

Bone Legion Commander said, “We are cooperating.”

Luminara smiled. “Same thing in this dungeon.”

Sunny chirped, “Same thing in this story!”

Haruto glared at Sunny. “You triggered the trap.”

Sunny chirped, innocent. “I didn’t! The floor did!”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** The tutorial triggered the trap.  
> **TIP:** This is funny.

Haruto pointed at Sunny. “You. You’re going on mute.”

Sunny chirped, delighted. “You can’t!”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned slowly toward the orb.

“Then we will,” he said, “carry it in a box.”

Sunny chirped. “What?”

Mira’s eyes lit up. “Oh my god. We can put Sunny in a jar.”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm. “A box is fine.”

Luminara’s smile turned wicked. “A box with a small air hole.”

Sunny chirped, suddenly less cheerful. “Wait—”

Haruto sighed, rubbing his face. “Later. We keep moving.”

The corridor ahead opened into another chamber, and the warm light vanished again.

The dungeon’s brightness dropped, shadows thickening.

Haruto felt it—an awareness, heavier than before.

Something deeper was tracking them.

Not just reacting to their emotions.

Evaluating their patterns.

And under the stone-and-mist scent, that clean note returned—sap and leaf, out of place and sharp as a blade edge.

Haruto’s gaze flicked to the wall instinctively.

For a moment he thought he saw it: a thin, pale scratch through the dust like a directional mark—too clean to be a crack, too deliberate to be random.

It was gone when he looked again, swallowed by the dungeon’s shifting light.

Haruto’s UI flickered once, and a line appeared in the corner of his vision.

> **HIDDEN STAT UPDATED:** Unity +1  
> **NOTICE:** A watcher has noticed.

Haruto swallowed.

“Okay,” he whispered to himself. “So now the dungeon hates us *and* something is watching.”

Sunny chirped faintly, as if trying to regain control of the mood. “Good job!”

Bone Legion Commander said, “Box.”
