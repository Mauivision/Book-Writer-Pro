## Chapter 10 — The Commander Collects

The three names hovered in the air like buttons you could ruin your life with.

Haruto stared at them until his eyes started to sting.

**Bone Legion Commander.**  
**Chiller Executioner.**  
**Ectoplasm Elemental.**

Sunny bobbed inches from his face, vibrating with joy. “Pick! Pick! Pick!”

Haruto didn’t look away from the hovering options. “If I pick wrong, does the dungeon kill me?”

The System chimed.

> **ANSWER:** Eventually.

Haruto nodded slowly. “Helpful.”

Seraphina stood rigid at his left shoulder, arms tense, as if she were physically holding herself back from cutting the floating names in half.

“You will choose the correct option,” Seraphina said, voice tight. “The option that does not invite undead into our sacred—”

“He already did,” Luminara murmured, smiling as if she were watching a play she’d seen before and still enjoyed.

Seraphina’s nostrils flared.

Mira leaned over Haruto’s other shoulder, eyes shining with chaos. “Pick the edgy axe guy,” she whispered. “He’s hot in a ‘might destroy furniture’ way.”

“Mirabelle,” Velvet warned softly.

Mira grinned. “What? It’s true.”

Velvet’s gaze returned to Haruto, calm and heavy with meaning. “You promised the commander,” she reminded him gently. “And the System recorded it.”

Haruto swallowed.

He could still feel the Veil curse-key’s faint hum in his inventory, like it wanted him to remember who helped him get it.

He could still see the bone-script message in his HUD:

*You owe me.*

And he could still feel the dungeon itself listening, waiting to punish indecision.

Haruto lifted his chin and reached toward the first option.

**Bone Legion Commander.**

His finger hadn’t even touched the text when Seraphina’s voice snapped like a whip.

“Player—”

Haruto didn’t flinch. He didn’t look away.

“We need him,” Haruto said, voice steady. “Not because I like it. Because the dungeon is built to force balance. We can either do it on our terms… or the dungeon will do it to us.”

Seraphina stared at him.

For a heartbeat, her expression looked like pride fighting panic.

Then she tightened her jaw and said, very quietly, “If you must.”

Haruto tapped the option.

The hovering names vanished.

The parchment in the air flashed once—half gold, half violet—and then dissolved into particles that sank into the stone floor like ink absorbed into paper.

The arena went still.

Even Sunny stopped bobbing for a moment, as if it sensed that something serious was about to happen.

The System chimed.

> **RECRUITMENT CONFIRMED:** Bone Legion Commander  
> **Status:** Pending arrival  
> **Tip:** Don’t insult him.  
> **Tip:** He will insult you first.

Haruto whispered, “Great.”

The air in the chamber chilled.

Not like a breeze.

Like a boundary.

The golden runes along the floor dimmed slightly, and the violet lines in the stone brightened as if someone had turned on a neon sign in a crypt.

Velvet’s posture changed—subtle, but real. She shifted her feet like a healer preparing to stand between two people who wanted to harm each other.

Luminara’s smile thinned, her attention sharpening.

Seraphina’s hand slid to her sword hilt.

Mira… Mira leaned forward like she’d paid for front-row seats.

And then the shadows near the far pillar moved.

Not like fog.

Not like a monster.

Like a person stepping out of a doorway that hadn’t been there a second ago.

Bone Legion Commander emerged with the calm confidence of a man arriving exactly on time to collect a debt.

He was taller than Haruto remembered from the Grand Radiant Hall—taller than seemed fair for a skeleton in a mantle, taller than seemed reasonable for a being with no muscles.

His bones were polished and carved with thin black engravings, like armor etched directly into his body. A dark cloak draped over his shoulders, clasped with a sigil shaped like a broken moon.

He carried no weapon in his hands.

Which meant he was either unarmed…

…or so dangerous he didn’t need one.

He stopped a few steps away, eye sockets fixed on Haruto.

Then, with a precise clack of his jaw, he bowed.

It was not a humble bow.

It was the kind of bow a noble gave another noble when both knew it was performance.

“Eternal Player,” he said, voice smooth and hollow at the same time. “You chose correctly.”

Seraphina’s sword half-drew with a hiss of metal.

“You do not get to judge his choices,” Seraphina snapped.

Bone Legion Commander didn’t even turn his skull toward her. He spoke to Haruto as if Seraphina were a weather effect.

“You acquired the Veil curse-key,” he continued. “You opened the Radiant gate. You cleared the prototype guardian.”

Haruto’s stomach dropped. “You were watching?”

Bone Legion Commander finally angled his skull toward Haruto, and somehow the absence of eyes still felt like a stare.

“Yes,” he said simply. “I watch my investments.”

Haruto flinched at the word.

Mira whispered, delighted, “Oh my god.”

Velvet’s smile stayed serene, but her voice was mild in the way a knife was mild. “Commander, do be careful how you speak in Radiant territory.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked once. “This is not Radiant territory. It is a dungeon. It belongs to the gods.”

Luminara’s eyes glittered. “He’s not wrong.”

Seraphina’s cheeks flushed with anger. “You—”

Haruto raised a hand.

Not dramatic.

Not heroic.

Just… leader.

“Stop,” Haruto said, voice quiet but firm.

Seraphina froze.

Mira blinked.

Luminara tilted her head, impressed.

Velvet’s gaze softened a fraction.

Bone Legion Commander’s skull angled, as if reassessing Haruto’s “stats.”

Haruto met the commander’s empty stare and forced himself not to shrink.

“You said I owe you,” Haruto said. “I chose you. That’s the recruitment. Are we done?”

Bone Legion Commander clacked his jaw, which might have been a laugh in his language.

“No,” he said.

Haruto’s heart sank.

“Of course not,” Haruto muttered.

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Of course not.

Bone Legion Commander stepped closer—just one step, but the chamber felt smaller for it.

“Recruitment is not ownership,” he said. “It is alignment. I am not here to be a pet. I am here to ensure you survive long enough to be useful.”

Seraphina snarled, “He is not a tool.”

Bone Legion Commander turned his skull slowly toward her. “Neither are you.”

Seraphina stiffened.

Luminara murmured, amused, “Oho.”

Bone Legion Commander continued, voice coldly professional. “Your party is unbalanced.”

Mira gasped. “Excuse me? I am perfectly balanced.”

Velvet’s gaze flicked to Mira, warning.

Mira smiled innocently and stepped back half a pace.

Bone Legion Commander ignored the comment. “You have Light. You have healing. You have damage. You have… morale.”

His skull tilted toward Mira, as if acknowledging her existence like a tax.

Mira beamed. “That’s me.”

“You do not have Shadow,” Bone Legion Commander said. “Not truly.”

Haruto’s fingers tightened unconsciously.

He remembered the Veil puzzle room. The chill. The “stylish gloom” status.

He’d touched the Moon Plate, yes.

But it wasn’t the same as having a Veil ally.

Bone Legion Commander lifted one bony hand and tapped his ribcage once, a hollow sound.

“Shadow is not evil,” he said. “It is a tool. A counterbalance. A method. Your world’s Light fears it because Light prefers to pretend it is complete.”

Seraphina’s voice came out strained. “We do not fear it.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked. “Then you won’t mind working with me.”

Seraphina’s cheeks turned pink.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (minor)  
> **Cause:** “rival authority figures”

Haruto whispered, “The dungeon ships my party.”

Sunny chirped, “I ship it too!”

Haruto glared at Sunny. “No one asked you.”

Sunny chirped, “Too bad!”

Velvet stepped forward, posture elegant and calm, and for a moment the tension shifted away from blades and toward diplomacy.

“Commander,” Velvet said softly, “if you are now part of this party, then you will follow the party’s rules.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned to her. “State them.”

Velvet’s smile warmed, which meant danger. “First: we protect the Player. Second: we do not sabotage allies. Third: we do not escalate conflict inside trap rooms.”

Mira raised a hand. “Fourth: we do not insult the tavern mistress.”

Seraphina snapped, “That is not—”

Haruto added quickly, “Fourth: no unnecessary property damage.”

Somewhere in the dungeon, Chiller Executioner sneezed.

Bone Legion Commander paused, as if processing this list like a document.

Then he nodded once.

“Agreed,” he said. “With one addition.”

Haruto’s stomach tightened. “What addition?”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull tilted toward Haruto, and his voice lowered slightly.

“When I give an order during combat,” he said, “you follow it. If you hesitate, you die. If you die, I waste time. I do not waste time.”

Seraphina’s sword shifted a fraction. “He does not take orders from you.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked, amused. “Then he dies. Your choice.”

Haruto swallowed.

He felt the weight of leadership again—this wasn’t about romance or comedy or even faction politics.

This was about competence.

About survival.

Haruto took a breath.

Then he nodded once.

“During combat,” Haruto said, “if you call something and it makes sense, I follow. Outside combat, I lead.”

The arena went quiet.

Even Sunny stopped chiming.

Bone Legion Commander stared at Haruto for a long moment.

Then, slowly, he bowed again.

This bow was different.

Still proud.

But… accepting.

“Acceptable,” he said.

Haruto’s lungs released a breath he hadn’t realized he’d trapped.

The System chimed.

> **PARTY UPDATE:** Bone Legion Commander added  
> **Synergy:** Light + Shadow enabled  
> **Warning:** Drama output increased

Mira clapped softly. “We have a skeleton now.”

Seraphina’s glare cut sideways. “We had skeletons in the Hall.”

Mira corrected cheerfully, “We have a *boss* skeleton now.”

Velvet’s voice stayed mild. “We have an ally.”

Luminara’s smile returned. “We have entertainment.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned to Sunny. “And what is that.”

Sunny chirped, “I am Sunny! Helpful tutorial companion!”

Bone Legion Commander stared.

Then he said, flatly, “I will destroy it.”

Sunny chirped, “You can’t!”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked. “We will see.”

Haruto startled into a laugh—one sharp, disbelieving sound.

It felt good.

It also felt like bait.

The dungeon’s runes pulsed faintly, listening.

The System chimed.

> **NOTICE:** Party composition changed  
> **NOTICE:** Dungeon recalculating…  
> **Tip:** It’s about to get worse.

Haruto looked at the open path ahead—deeper into Sky Dungeon #7, beyond the arena, where corridors waited like teeth.

He tightened his grip on the Split Sigil charm in his inventory, felt the cuff on his wrist, and accepted—just for now—that this world would keep trying to break him.

But he had a party.

A ridiculous, dangerous, dramatic party.

And now he had Shadow at his side.

Haruto exhaled.

“Okay,” he said. “We move.”
