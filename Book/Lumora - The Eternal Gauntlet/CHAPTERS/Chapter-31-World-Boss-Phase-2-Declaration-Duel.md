## Chapter 31 — World Boss Phase 2 (Declaration Duel)

Phase two looked like a wedding and a duel had filed a joint quest.

The arena's floor split into two strips—gold and violet—running parallel toward the Correction Warden like train tracks designed by a romantic comedy writer who hated everyone.

> **PHASE 2:** Declaration Duel  
> **Requirement:** alternating Light + Veil sequence breaks boss shields  
> **Failure:** "cowardice" + "soft-hearted" debuffs (stacking)

Haruto stared at the UI and felt his soul leave his body briefly.

"You have got to be kidding."

Sunny chirped, weakly enthusiastic: "Dramatic declarations increase critical chance!"

The Warden's shields layered around its core—three concentric rings, each labeled with a faction keyword: SANCTIFIED, UNAUTHORIZED, CORRECTION PENDING.

Luminara read over Haruto's shoulder. "It wants synchronized posturing."

"Synchronized," Seraphina repeated, voice dangerous.

Bone Legion Commander inclined his skull. "We have argued professionally before."

"That is not the same thing," Seraphina said.

> **RIVALRY METER:** 3/5  
> **Note:** proximity increases spawn—wait, wrong raid—proximity increases *combo window.*

Haruto stepped between them—not blocking, directing. "Listen. Phase one punished fighting. Phase two wants you to fight *productively.* Like the rivalry board in Sky Dungeon. Light declares. Shadow insults. But you're on the same team."

Seraphina's cheeks flushed. "I do not insult allies."

Bone Legion Commander said, flat, "You insult me constantly."

"I *critique!*"

"Insult," the Commander corrected.

The Warden struck the gold strip.

A wave of sanctified force rolled toward them—beautiful, blinding, absolutely going to hurt.

> **SHIELD LAYER 1:** Sanctified (Radiant break required)

Seraphina moved before Haruto finished reading.

She planted her feet on the gold strip, raised her sword, and declared—voice ringing like a bell forged for exactly this kind of embarrassment:

"By the Radiant Dominion, I stand between harm and the innocent! I stand for order, mercy, and the shield that does not break!"

The wave hit her shield and fractured—gold splintering into harmless light.

> **SEQUENCE:** Light ✓  
> **Next:** Veil response required within 5 seconds

Bone Legion Commander stepped onto the violet strip.

He opened his mouth.

Paused.

Seraphina whispered, furious, "Insult the strategy, not me."

The Commander's skull turned slightly toward her.

"...Your declaration lacks tactical brevity," he said.

Seraphina choked.

Haruto's UI chimed:

> **SEQUENCE:** Veil ✓  
> **Shield Layer 1:** broken  
> **Combo bonus:** synchronized contempt (+damage)

Synchronized contempt.

Haruto filed that under phrases he never wanted to explain to anyone from Earth.

The Warden recoiled—shields flickering, visor scrolling ERROR.

Mira whooped from the rear line. "They're flirting!"

"We are not," Seraphina and the Commander said together.

> **RIVALRY METER:** 4/5  
> **Warning:** meter max triggers bonus mechanic

"Stop syncing accidentally," Haruto begged.

Layer two activated.

> **SHIELD LAYER 2:** Unauthorized (requires dual-strip overlap)

The gold and violet strips slid together into a shared center lane—narrow, exposed, stupid.

Haruto's stomach dropped. "It wants you in the same lane."

Seraphina looked at Bone Legion Commander.

Bone Legion Commander looked at Seraphina.

Two proud, violent people realizing the raid required them to occupy the same narrative space without killing each other.

Elara's voice cut in, sharp. "Don't perform for the boss. Perform for the party."

Seraphina swallowed pride like it was medicine.

She stepped into the center lane.

Bone Legion Commander stepped with her—shoulder to shoulder, not touching, closer than either preferred.

The Warden unleashed a crossfire beam—gold and violet twisted together, meant to punish solo heroes and split teams.

Seraphina declared again, shorter this time, rawer:

"I protect because I choose to—not because the System assigned me a role."

The Commander answered—not insult, counterpoint:

"And I discipline because chaos kills the people you swear to save."

The beam shattered against their combined stance.

> **SHIELD LAYER 2:** broken  
> **Hidden stat:** Unity +2  
> **Note:** rivalry harnessed without fracture

Haruto felt it through Party Leader Authority—a spike of real power, messy and honest, nothing like the gods' polished buffs.

Velvet exhaled behind him, relief disguised as professionalism.

Luminara murmured, "There they are."

The Warden's visor changed.

Scrolling text accelerated—no longer faction keywords.

> **ANOMALY DETECTED:** cross-faction synergy stable  
> **Countermeasure:** target Party Leader Authority  
> **Phase 2 adaptation:** Authority Severance

The third shield layer didn't glow gold or violet.

It pulsed slate gray—Correction color—and wrapped the Warden's core like armor made of rules.

Haruto's wrist burned.

Cyan tab flickered, angry.

The boss turned its visor toward him specifically.

A new window opened—only for Haruto, but everyone could see his face when he read it:

> **BOSS MECHANIC:** Authority Severance  
> **Effect:** disables cross-faction recruitment + override functions  
> **Duration:** until party proves "non-anomalous" alignment OR Phase 3 trigger

"They're coming for you," Elara said.

Not panic.

Fact.

Seraphina stepped out of the center lane, shield rising toward the Warden—not away from Haruto. "Over my—"

"Don't declare at the boss," Haruto snapped. "Declare at *me* if you have to. Tell it I'm not alone."

Seraphina's jaw tightened.

She looked at Haruto—not the symbol, the person.

"You are not alone," she said.

Bone Legion Commander's shadow tethers snapped to Haruto's ankles, anchoring. "Confirmed."

Velvet's hand on his back—steady pressure. "Always."

Mira shouted, "Group hug later! Boss now!"

The Warden raised both gavels.

Its visor displayed a single line for the whole arena to read:

> **Phase 3 condition:** Party Unity Override required  
> **Warning:** unauthorized mechanic; deploy at own risk

Haruto's mouth went dry.

He'd felt Override lurking in Party Leader Authority since the first time he'd recruited across factions—an option grayed out, labeled PROVISIONAL, like the world hadn't decided whether to let him have it.

The Warden cracked its neck—joints like gavel hinges.

"Oh good," Haruto whispered. "The tutorial finally ends."

Sunny made a sound that wasn't a chirp.

Phase three loaded.
