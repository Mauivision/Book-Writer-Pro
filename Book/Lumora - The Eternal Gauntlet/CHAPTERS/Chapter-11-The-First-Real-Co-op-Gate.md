## Chapter 11 — The First Real Co-op Gate

With Bone Legion Commander now walking in the same direction as them, the dungeon changed its attitude.

Haruto felt it immediately.

The air in the corridor beyond the arena sharpened—as if Sky Dungeon #7 had stopped treating them like a tutorial party and started treating them like a *problem.*

The walls narrowed again, runes dimmer, the light less flattering. Gold veins in the stone appeared only in thin traces now, interrupted by violet lines like hairline cracks. A faint hum ran through the floor beneath Haruto’s boots, a low vibration that made his teeth feel slightly too close together.

Seraphina walked on Haruto’s left.

Bone Legion Commander walked on his right.

That alone felt like a war crime against the Radiant aesthetic.

Luminara drifted behind them with the look of someone watching two rival protagonists share a hallway.

Velvet kept to Haruto’s rear-left, calm as ever, her presence like an anchor.

Mira walked behind everyone, humming, because of course she did.

Sunny floated above Haruto’s head like a curse that had learned to chirp.

“Look at your balanced party!” Sunny sang. “So healthy! So functional!”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned slowly toward the orb.

“I will destroy it,” he said flatly.

Sunny chirped, delighted. “You can’t!”

“Noted,” Bone Legion Commander replied. “I will still attempt it.”

Haruto whispered, “Please don’t start a fight with the tutorial.”

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked once. “It started it.”

Seraphina’s voice was tight. “Stop wasting breath. The dungeon listens.”

Sunny chirped, “She’s right! The dungeon listens! Like a good friend!”

Haruto muttered, “We have different definitions of friend.”

The System chimed softly.

> **ZONE:** Mid-Dungeon Corridor  
> **Threat:** Rising  
> **Tip:** Social harmony reduces spawn rate.  
> **Tip:** You will not achieve social harmony.

Haruto exhaled through his nose. “Okay.”

Ahead, the corridor opened into another chamber.

This one was rectangular and longer than it was wide, with a ceiling that arched like a cathedral that had decided to become a hallway.

At the far end stood a door.

Not the Radiant Seal Gate from earlier.

This door looked older, less polished—stone framed by two statues.

The left statue was a Radiant knight, sword raised, face serene.

The right statue was a hooded figure carved in shadow-stone, one hand extended as if offering something unseen.

Between them, the door itself was split down the center.

Left half: gold inlay, radiant runes.

Right half: dark stone, violet cracks.

Haruto’s UI pulsed.

> **CO-OP GATE DETECTED:** Twin Lock  
> **Requirement:** Light activation + Shadow activation + timed sequence  
> **Warning:** Gate will spawn enemies if you hesitate.

Seraphina stepped forward, eyes narrowing. “A gate that acknowledges Shadow.”

Bone Legion Commander’s skull tilted. “A gate that acknowledges reality.”

Seraphina’s grip tightened.

Haruto raised a hand instantly. “No. Not now. Save your rivalry for after we’re alive.”

Both of them looked at him.

Haruto’s throat tightened, but he held eye contact anyway.

Seraphina’s gaze softened a fraction—reluctant acceptance.

Bone Legion Commander’s jaw clicked—approval, maybe.

Sunny chimed. “Wow! The Player is parenting!”

Haruto whispered, “Stop.”

Sunny chirped, “No!”

They approached the Twin Lock.

The floor in front of it was marked with two circles—one gold, one violet—like positions on a board.

Two plates, embedded into the stone.

The System chimed, and for a moment the font looked almost excited.

> **INSTRUCTION:**  
> **Light Plate:** Radiant oath-bearer / sanctified caster  
> **Shadow Plate:** Veil-aligned entity / curse-tech / undead commander  
> **Timing:** Both within 5 seconds  
> **Penalty for failure:** “Unhelpful spawn”

Mira whispered, “Unhelpful spawn is my brand.”

Velvet’s voice was calm. “Seraphina. Commander. Step on the plates.”

Seraphina and Bone Legion Commander moved at the same time.

They both stepped forward—

—and stopped.

Not because they were afraid.

Because neither wanted to be the first to move like they were following the other’s lead.

Haruto’s eye twitched.

The dungeon hummed louder.

The System chimed.

> **WARNING:** Hesitation detected.  
> **TIP:** Pride is a debuff.

Luminara sighed. “You two are adorable.”

Seraphina snapped, “Do not—”

Bone Legion Commander said, flatly, “We are not.”

Sunny chirped, “They are!”

The floor shuddered.

Small vents opened along the room’s walls.

Haruto’s UI flashed red.

> **SPAWN TIMER:** 10… 9… 8…

Haruto’s voice sharpened. “SERAPHINA. COMMANDER. PLATES. NOW.”

The moment he barked the order, something inside Haruto clicked again—authority not as dominance, but as decision.

Seraphina moved first, jaw clenched, stepping onto the gold circle.

Bone Legion Commander stepped onto the violet circle half a second later.

The plates flared.

Gold light rose around Seraphina like a halo.

Violet mist curled around the commander’s boots like a cloak.

The door did not open.

Instead, a new window popped up.

Not the System.

Not Sunny.

This one floated between the statues like a judge’s verdict.

> **SEQUENCE REQUIRED:**  
> **Step 1:** Light declares intent.  
> **Step 2:** Shadow accepts terms.  
> **Step 3:** Player confirms unity.

Seraphina’s mouth opened.

Then closed.

Her cheeks pinked.

“It wants me to… declare intent,” she muttered, scandalized.

Luminara’s smile turned wicked. “Oh?”

Mira clasped her hands. “Say something romantic!”

Seraphina snapped, “It is not romantic!”

Sunny chirped brightly, “Everything is romantic!”

The spawn timer continued.

> **SPAWN TIMER:** 6… 5…

Seraphina’s eyes narrowed with furious dignity.

She lifted her chin and spoke like she was pronouncing a sacred vow.

“By the Radiant Dominion,” Seraphina declared, voice echoing through the chamber, “I swear to protect this party and its Player with unwavering light.”

Gold flared.

The door’s left half pulsed.

The window updated.

> **STEP 1 COMPLETE**  
> **STEP 2:** Shadow accepts terms.

Bone Legion Commander’s skull turned slightly, jaw clicking once as if annoyed by the theatrics.

Then he spoke, voice low and precise.

“By the Veil Syndicate,” he said, “I acknowledge this alliance for mutual survival. I will not betray the party within dungeon bounds.”

Violet mist surged.

The door’s right half pulsed.

The window updated.

> **STEP 2 COMPLETE**  
> **STEP 3:** Player confirms unity.

Haruto’s throat went dry.

He could feel every eye in the room on him.

Seraphina’s intense, honorable gaze.

Bone Legion Commander’s empty stare, somehow heavier.

Luminara’s amused attention.

Velvet’s calm expectation.

Mira’s delighted anticipation.

Sunny’s smug little float.

The spawn timer ticked down.

> **SPAWN TIMER:** 2… 1…

Haruto took a breath.

Not for drama.

Not for romance.

For survival.

He lifted his cuffed wrist and placed his palm over the Radiant Party Cuff, as if grounding himself.

“I confirm unity,” Haruto said, voice steady. “We clear this dungeon together. Light and Shadow. No one gets left behind.”

The moment the words left his mouth—

The door flared blindingly.

Gold and violet surged, twisting together like two streams forced into one river.

The statues’ eyes lit.

The Twin Lock clicked.

And the door opened.

The spawn vents snapped shut mid-release with a disappointed *clack.*

Haruto’s UI chimed triumphantly.

> **CO-OP GATE CLEARED**  
> **Bonus:** Party Unity +1 (temporary)  
> **Penalty avoided:** “Unhelpful spawn”

Haruto released a breath he hadn’t realized he’d been holding.

“See?” Haruto said, trying to keep his voice calm. “Teamwork.”

Seraphina stepped off the plate, cheeks still faintly pink. “It was a ridiculous requirement.”

Bone Legion Commander stepped off his plate, cloak settling. “It was an efficient requirement.”

Sunny chirped, “It was adorable!”

Bone Legion Commander turned his skull toward Sunny slowly.

“You,” he said, “are the unhelpful spawn.”

Sunny chirped, delighted. “Thank you!”

Velvet’s lips curved, just barely. “Well done,” she murmured to Haruto, voice soft enough to be private without being *too* private.

Haruto’s face warmed anyway.

Mira noticed instantly and whispered, “He’s blushing again.”

Haruto hissed, “Mira—”

Seraphina snapped, “Focus!”

Luminara’s smile returned. “The dungeon will punish you for that.”

The System chimed on cue.

> **NOTICE:** Dungeon recalculating due to “unity spike”  
> **Tip:** Your success is annoying it.

Haruto stared at the open doorway.

Beyond it, the corridor descended deeper into Sky Dungeon #7, light fading, shadows thickening, and the faint hum of something bigger waiting ahead.

Haruto tightened his grip on the idea that he could lead.

Then he stepped forward.

“Okay,” Haruto said quietly. “Next lesson.”
