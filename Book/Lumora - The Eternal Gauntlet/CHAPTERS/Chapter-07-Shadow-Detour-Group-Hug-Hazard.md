## Chapter 07 — Shadow Detour (Group Hug Hazard)

The detour corridor breathed like a living thing.

Cold mist rolled out in slow waves, curling around Haruto’s ankles as if the dungeon were trying to grab him by the socks and drag him into a new genre.

The walls changed too.

The pale, radiant stone gave way to darker blocks—still clean, still carved with ridiculous care, but etched with patterns that made Haruto think of graveyards and arcade neon at the same time. Thin lines of blue-violet light ran through the seams like circuitry, pulsing faintly in time with his heartbeat.

Haruto did not like the way the corridor’s lighting seemed to sync with his emotions.

He held the Veil curse-key tighter, and it hummed softly in his palm, like it approved of the gloom.

Seraphina walked in front as always, but even she seemed uncomfortable here. Her armor’s glow dimmed slightly, as if the dungeon were politely refusing to let her be the brightest thing in the room.

Luminara floated beside Haruto, her flame-hair dialed down to a gentle ember. “Welcome to the Veil side of the island,” she murmured.

“It feels illegal,” Haruto said.

Mira whispered, thrilled, “It feels romantic.”

Seraphina snapped, “It feels cursed.”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm. “It feels watched.”

Sunny bobbed at shoulder height, smugly cheerful. “Congratulations! You have unlocked an optional route!”

Haruto’s eye twitched. “Optional means mandatory, right?”

Sunny chirped, “Optional means you can choose whether to suffer now or later!”

Haruto stared at it. “That’s the same thing.”

Sunny spun, as if pleased with itself. “Exactly!”

The corridor widened into a chamber that looked like a mirror of the Radiant puzzle room—except darker, colder, and somehow more stylish about it.

Mist pooled along the floor like water that didn’t trust anyone.

In the center stood a stone arch with no door. Just a frame, carved with moon-shaped motifs.

Inside the frame, the air shimmered.

Not with light.

With shadow.

Haruto’s UI chimed, quieter than usual, like it didn’t want to disturb the vibe.

> **HAZARD ZONE:** Veil Fog Gate  
> **Effect:** reduces visibility; increases “misunderstanding” chance  
> **Tip:** Do not flirt in fog.

Mira leaned toward Haruto. “What counts as flirting?”

The System chimed instantly.

> **ANSWER:** You.

Haruto made a strangled sound.

Seraphina stepped toward the fog gate, sword raised. “We pass through quickly. Stay close.”

Haruto immediately imagined all of them walking into the mist, losing each other, and then being jump-scared by their own teammates.

He also imagined the System popping up a tutorial titled *“Lesson 6: Panic.”*

He took a breath, forcing calm into his lungs.

*No spikes.*

*No drama.*

*No—*

Mira slipped her hand around his arm.

Haruto froze.

“Life-or-death adjacency,” Mira whispered, innocent.

Haruto’s face warmed.

The System chimed.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased (minor)  
> **NOTE:** Fog intensifies “touch damage.”

Seraphina turned her head slowly. “Touch… damage?”

Luminara’s lips curved. “Oh?”

Velvet’s smile stayed serene, but her gaze sharpened. “Mira.”

Mira released Haruto’s arm instantly, hands up. “No touching in fog. Got it. Rules are rules.”

Haruto exhaled. “Thank you.”

Sunny bobbed. “Entering hazard zone in three… two… one!”

Haruto hissed, “Why are you counting like—”

They stepped through.

The world went gray.

Not darkness.

A thick, pearly fog that swallowed edges and muffled sound. Haruto could still see Seraphina’s armor glow faintly ahead, and Luminara’s ember-hair to his right, but everything else was reduced to silhouettes and suggestions.

The fog smelled faintly of rain on stone and something sweet—like candy from a haunted arcade.

Mira’s voice came from somewhere behind him. “Haruto? Are you—”

“I’m here,” Haruto said quickly.

Velvet’s voice slid in from his left, calm and close. “Keep your breathing steady.”

“I am,” Haruto lied.

A whisper of motion brushed past his shoulder.

Haruto jerked.

Seraphina’s voice snapped from ahead. “Hold position!”

Luminara’s voice stayed amused. “Something just moved.”

Sunny chimed brightly in the fog like a bell in a horror movie. “Great news! You are being tested!”

Haruto whispered, “That’s never good news.”

The System chimed with rare sympathy.

> **TIP:** Correct.

The fog shifted.

Shapes formed—thin, humanoid silhouettes made of mist and shadow, drifting toward the party.

Above one of them, a label appeared in Haruto’s vision, flickering like a glitch:

> **Mist Mimic** — Lv. 2  
> **Trait:** copies what you expect

Haruto’s stomach dropped. “Copies what I expect?”

Velvet’s voice stayed calm. “Do not imagine anything.”

Haruto stared at her silhouette. “That’s impossible!”

The System chimed.

> **TIP:** Skill issue.

Seraphina’s sword flashed, cutting through fog—and for a moment, the blade’s glow carved the chamber into clarity.

The Mist Mimic split in half.

Then rejoined itself like the cut had been a suggestion.

Seraphina’s breath hitched in outrage. “It ignored my strike.”

Luminara lifted her hand and snapped her fingers.

A thin line of heat ran through the fog like a laser pointer.

The Mist Mimic shivered, edges blurring, then—

—reformed, still intact.

Luminara hummed. “Physical and heat don’t stick.”

Velvet’s voice was thoughtful. “Then we need purification.”

Haruto’s brain supplied an image of Velvet gently pressing her glowing hand to the mist and whispering a compliment until it dissolved.

Haruto’s face warmed.

The System chimed loudly.

> **JEALOUSY AGGRO:** Increased  
> **FOG REACTION:** Mimics strengthened by “private thoughts”

Haruto made a sound like he’d been punched in dignity.

“STOP READING MY THOUGHTS!” he yelled at the air.

Sunny chimed. “The dungeon can’t read your thoughts.”

Haruto snapped, “Then why does it react to them?”

Sunny replied cheerfully, “Because you show them on your face!”

Seraphina barked, “Haruto, focus!”

Haruto forced his mind blank.

Which immediately made him think of how to force his mind blank.

Which made his stress spike.

Which—

The fog thickened.

Three more Mist Mimics drifted into range.

The System chimed.

> **SPAWN INCREASE:** Emotional loop detected

Mira’s voice rose in panic. “Okay, no! No! This is not happening!”

Haruto couldn’t see her clearly, but he heard her rummaging through her satchel like she was searching for the one item that could beat an existential mechanic.

“Mira,” Velvet said calmly, “what do you have?”

“Something,” Mira declared, and then Haruto heard a cork pop.

A sweet scent burst into the fog—warm and sugary, like honey and cinnamon.

Haruto’s UI chimed in confusion.

> **ITEM USED:** “Comfort Stew” (Aroma)  
> **Effect:** reduces panic; increases nostalgia  
> **Side effect:** attracts hugs

Haruto whispered, horrified, “Attracts—”

Something squelched.

A familiar, cheerful voice rang out from the fog.

“HUGS?!”

Haruto’s blood ran cold.

Seraphina’s voice rose in instant horror. “Absolutely not!”

The fog bulged.

And then—

—a glowing blob bounced into view, luminous and excited, tendrils forming hearts in midair.

Ectoplasm Elemental.

It had somehow entered the dungeon.

It had somehow followed the smell of stew.

And it was now, apparently, part of this hazard.

“Group hug time!” Ecto squealed.

Haruto shouted, “No! Not in fog!”

Ecto bounced closer.

The Mist Mimics reacted.

They shifted shapes, copying what they “expected,” and what the room now expected—thanks to Ecto—was **hugging.**

The Mimics’ arms stretched, misty tendrils extending like embrace-shaped traps.

Seraphina swung her sword in a wide arc, trying to clear space. “Back!”

The fog swallowed the glow.

The Mimics drifted through the swing like ghosts.

Velvet’s voice sharpened. “Seraphina, barrier!”

Seraphina slammed her sword point-down again.

Light flared, a dome of Radiance forming—thinner than before, strained by the Veil fog.

The Mimics hit the barrier and hissed, edges burning away.

It worked.

For one second.

Then Ecto bounced into the barrier from the inside, pressing against it like a kid against aquarium glass.

“Hug barrier!” Ecto cheered.

The barrier wobbled.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **WARNING:** Barrier destabilized by “friendly pressure”

Haruto screamed, “FRIENDLY PRESSURE IS NOT A THING!”

Sunny chirped brightly, “It is now!”

Haruto’s brain finally clicked into a new mode.

Not panic.

Not reaction.

Leadership.

If the dungeon was treating emotions like mechanics, then he had to treat *people* like mechanics too.

Not in a cold way.

In a *team* way.

Haruto inhaled, forcing his voice steady.

“Ecto!” Haruto shouted. “New rule!”

Ecto froze mid-bounce, tendrils forming a question mark. “Rule?”

Haruto pointed toward the nearest Mist Mimic pressing against the barrier. “Hug that.”

Ecto’s core brightened. “Hug enemy?!”

“Yes!” Haruto said. “Hug it so hard it can’t copy anything else!”

Seraphina shouted, “Player, that is—”

“Brilliant,” Luminara finished, smiling.

Velvet’s voice stayed calm but approving. “It will anchor the mimic’s form.”

Ecto bounced once, twice, then launched itself at the Mist Mimic like a living tackle.

It wrapped the mimic in glowing goo.

The mimic shuddered, its mist-form struggling to maintain shape.

The hug—absurdly—forced it to become more solid.

Velvet lifted her hand.

A pulse of cool, cleansing light washed over the hugged mimic.

The mimic let out a sound like a sigh and dissolved completely, leaving only a faint sparkle in the fog.

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **ENEMY DEFEATED:** Mist Mimic  
> **Method:** Hug + Purify  
> **Note:** This will be in Patch Notes.

Haruto didn’t have time to celebrate.

Two more mimics drifted in.

Haruto snapped, “Ecto! Again! Hug the next one!”

Ecto saluted with a tendril. “Yes, boss!”

Seraphina’s head whipped toward Haruto. “Do not call him boss—”

Luminara’s voice was delighted. “Too late.”

The System chimed.

> **TITLE UNLOCK PROGRESS:** “Hug Commander” 1/3

Haruto’s face went pale. “No.”

Sunny chirped, “Yes!”

Ecto tackled the next mimic.

Velvet purified it.

Luminara snapped a line of heat through the fog to keep the last mimic away from Haruto.

Seraphina held the barrier steady, jaw clenched, as if sheer willpower could prevent the dungeon from being ridiculous.

Within moments, the fog chamber fell quiet.

The mist thinned slightly, as if the dungeon itself were reconsidering its life choices.

Sunny bobbed, cheerful as ever. “Congratulations! You have learned Lesson 3: Detours!”

Haruto snapped, “We learned that hugs are a weapon!”

Sunny replied, “That too!”

The fog gate ahead shimmered.

The chamber’s shadow arch—previously behind them—now glowed faintly, like an exit opening.

Velvet’s voice was calm. “The hazard is ending. We move.”

Seraphina sheathed her sword with a sharp motion. “Ectoplasm Elemental,” she said, voice strained, “you are… not part of the party.”

Ecto bounced happily beside Haruto. “I’m part of the hugs!”

Mira sighed dreamily. “Honestly, same.”

Seraphina’s armor creaked again.

Haruto tightened his grip on the Veil key and spoke quickly before the dungeon could spawn anything else.

“Ecto,” Haruto said, forcing gentleness into his voice, “thank you. But you have to go.”

Ecto’s tendrils drooped. “No hug?”

Haruto swallowed.

He could feel the trap potential in the air.

He could also see Ecto’s pure sincerity.

“One hug,” Haruto said carefully, “after we’re out of the dungeon.”

Ecto brightened like a lantern. “Promise hug!”

The System chimed instantly.

> **CONTRACT UPDATE:** “Promise hug” clause registered  
> **Penalty for failure:** -1 morale (party), +1 sadness (Ecto)

Haruto whispered, “Why is sadness a stat.”

Sunny chirped, “Because it’s funny!”

They stepped through the exit shimmer.

The fog dropped away all at once, like someone had toggled an effect off.

The world snapped back into bright Sky Dungeon clarity.

Haruto blinked, eyes watering at the sudden light.

They were back in the wide chamber near the Radiant Seal Gate.

The enormous door loomed ahead, still pulsing with sanctified glow.

But now—now the gate felt different.

The runes around it pulsed faster.

The floor vibrated faintly under Haruto’s boots.

Like something on the other side had been listening.

Sunny floated up proudly. “Welcome back! Insert the key to proceed!”

Haruto lifted the Veil curse-key.

The metal hummed in his palm, eager.

Seraphina stepped to the Sun Plate embedded in the gate’s frame and placed her palm on it without hesitation.

Radiant light flared.

Haruto slid the Veil key toward the lock.

The moment the key touched the seal—

The gate screamed.

Not in sound.

In light and shadow.

The entire chamber flashed, half gold, half violet, and the runes around the door lit up like a heart going into overdrive.

Haruto staggered back, blinking hard.

Velvet’s hand caught his sleeve—quick, practical. Life-or-death emergency. Allowed.

“Steady,” she murmured.

Haruto nodded, breath shaky.

The key turned.

The seal split down the middle with a clean, perfect line.

And from behind the gate, a low rumble rolled out—deep enough to feel in Haruto’s ribs.

Seraphina’s eyes widened. “That’s not a door mechanism.”

Luminara’s smile vanished, replaced by focus. “No.”

Mira whispered, delighted and terrified, “Something woke up.”

Sunny chirped brightly, as if announcing a prize.

“CONGRATULATIONS!” it sang. “You have unlocked the next area!”

Haruto’s UI chimed.

> **NOTICE:** Gate opened  
> **NOTICE:** Mini-boss agitation detected  
> **TIP:** Try not to die immediately after succeeding.

The gate began to swing inward.

And the air beyond it smelled like warm stone, old lightning, and something that had been sleeping very badly.
