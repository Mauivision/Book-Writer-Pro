## Chapter 28 — Public Choice Cliffhanger

Dawn over Castle Lumora looked like a loading screen that wanted applause.

Gold light poured across the sky-islands. Marble warmed. Banners unfurled. Somewhere a choir rehearsed a hymn with too much confidence.

Haruto stood on the outer steps of the Grand Radiant Hall and wished he were back in a tutorial dungeon where the worst thing that could happen was a lying quest giver.

The steps had become a stage.

Radiant nobles packed the upper tiers, white and gleaming, faces polished for judgment. Veil representatives lined the fog-shrouded lower approach—Nyx at the front, Bone Legion Commander behind her like a promise of consequences. Between them, the ordinary citizens of Lumora filled the square in a restless mass that generated its own UI overlay:

> **EVENT:** Public Alignment Trial  
> **Audience:** faction + civilian  
> **Objective:** Eternal Player selects primary allegiance  
> **Failure state:** corrective raid (imminent)

Haruto's party flanked him in the only configuration the System seemed to respect: mixed, visible, impossible to ignore.

Seraphina on his right, armor catching sun like a second sun.

Bone Legion Commander on his left, shadow pooling at his boots.

Luminara and Velvet behind him—power and triage, heat and calm.

Mira had somehow acquired a vendor tray of "support potions" and stood near the steps like a tavern annexed the apocalypse.

Ecto and Chiller Executioner occupied the fog line with the Veil, Ecto waving at civilians, Chiller leaning on his axe looking offended by architecture.

Elara stood one step below Haruto, not claiming him, not claiming anyone—watching the crowd the way soldiers watched horizons.

Sunny's crate sat at Haruto's feet.

The orb inside hadn't chirped since last night.

That scared him more than the crowd.

Chancellor Aurex Valebright spoke first, because bureaucrats loved a microphone.

"Eternal Player Takahashi," Aurex said, smile gentle and sharp. "Lumora has been… *generous* with you. Cross-faction recruitment violates sanctified matchmaking. The Radiant Dominion offers anchor, protection, and purpose. Choose Light. Choose stability. Choose the story that keeps people alive."

Nyx's laugh drifted up from the fog line. "Choose * leash,* you mean."

She stepped forward, voice carrying without shouting.

"Veil Syndicate offers the Player freedom," Nyx said. "No marble cages. No purity theater. Choose Shadow. Choose honesty. Choose the faction that admits the world is a game and plays to win."

The crowd murmured—betting energy without the board, pressure without release.

Haruto's HUD chimed.

> **CHOICE WINDOW:** Primary Faction Alignment  
> **Option A:** Radiant Dominion  
> **Option B:** Veil Syndicate  
> **Timer:** 60 seconds

No third option.

Of course.

Haruto looked at the timer and felt something in his chest go very still.

Seraphina's hand flexed near her sword hilt—not commanding him, restraining herself.

Velvet's presence stayed steady at his back.

Bone Legion Commander's skull turned slightly toward him. Waiting.

Elara whispered, barely audible, "Say it."

Haruto cleared his throat.

"You're both asking me to choose ownership," he said, voice carrying farther than he expected—Party Leader Authority doing its quiet work, making people listen. "Radiant wants me as symbol. Veil wants me as weapon. The System wants me as *alignment* so it can stop being nervous."

Aurex's smile tightened. "Player—"

"I'm not finished."

Haruto lifted his wrist.

The cyan route tab pulsed like a heartbeat refusing to be categorized.

"There is a third path," Haruto said. "Unity without ownership. Cooperation without custody. I lead a mixed party because mixed is *true.* Because people aren't faction slots. Because Seraphina and the Commander argue like a married couple and Luminara sets the air on fire and Velvet keeps us alive and Mira turns disasters into breakfast and Ecto hugs monsters until they feel bad about it—"

Mira shouted, "Accurate!"

"—and I'm not dissolving that because your politics are scared of a college kid with good instincts."

The square reacted like he'd thrown a grenade made of feelings.

Radiant nobles gasped.

Veil representatives cheered—then caught themselves, because cheering was also a trap.

Halvion stepped forward, voice cutting. "This is heresy."

Nyx smiled, slow and delighted. "This is *entertainment.*"

Haruto's timer hit zero.

The choice window did not accept A or B.

It *cracked*—text fracturing across the sky like broken glass:

> **INVALID RESPONSE:** third-path declaration registered  
> **System state:** trigger acknowledged  
> **Corrective event:** unlocking—

The marble steps trembled.

Not an earthquake.

A *pulse*—deep, rhythmic, like the world grinding teeth.

Haruto's party closed ranks without being ordered.

Seraphina's shield snapped up.

Bone Legion Commander's shadows flared into tether lines.

Velvet's hands glowed with preemptive healing.

Luminara's hair ignited from gold to white-hot.

Elara drew her blade—not at the crowd, at the sky.

The sky answered.

A seam appeared above the Grand Radiant Hall—jagged, wrong, slate gray against all that heroic gold. Through the seam, something moved: vast, angular, a silhouette like a judge built from raid mechanics and bad intentions.

The Patch Herald's voice boomed across the square, cheerful and terrible:

"Attention, Lumora! A **World Boss Event** has been scheduled for your continued educational benefit!"

The crowd screamed.

Haruto's HUD exploded into red:

> **RAID ALERT:** Correction Warden — Phase Raid  
> **Requirement:** mixed-faction party (mandatory)  
> **Failure:** zone rollback + reputation catastrophe  
> **Start:** IMMINENT

Sunny's crate rattled.

For one raw second, the orb's voice slipped out of chirp and into something else—flat, distant, like a log entry:

"—unity threshold exceeded; deploying immune response—"

Then Sunny snapped back. "Raid! Raid! Fun!"

Haruto grabbed the crate, heart hammering.

He looked at his party.

He looked at the sky splitting open like a door the gods couldn't close fast enough.

"No ownership," Haruto said, mostly to himself. "Just survival."

The seam widened.

The world-boss notification flashed once more, hard enough to burn afterimages:

> **Raid event imminent.**

And the square—marble, fog, banners, choir, betting crowd, politics and prayer and panic—became a loading zone.
