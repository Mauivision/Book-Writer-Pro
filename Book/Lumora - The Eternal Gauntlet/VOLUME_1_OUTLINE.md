## Volume 1 Outline — *Summoned, Surrounded, and Suddenly Very Popular*

### Theme
Haruto refuses faction ownership and starts building a real party—earning power through teamwork and sincerity in a world that tries to reduce relationships to “systems.”

### Level band (Book 1)
- **This book covers Levels 1–5.**
- **Big Boss (Book finale / Level 5 capstone)**: the **World Boss Raid** (Arc 5) — the System forces public choice, then punishes disunity until Haruto unlocks **Party Unity Override**.

### Continuity note (post-draft integration)
- Chapters **12–15** now include subtle **Verdant foreshadow** (sap/leaf tells + mirror flickers).
- **Chapter 16** now ends with **Elara Sylvandrel** revealed and a brief **Equinox** seam-tease.
- A bridge chapter has been added: `CHAPTERS/Chapter-17-Verdant-Interrupt.md`

## Season 2 / Volume 2 Trope Plan — *Verdant Spires & The Vow Mechanic*

### Core trope
**“Comedy party meets mythic responsibility.”** The Systems that joke about jealousy are revealed as **World Core protocols**. Haruto stops being “lucky” and starts being **selected**.

### Escalation (without losing the fun)
- **Cyan route UI** appears more often (Codex fragments, “Verdant” route updates).
- **Sunny** becomes more erratic as Unity rises (monitor-fragment stress; comedy + menace).
- **Corrections** spawn: anti-meta enemies that punish cross-faction synergy and “healthy choices.”
- **Politics tighten**: Radiant hardliners and Veil opportunists both try to define Elara as theirs (and fail).

### Elara + Haruto romance arc (earned “elf wife” payoff)
- **Meet-cute under threat** (Bridge Chapter): Elara warns, tests, and then helps.
- **Trust ladder**: Haruto learns the Gauntlet’s verbs (Build/Destroy/Move) have moral weight; Elara respects restraint.
- **Jealousy comedy collisions**: Mira/Velvet/Luminara/Seraphina react to “quiet competent elf warrior” energy; the System weaponizes it.
- **Resonance reveal**: Elara’s bloodline/verdant signature can “hear” the cyan route; she stabilizes Haruto when the Gauntlet overdrives.
- **Vow / Rite climax** (end of Volume 2): an **Elf Vow Mechanic** that is romantic and mechanical:
  - It functions as a bond-ritual that **stabilizes Overdrive** and unlocks a new “Renewal” pathway without forcing a full wedding too early.
  - It is framed as “provisional” by elf tradition (trial vow), with a **true wedding** promised when the world is safe.

### Volume 2 set pieces (suggested)
- **Verdant Spires node**: forest + brass ruin blend; introduces Elara’s home rules.
- **Architect Ruins under Sky Dungeon #7**: forces Radiant+Veil+Verdant cooperation.
- **Equinox appearances**: short scene-cuts that tempt Haruto toward rewriting reality for “fun.”
- **Boss**: a “Rollback/Correction Sentinel” that targets Unity and bond events specifically.
