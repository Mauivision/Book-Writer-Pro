## Faction pressure mechanics

### Ownership attempts
Both factions try to "own" Haruto via:
- Radiant: hero branding, hearings, Velvet conversion
- Veil: contracts, leverage, "freedom" as chains

### Third path (Haruto rule)
Player choices that set:
- `refused_radiant_ownership`
- `refused_veil_contract`
- `party_holds_keys` (not faction)

**Mechanical reward:** Unity meter gains faster; triggers dungeon retaliation + Correction Heat.

### Forced co-op (world boss)
System mandate M28–M35: mixed-faction party required. Hardliners (Halvion, Veil opportunists) sabotage in M29.
