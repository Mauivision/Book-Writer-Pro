What this is

A rules-light, tarot-first story game: players bring any standard 78-card tarot deck (Rider–Waite style names assumed for reference text). One person facilitates time and prompts (call them the Guide); everyone else plays protagonists. This page is the rules and oracle companion — keep a phone, tablet, or laptop at the table, or print the rules later if you prefer paper.

No collectible card packs required. If you do not have a deck yet, use the digital draw in the Play tab and imagine the art you like.

What you need

  • One tarot deck per table (shared) or one per player (solo journaling) — your choice.
  • 2+ people recommended; solo works as a journaling loop.
  • Notes, a timer if you like tight scenes, and safety tools your group already trusts.

Core tracks (pick one style)

Radiant Veil Unity Jealousy

Generic mode: each protagonist has Hope (0–5), Strain (0–5), and a shared Bond token (0–5). When fiction earns it, the Guide awards +1 Hope or Bond; when the story bites, +1 Strain. If any personal track hits 5, the next beat must address it (breakdown, confession, bargain, or rest).

Lumora mode: rename Hope → Unity posture, Strain → Jealousy heat, Bond → Party trust. Use Radiant/Veil as stances in narration, not alignment: they are different kinds of courage.

Loop

  1. Scene question. The Guide names a clear question: “What breaks at the tavern?”
  2. Draw. Pull cards into the chosen spread (physical deck or digital assist).
  3. Read together. Use prompts on this site as sparks, not scripture. First impression counts.
  4. Play a beat. 5–15 minutes of roleplay or narration.
  5. Adjust tracks. Mark Hope / Strain / Bond (or Unity / Jealousy / trust).
  6. Cut or continue. If the Door card feels answered, fade out; if not, pull one more card only on purpose.

Guide principles

  • Ask pointed questions; reward honest answers with screen time.
  • Reversals are optional. If you use them, treat them as “tilt,” not “bad.”
  • Minors are everyday magic; Majors are turning points — give Majors a slow beat.
  • When dice would help: flip a coin, or let the highest Strain player choose who takes the risk.

    Ranks — story scale

    • Ace — seed / offer
    • 2–4 — tension, alliance, stability
    • 5–7 — friction, return, trial
    • 8–10 — grind, exhaustion, consequence peak
    • Page — rumor / student
    • Knight — obsession / charge
    • Queen — care with teeth
    • King — authority with cost
