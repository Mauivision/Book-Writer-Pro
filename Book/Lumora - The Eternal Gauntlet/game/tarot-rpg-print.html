What you need

  • 1 tarot deck (shared) or 1 per player (solo).
  • Notes. Optional timer.
  • A “Guide” to pace scenes and ask questions.

Reversals optional. If reversed: treat as tilt (delay, excess, inversion).

Core tracks (quick)

Hope 0–5, Strain 0–5, shared Bond 0–5.

  • Earn honest progress → +1 Hope or +1 Bond.
  • Pay a real cost / take a hit → +1 Strain.
  • When any track hits 5, the next beat must address it.

Lumora rename: Hope→Unity posture, Strain→Jealousy heat, Bond→Party trust.

Loop

  1. Scene question: “What breaks at the tavern?”
  2. Spread: choose a spread and draw into positions.
  3. Read: take first impressions; prompts are sparks.
  4. Play: 5–15 minutes of narration / roleplay.
  5. Mark: Hope / Strain / Bond adjustments.
  6. Cut: end on the Door (or pull one more card only on purpose).

Three-card beat

  • Now: what’s true right now?
  • Friction: what resists / complicates?
  • Door: what opens if you engage honestly?

Five-card act

  • Goal, Obstacle, Secret, Cost, If you push.

Suits

  • Wands: drive, craft, conflict, initiative
  • Cups: emotion, bonds, morale, memory
  • Swords: truth, pressure, sharp choices, anxiety
  • Pentacles: resources, body, reputation, loot

Ranks

  • Ace seed / offer
  • 2–4 tension, alliance, stability
  • 5–7 friction, return, trial
  • 8–10 grind, exhaustion, consequence peak
  • Page rumor / student
  • Knight obsession / charge
  • Queen care with teeth
  • King authority with cost
