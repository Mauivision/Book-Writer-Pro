## Lumora: The Eternal Gauntlet

### Status
- **Phase**: Planning → Drafting (Volume 1)
- **Tone**: Silly anime-drama, dungeon-crawler chaos, PG-13 flirty comedy, found-family heart

### Logline
A normal college gamer is isekai’d into **Lumora**, a living multiplayer dungeon-crawler world run by bored twin gods of Light and Shadow—then breaks the “matchmaking rules” by recruiting from *both* factions, turning every dungeon run into a co-op soap opera where **respawn is free** and **jealousy is lethal**.

### Series Tagline
**Respawn is free. Romance is not.**

### Core Hook
- **Haruto Takahashi** becomes the **Eternal Player**, gaining **Party Leader Authority**—the only ability that allows **cross-faction recruitment** (Radiant Dominion + Veil Syndicate).
- The “System” reacts with escalating difficulty, social mechanics (Jealousy Aggro), and godly **Patch Notes**.

### New (Verdant Route / Elara integration)
- **Elara Sylvandrel** enters as a **Verdant “interrupt”** character tied to a cyan route signature and deeper world mechanics.
- A bridge chapter has been added: **`CHAPTERS/Chapter-17-Verdant-Interrupt.md`**.
- Optional deep lore is available under **`SIDE_STORIES/Crystal-Gauntlet-Lore/`** (Architect Codex fragments).

### Folder Guide
- **`CHAPTERS/`**: Manuscript chapters (one chapter per `.md`)
- **`CHARACTER_PROFILES.md`**: Cast roster and quick sheets
- **`SERIES_BIBLE.md`**: World rules, factions, locations, recurring mechanics
- **`VOLUME_1_OUTLINE.md`**: Chapter-by-chapter blueprint for Volume 1
- **`SIDE_STORIES/`**: Optional canon expansions (Codex fragments, branch arcs)
