HOW TO OPEN AND WALK AROUND THE WORLD
======================================

1. Open Godot 4 (the editor).
2. Click "Import" and select the folder:
   lumora-gauntlet-3d
   (or drag the folder into Godot).

3. Once the project opens, press the Play button (top right) or F5.

4. You will see the Title Screen.
   - Click "Enter the Meadow" or press Enter/Space.

5. You are now in the Verdant Leak Meadow.
   Controls:
   - WASD or Arrow Keys = Move
   - Space = Dash
   - Walk toward the east side of the map (right side) to enter the Forest Ruin Gate.
   - Walk into the west trigger in the Forest zone to return.

The world is currently greybox (simple shapes) so we can quickly design and expand it.

You can freely walk around both zones right now.

Next we can add more detail, elevation, props, NPCs, etc.
