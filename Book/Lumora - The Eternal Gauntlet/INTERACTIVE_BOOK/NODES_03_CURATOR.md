## N070 — Curator Waiting Room

The door opens onto a waiting room.

Not metaphorically. Not “arena.” Not “boss chamber.”

An actual waiting room with floating numbered tickets, chairs arranged to enforce awkward silence, and a bell that never rings but still makes your spine brace.

Haruto’s quest log flickers like it’s embarrassed to be here.

> **System:** Objective added: *Be audited.*

Across the room, the **Sky Dungeon Curator** stands behind a counter that shouldn’t fit inside a dungeon. Its face is a tasteful absence: a hood, a mask, a suggestion of eyes where rules go to watch you.

It stamps something. The sound is louder than damage.

“Welcome,” it says, voice smooth as a denial. “Please confirm your party composition.”

The UI helpfully offers buttons.

- **Declare Veil alignment**
- **Declare Mixed Party (discouraged)**

The Curator tilts its head.

“Interesting,” it says, as if that word is a lever.
