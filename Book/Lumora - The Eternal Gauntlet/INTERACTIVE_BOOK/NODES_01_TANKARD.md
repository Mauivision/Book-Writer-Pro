## N010 — Golden Tankard Quip

The Golden Tankard is exactly what you’d build if you wanted a tavern to feel like a buff.

Lanternlight hangs in warm circles. The air smells like roasted spice and irresponsible optimism. A **Unity icon** flickers over one table where strangers are laughing like they’ve known each other since the patch before last.

At the bar, Mira looks up from polishing a tankard and makes eye contact like it’s a debuff application.

“You’re new,” she says, and the way she says it is a **System diagnosis**, not a guess. “Welcome to Lumora. Don’t worry—dying is free.”

She leans in.

“But romance is not.”

## N020 — Mira’s Gossip Offer

Mira slides a drink across the bar that looks like liquid sunlight and tastes like someone dared honey to become a weapon.

“Fastest route?” she repeats. “Sure. But information in Lumora has a cost.”

She doesn’t say *gold*. She doesn’t say *mana*. She doesn’t say *blood*.

She says, “A story.”

From the corner, a group of Radiant players pauses mid-laugh. From the stairwell, Veil laughter rises like a party that knows it’s not invited.

Mira taps the counter twice. A tiny UI chip appears:

> **Gossip Token:** *Spend to reroll a bad first impression.*

“Sky Dungeon #7 is a starter dungeon,” Mira says sweetly. “Which means it quietly cheats. The first lock wants Light and Shadow in a sequence. The lock also loves to watch you panic.”

Her smile sharpens.

“So tell me. Are you going in alone… or are you going to do the thing the gods hate?”

## N030 — Start Dungeon Run (Loud Choice)

Haruto turns, lifts his chin, and speaks like the tavern is an arena.

“I’m forming a party for Sky Dungeon #7,” he says. “Radiant and Veil. Together.”

Silence drops so hard it feels like damage.

Two seconds later, the tavern erupts into *whispers*—the kind that carry their own **Jealousy Aggro**.

Above Haruto’s head, a warning icon blinks into existence.

> **System Warning:** Mixed-party optics detected. Difficulty may scale “for entertainment.”

Mira watches him like she’s watching fireworks near a library.

“Bold,” she murmurs. “Stupid, maybe. But bold.”

Outside, the sky deepens into the kind of blue that means a boss is loading.
