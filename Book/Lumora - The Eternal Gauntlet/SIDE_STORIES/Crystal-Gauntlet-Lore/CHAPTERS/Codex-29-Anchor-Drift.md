## Codex 29 — Anchor Drift

Anchor burn was the part Haruto could *see*.

The faint bruised glow.

The subtle cyan inclusions in places the body insisted were only flesh.

The way the anchor’s breath sometimes hitched when the world tried to rewrite and failed.

Haruto had told himself: scars are survivable.

Then the codex showed him the part that wasn’t visible.

The part that made scars into language.

The cyan tab pulsed.

Haruto opened it.

The world blinked.

He stood in a Verdant zone again—green light engineered into calm, brass lattice under soil, stabilization disguised as nature.

Two silhouettes walked through the trees.

Outsider.

Anchor.

They weren’t holding hands.

They weren’t performing continuity.

They were simply near enough that the cyan thread stayed faintly present between them like a background process.

The codex text appeared in cyan.

> **ANCHOR DRIFT**  
> Anchor burn is the physical mark.  
> Drift is the cognitive cost.

Haruto’s stomach tightened.

The scene slowed.

The anchor paused by a river of aether-light and frowned.

Not in pain.

In confusion.

They looked at their own reflection as if expecting a UI window to float over it.

For a heartbeat, nothing happened.

Then—

a tiny line of text appeared at the edge of their vision.

Not cyan.

Not gold.

Not violet.

Faint neutral white.

Barely there.

> **TIP:** Don’t panic.

Haruto’s chest went cold.

That line didn’t belong to anchors.

That line belonged to hosts.

The anchor blinked hard.

The tip vanished.

They stared at the river, breathing too fast like they were trying to outpace a thought.

The codex labeled the moment with brutal calm.

> **DRIFT EVENT:** UI artifact intrusion

Haruto swallowed.

The scene advanced.

The outsider stumbled—rewrite pressure rising.

The anchor reached out instinctively and steadied their wrist.

The cyan thread brightened.

Overdrive pressure eased.

The world stabilized.

And the anchor’s eyes widened as if something inside their head had been pulled sideways.

This time, the intrusion was stronger.

A small icon flashed at the edge of their vision:

A tiny cyan tab.

Just a flicker.

Then gone.

The anchor whispered, “What is… that?”

The outsider didn’t answer.

Maybe they couldn’t.

Maybe they didn’t want to put it into words and make it proof.

The codex offered a note that made Haruto’s throat tighten.

> **Shared discipline creates shared interface risk.**  
> The system’s language spreads along continuity links.

Spreads.

Like infection.

Like debt.

Like a tax paid in thoughts.

The anchor moved again, faster now, like someone trying to outrun a sensation crawling under their skin.

They muttered under their breath:

“Quest… update?”

Haruto’s stomach dropped.

The words came out wrong in their mouth, like a foreign language forced into a throat that didn’t want it.

The anchor stopped, horrified by themselves.

They pressed a hand to their temple.

The codex labeled the escalation.

> **DRIFT SEVERITY:** increasing  
> Indicator: system vocabulary bleed

Haruto whispered, “So this is the real cost.”

Not dying.

Not burning.

Becoming compatible with the UI.

The anchor looked up at the outsider and said, voice shaking:

“Am I… becoming you?”

The outsider’s shoulders tightened.

They shook their head once.

No.

Not becoming.

But being touched.

The codex text appeared again, smaller.

> **Anchor drift is usually temporary.**  
> It worsens with witness pressure.  
> It worsens with spikes.

Haruto’s chest tightened.

So the more they were seen—

the more the system would imprint itself on the anchor.

And the more the anchor became a target, not for correction, but for *categorization.*

The scene dimmed.

The audit halo rotated overhead, faint but present.

The anchor took a slow breath and forced themselves to speak something else—something human, not system language.

A name.

Not a title.

Not a role.

Just a name, said quietly like a small rebellion.

The intrusive tip at the edge of their vision flickered—

and faded.

The codex displayed its conclusion.

> **Naming resists drift.**  
> System labels accelerate drift.

The vision snapped shut.

Haruto returned to the real world with his cuff warm on his wrist and a new fear tightening his chest:

Two hands could save him.

But the system would try to climb that bridge too.
