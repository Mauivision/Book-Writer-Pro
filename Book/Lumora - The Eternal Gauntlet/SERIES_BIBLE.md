## Series Bible — Lumora: The Eternal Gauntlet

### 1) What is Lumora?
Lumora is a planet where reality runs on visible “game logic.” Quest windows appear in midair. Health bars hover above living beings. Dungeons rebuild themselves like live-service content. Death is a brief respawn with sassy loading-screen commentary.

Long ago, twin gods got bored and “converted” the world into an endless multiplayer dungeon-crawler:
- **Luminos** — God of All Light (hero aesthetics, sanctified rules, radiant branding)
- **Umbrax** — God of All Shadow (loopholes, curses, stylish chaos, dark neon wonder)

The gods watch for entertainment and occasionally push balance changes as **Patch Notes**.

### 2) The System (UI) rules writers can rely on
- **Quest Log**: objective text can update in real time (often at the worst moment).
- **Tutorial Tips**: snarky, sometimes misleading, always comedic.
- **Romance Flags**: hearts and “bond prompts” appear as literal UI. Flirty scenes can trigger buffs *and* danger.
- **Respawn**: death is temporary, but repeated deaths can cause debuffs and faction reputation loss.
- **Route Signatures (Cyan / Verdant)**: rare UI color that signals deeper world protocols (Codex unlocks, “correction” events, and Elara-linked resonance).

### 3) Respawn & Consequences
- **Respawn** occurs at the nearest valid shrine/safe-zone anchor.
- **Penalty** is social + mechanical:
  - **Reputation** loss with Radiant Dominion / Veil Syndicate
  - temporary “embarrassment” debuffs that create comedy and pressure
- **Rule of Fun**: death isn’t gore; it’s slapstick + stakes.

### 4) Factions

#### Radiant Dominion (Surface)
- **Aesthetic**: gleaming marble castles, sky-islands, perpetual golden sunlight
- **Public story**: noble protectors of order
- **Strengths**: healing, shielding, sanctified artifacts, morale buffs
- **Weaknesses**: rigid doctrine, optics, pride, political theater

#### Veil Syndicate (Below)
- **Aesthetic**: haunted crypt cities, fog graveyards, neon-lit ghost arcades, eternal parties
- **Public story**: edgy villains and opportunists
- **Strengths**: debuffs, stealth, curse-tech, unconventional tactics, map tricks
- **Weaknesses**: internal rivalry, improvisational chaos, “pranks as policy”

### 5) Forced Co-op Rule (World Boss Events)
When a world boss spawns, the System issues a mandatory event:
- mixed-faction cooperation is strongly recommended (and humiliatingly announced)
- failure risks zone wipe and rollback-like consequences

### 6) Key Locations (Volume 1 anchors)
- **Castle Lumora** (Radiant capital hub)
  - **Grand Radiant Hall** (summoning chamber)
  - **Radiant Council chambers** (politics + trials)
  - **The Golden Tankard** (Mira’s tavern hub, gossip + buffs + comedy)
- **Veil Crypts** (Veil hub)
  - Bone Legion barracks
  - Ghost Arcade district
  - fog gardens for secret meetings
- **Sky Dungeon #7**
  - floating island dungeon with light/shadow locks
  - designed as a “starter dungeon” that quietly cheats

### 7) Recurring Mechanics that drive scenes
- **Jealousy Aggro**: romantic tension increases spawns and boss aggression.
- **Loot Binding**: certain drops bind to the Player, creating rivalry and plot hooks.
- **Patch Notes**: gods adjust mechanics between arcs/volumes; comedy + escalation.
- **Found Family Buff**: genuine teamwork and honest communication create real power spikes.
- **Corrections**: anti-meta “world immune response” spawns that punish cross-faction synergy when Unity rises too fast.
- **Elf Vow Mechanic (Season 2 / Volume 2)**: a romantic, mechanical bond-rite that stabilizes Overdrive/protocol surges (provisional vow first; true wedding later payoff).

### 8) Series progression (books = level bands)

Lumora is written like a dungeon-crawler campaign: each book covers a **level band** and ends with a **Big Boss** that forces an emotional + mechanical breakthrough.

#### Book 1 — Levels 1–5 (starter band)
- **Core promise**: “new world” + party assembly + the System’s cruelty-by-comedy.
- **Mechanical growth**: stance mastery (Radiant/Veil), Jealousy Aggro awareness, first real Unity spikes.
- **Capstone / Big Boss (end of Book 1, Level 5)**: **World Boss Event — The Correction Warden (Phase Raid)**  
  A world-boss style fight that punishes disunity, weaponizes jealousy, and forces Haruto’s first **Party Unity Override** on-screen.
- **Reward**: bound relic hinting at the Eternal Gauntlet; Haruto becomes publicly unignorable.

#### Book 2 — Levels 5–8 (mid band / “the world pushes back”)
- **Level 5 overlap (by design)**: Book 2 opens with the **aftermath** of Book 1’s boss and the new “balance era” (politics tighten, Corrections spawn more often).
- **Core promise**: Verdant/Cyan route becomes persistent; unity isn’t just powerful—it's provocative to the world.
- **Mechanical growth**: Corrections as recurring threat, deeper route rules, vow/bond mechanics become literal stabilizers.
- **Capstone / Big Boss (end of Book 2, Level 8)**: **Rollback / Correction Sentinel (“Patch Rewind”)**  
  A boss that attempts to “rewind” social progress: it targets bond events, disables synergy, and tries to split the party into faction-aligned versions of themselves.
- **Reward**: **Elf Vow Mechanic** (provisional rite) unlocks “Renewal” pathway; the party proves unity can survive forced resets.
