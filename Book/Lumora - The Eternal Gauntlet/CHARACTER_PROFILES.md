

\## Character Profiles — Lumora: The Eternal Gauntlet

### Core
- **Haruto Takahashi** — *The Eternal Player*
  - **Role**: isekai’d gamer protagonist; reluctant party leader
  - **Skill**: **Party Leader Authority** (cross-faction recruitment + “co-op override”)
  - **Personality**: dry humor, easily flustered, secretly loves the chaos
  - **Series function**: breaks the world’s intended design; forces factions to collaborate
  - **Route flag**: cyan “Verdant” UI signatures appear as Unity rises; hints the world has deeper protocols than gods’ Patch Notes

- **The System (UI / Loading Screen Narrator)**
  - **Role**: petty “GM” voice + popups; comedic timing weaponized
  - **Style**: snarky tips, romance flags, patch notes, reputation warnings

---

## The Twin Gods (Dev-level antagonists)
- **Luminos** — *God of All Light*: hero branding, sanctified rules, dramatic aesthetics.
- **Umbrax** — *God of All Shadow*: loopholes, curses, stylish chaos, “fun” at others’ expense.
- **Patch Herald** — delivers Patch Notes and event announcements; unnervingly cheerful.

---

## Radiant Dominion (Surface)

### Main Heroes (Volume 1 party core)
- **Seraphina** — *Radiant Paladin*
  - **Vibe**: dramatic protector, honorable, secretly tsundere
  - **Comedy trigger**: declarations of purity ruined by accidental reflections, flirty interruptions, and UI popups
  - **Emotional need**: to be chosen as a person, not just “the shield”

- **Luminara** — *Solar Sorceress*
  - **Vibe**: teasing powerhouse, “ara-ara” confidence, always composed (until she isn’t)
  - **Comedy trigger**: weaponized flirting + temperature rises at inconvenient times
  - **Emotional need**: proof she isn’t just a “designed character” in the gods’ game

### Flirty Support (hub + healing + social pressure)
- **Lady Mirabelle “Mira” Heartflame** — *Golden Tankard tavern mistress*
  - **Goal**: make Haruto blush so hard he forgets faction politics
  - **Tools**: “recovery items,” gossip network, shameless confidence
  - **Hidden depth**: refuses to be a scripted hub NPC; wants agency

- **High Priestess Velvet Dawn** — *Radiant cleric*
  - **Goal**: convert the Player to the Radiant Dominion… preferably in private
  - **Tools**: healing, cleansing, political leverage, quiet dominance
  - **Hidden depth**: cares for real, but hates losing control of outcomes

### Radiant Council (recurring political friction)
- **Chancellor Aurex Valebright** — smiling bureaucrat; treats heroes like assets.
- **Arch-Justicar Halvion** — hardliner; calls Haruto an “anomaly.”
- **Dame Solenne Skyward** — military strategist; respects results over doctrine.
- **Treasurer Mirtha Giltveil** — controls upgrades and resources; deals in favors.
- **Oracle-Registrar Ysilde** — records quests/prophecies; notices rollback patterns.

## Veil Syndicate (Below)

### Ghoul Buddies (Volume 1 party core)
- **Bone Legion Commander**
  - **Role**: stoic skeleton warlord; tactics and discipline
  - **Comedy trigger**: claims to hate drama while being wildly dramatic
  - **Soft spot**: respects competence; unexpectedly loyal to the party’s code

- **Chiller Executioner**
  - **Role**: edgy axe specialist; burst damage and “oops” destruction
  - **Comedy trigger**: collateral damage + deadpan apologies
  - **Soft spot**: wants to be valued beyond being “the weapon”

- **Ectoplasm Elemental**
  - **Role**: chaotic hug-blob; utility wildcard; emotional glue
  - **Comedy trigger**: group hugs mid-fight; gets stuck in traps
  - **Soft spot**: pure sincerity; accidentally says the truth that fixes fights

### Veil leadership & scene setters
- **Syndic Madame Nyx Marrowveil** — elegant negotiator; loves leverage and theatrics.
- **Hexwright “Ticker” Vonn** — curse-tech inventor; neon relics and illicit portals.
- **Gravetender Odo** — keeper of shrines/respawn logic; knows the underworld rules.
- **Mist-DJ Kairo Lux** — arcade district kingpin; information flows through parties.

## Verdant Route (new core character / Season 2 catalyst)
- **Elara Sylvandrel** — *Young Elf Warrior (Verdant Interrupt)*
  - **Role**: outsider-to-the-factions sentinel; reframes Radiant/Veil as a limited worldview
  - **Vibe**: quiet competence, disciplined, razor-wit when needed; not impressed by “hero branding”
  - **Signature**: emerald eyes with faint cyan flecks (resonance with the cyan route / gauntlet protocols)
  - **Function**: introduces “corrections,” warns about the Gauntlet awakening, and anchors the romance arc toward an elf vow/rite payoff
  - **Comedy trigger**: says devastatingly honest things that cause the System to panic and everyone else to spiral
  - **Emotional need**: to protect living balance (not faction optics) and choose trust without being forced
