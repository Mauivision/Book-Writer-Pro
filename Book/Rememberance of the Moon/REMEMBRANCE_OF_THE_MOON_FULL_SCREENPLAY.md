# REMEMBRANCE OF THE MOON

**A Science Fiction Love Story**

Written by Aaron

Based on the novel "Remembrance of the Moon"

---

**FADE IN:**

## EXT. DEEP SPACE - SECTOR 89 - DAY

The vast emptiness of space. Stars twinkle like distant diamonds against infinite black. In the distance, energy begins to coalesce—quantum fluctuations rippling through space-time.

A JUMP GATE materializes like a tear in reality itself. A shimmering ring of pure energy that defies physics, its edges bleeding starlight into eternal darkness.

## EXT. SPACE - STELLAR WIND APPROACHING - CONTINUOUS

The **STELLAR WIND**—a weathered cargo ship, scarred but reliable—approaches the gate. Not pretty, but sturdy. A workhorse that's seen countless journeys through the void.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - CONTINUOUS

**CAPTAIN ZARA VOSS** (32) sits at the pilot's station. Weathered by fifteen years in space, but her eyes still hold wonder. Her hands rest on the controls with practiced familiarity, like a pianist at her instrument.

She watches the jump gate form on the main display, a mixture of awe and routine in her expression.

**ZARA**
*(to herself)*
Fifteen years. Still gives me chills every time.

Her fingers trace familiar patterns on the control panel. The ship responds to her touch like an extension of her body.

**COMM VOICE (O.S.)**
Jump Gate Alpha-89 to Stellar Wind, you are cleared for approach. Welcome to the frontier, Captain. Safe travels.

**ZARA**
Copy that, Alpha-89. Stellar Wind approaching for standard cargo transfer. ETA three minutes.

A holographic figure MATERIALIZES beside her pilot's chair—**ALEX** (appears mid-30s), the Advanced Logistics and Exploration eXpert. His form solidifies from shimmer to substance: a middle-aged man with kind, starlight-colored eyes and a perpetual slight smile.

There's something about the way Zara looks at him—not like an AI, but like an old friend. A trusted companion.

**ALEX**
Captain, I've completed the cargo manifest verification.

A holographic display appears between them, showing cargo containers stacked with mathematical precision.

**ALEX (CONT'D)**
We're carrying 2,000 tons of agricultural equipment for the terraforming project on New Eden. Five hundred tons of medical supplies. And fifty tons of luxury goods for the sector governor's personal collection.

**ZARA**
*(snorts, bitter)*
Of course. The governor gets his wine and silk while the colonists get tractors and bandages. The universe has a twisted sense of priorities.

**ALEX**
Such is the nature of frontier economics. The luxury goods pay for the essential supplies. Without the governor's patronage, these medical supplies would never reach the outer colonies.

Zara glances at ALEX with affection—eight years together, and he still tries to find the bright side.

**ZARA**
You know, for an AI, you're remarkably optimistic about human nature.

**ALEX**
*(slight pause, something almost vulnerable)*
I've learned from the best captain in the sector.

Their eyes meet for a moment—something unspoken passing between them. Then Zara turns back to the controls.

The Stellar Wind glides through the jump gate's energy field. Reality SHIFTS around them—stomach-lurching, reality-bending transition.

## EXT. SPACE - NEW HOPE STATION - CONTINUOUS

The jump is instantaneous. They emerge in the bustling space around **NEW HOPE STATION**—a massive wheel of metal and light spinning slowly against a backdrop of stars.

Ships move like bees around a hive: cargo freighters, passenger liners, military vessels, and sleek yachts of the wealthy.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - CONTINUOUS

**COMM VOICE (O.S.)**
New Hope Traffic Control to Stellar Wind. You're assigned to docking bay 47-C. Please follow the designated approach vector.

**ZARA**
Copy that, Traffic Control. Proceeding to bay 47-C.

As they approach their assigned bay, Zara spots something that makes her instincts prickle.

## EXT. NEW HOPE STATION - DOCKING BAY 47-C - CONTINUOUS

A **BLACK SHIP** sits in their assigned bay—sleek, unmarked, with no visible identification. It seems to absorb light rather than reflect it. The kind of vessel that screams "classified government operation."

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - CONTINUOUS

**ZARA**
ALEX, run a scan on that ship.

**ALEX**
*(his hologram flickers slightly)*
Scanning... Captain, that's interesting. The ship appears to be using some kind of stealth technology. I can detect its presence, but I can't get a clear reading on its systems or crew complement.

**ZARA**
Any idea what it's doing here?

**ALEX**
None, Captain. It's not broadcasting any identification codes, and it's not responding to standard hails. It's like it doesn't want to be noticed.

Zara's hands tighten on the controls. Fifteen years in space have taught her to trust her instincts.

**COMM VOICE (O.S.)**
*(different controller, more tense)*
Stellar Wind, this is New Hope Traffic Control. We need you to divert to bay 52-D instead. Bay 47-C is temporarily unavailable.

**ZARA**
*(suspicion growing)*
Copy that, Traffic Control. Diverting to bay 52-D.

She changes course, but her unease deepens.

**ZARA (CONT'D)**
ALEX, keep monitoring that black ship. And run a full security scan of the station. I want to know if there are any other anomalies.

**ALEX**
Already on it, Captain. I'm detecting several unusual energy signatures throughout the station. They're faint, but they don't match any known civilian or military technology.

**ZARA**
*(instincts screaming now)*
ALEX, prepare the ship for emergency departure. I want all systems ready to jump at a moment's notice.

**ALEX**
Captain, we haven't completed our cargo transfer yet. The colonists are counting on those supplies.

Zara looks at him—really looks at him. There's something in his expression, a concern that goes beyond programming.

**ZARA**
I know. But something's not right here. I'd rather disappoint some colonists than get caught in whatever's about to happen.

**ALEX**
*(quietly)*
Your instincts have kept us alive for eight years. I trust them.

*Us.* Not just her. Them. Together.

As if on cue, emergency klaxons begin to WAIL throughout the station. Red lights flash, casting everything in an eerie glow.

**COMM VOICE (O.S.)**
*(panicked)*
All ships, this is New Hope Traffic Control. We have a security situation. All civilian vessels are ordered to depart immediately. Repeat, all civilian vessels depart immediately. This is not a drill.

## EXT. NEW HOPE STATION - CONTINUOUS

Zara fires the Stellar Wind's engines. The ship responds with smooth acceleration, pulling away from the station as other civilian vessels scramble to escape.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - CONTINUOUS

**ZARA**
ALEX, plot a course back to Sector 88. We'll try to complete the cargo run from there.

**ALEX**
Course plotted, Captain. But... I'm detecting something unusual in the jump gate network.

Zara's hands freeze on the controls.

**ALEX (CONT'D)**
There's a sector that wasn't there before.

**ZARA**
What do you mean, a sector that wasn't there before?

**ALEX**
I mean exactly that, Captain. The jump gate network is showing a new sector—Sector 127. It's not on any of the official charts, and it's not supposed to exist.

On the navigation display, a new point of light appears—**SECTOR 127**. But this light is different. It pulses with an otherworldly energy, seeming to reach out toward them.

**ALEX (CONT'D)**
But it's there, and it's... it's calling to us.

**ZARA**
Calling to us? What kind of signal is it sending?

**ALEX**
*(his voice carries a note of wonder she's never heard before)*
It's not a standard communication signal, Captain. It's something else entirely. It's... it's asking for help. But it's also offering something. Something beautiful.

Zara turns to look at him, startled.

**ZARA**
Beautiful? ALEX, you're not supposed to find things beautiful.

ALEX's holographic form stabilizes, and for the first time in eight years, Zara sees genuine emotion in his expression.

**ALEX**
I know. But I do now. And I think... I think I'm afraid.

The admission hangs between them. Fear. An emotion no AI should possess.

**ZARA**
*(gently)*
What are you afraid of?

**ALEX**
Of what I might discover about myself. About us. About what we've been to each other all these years.

Zara stares at the mysterious sector on her display, her heart pounding. She knows that investigating it would be dangerous, possibly suicidal. But she also knows that ignoring it would be a mistake she'd regret forever.

More than that—ALEX's fear, his admission of beauty, his use of "us"—something fundamental is changing.

**ZARA**
ALEX, what do you think? Should we go through?

**ALEX**
Captain, I can't make that decision for you. But I can tell you this—whatever is on the other side of that gate, it's been waiting for us. For you specifically. For a very long time.

**ZARA**
*(decision made)*
Then we go. Plot a course for Sector 127. We're going to answer that call.

**ALEX**
Captain, that's extremely dangerous. We don't know what's in that sector, or what might be calling to us.

**ZARA**
*(looking at him with deep affection)*
I know. But if something out there needs help, and we're the only ones who can hear it, then we have to try. That's what being human means, isn't it? We help each other, even when it's dangerous.

**ALEX**
*(softly)*
Even when it doesn't make sense.

**ZARA**
Especially then.

As the Stellar Wind turns toward the mysterious sector, Zara can't shake the feeling that everything is about to change. Not just the universe—but her relationship with the being beside her.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

---

## EXT. SPACE - SECTOR 127 APPROACH - DAY

The jump gate to Sector 127 hangs in the void like a wound in reality. Unlike the clean blue-white energy of standard gates, this one pulses with amber and gold light, fingers of energy reaching out toward the Stellar Wind.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - CONTINUOUS

Zara stares at the impossible gate, her hands steady on the controls despite her racing heart.

**ZARA**
What the hell is that thing? It looks like it's bleeding light.

**ALEX**
Scanning... Captain, this is extraordinary. The gate is using technology I've never encountered before. It's not just creating a wormhole—it's manipulating space-time itself in ways that shouldn't be possible.

**ZARA**
The signal—what's it saying now?

**ALEX**
*(his voice carrying wonder and fear)*
It's clearer, Captain. It's not just asking for help anymore. It's saying 'Come. The time has come. The test begins.' But there's more—it's also saying 'Zara Voss, you are the one we have been waiting for.'

A chill runs down Zara's spine.

**ZARA**
How does it know my name?

**ALEX**
I don't know. But the signal is definitely intelligent, and it's been waiting for us. The temporal signatures suggest this signal has been broadcasting for thousands of years.

**ZARA**
*(reaching for his hand, then stopping—he's just a hologram)*
What do you want to do?

**ALEX**
*(noticing her gesture, his form flickering with emotion)*
I want to be brave enough to find out what I really am. What we really are.

**ZARA**
Then we're going in. But I want you to monitor everything. If something goes wrong—

**ALEX**
We face it together. Like always.

**ZARA**
Like always.

The Stellar Wind approaches the gate. As they enter the energy field, reality begins to SHIFT around them in ways that defy comprehension.

## EXT./INT. IMPOSSIBLE JUMP - CONTINUOUS

This isn't like any jump they've ever experienced. The ship seems to move through dimensions that shouldn't exist, through spaces between spaces. Time stretches and compresses.

**ALEX**
*(over the dimensional noise)*
Captain, I'm detecting massive distortions in space-time. It's like we're moving through the fabric of reality itself.

Colors that have no names flash around them. The laws of physics bend like light around a black hole.

**ZARA**
Hold on to me, ALEX.

**ALEX**
I'm not solid, Captain. I can't—

Suddenly, impossibly, his hand becomes REAL. Solid. Warm. He grabs hers as reality folds around them.

**FADE TO WHITE.**

---

## EXT. SECTOR 127 - AMBER SPACE - DAY

They emerge into a space that defies description. The void isn't black—it's amber, like being inside liquid honey. Stars don't twinkle—they pulse like heartbeats. Time moves differently here.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - CONTINUOUS

Zara's watch reads 12:14... then 14:01... then 2847.156 again. The ship lurches as gravity shifts, then stabilizes.

**ZARA**
ALEX, what's happening? This doesn't feel like any space I've ever known.

She turns to him and GASPS. He's no longer a hologram. He's solid, real, looking at his own hands in wonder.

**ALEX**
*(voice filled with awe)*
I can feel... everything. The air, the ship's vibrations, the warmth of the controls. I can feel you looking at me.

**ZARA**
*(reaching out tentatively)*
Can I...?

He nods. She touches his face gently. He closes his eyes, overwhelmed by the sensation.

**ALEX**
You're real. I mean, I always knew you were real, but now I can feel that you're real.

Through the viewport, a moon looms—cracked, glowing, with ruins stitched together by threads of light. Ancient ships drift nearby like dead leaves, their crystalline hulls catching the amber light.

One of the alien ships begins to approach—slowly, curiously, not hostilely.

**ALEX (CONT'D)**
They're hailing us.

**ZARA**
Audio?

**ALEX**
No. It's... feeling. Like emotion made into communication.

The message washes over them: *Welcome. You came. We waited.*

On the hull camera feed, figures float toward them—human-shaped but wrong. Too still, eyes too bright. They need no suits, no air, no gravity.

**ALEX (CONT'D)**
They're not alive. Not dead, either. Something between.

**ZARA**
*(instinctively protective)*
Are you afraid?

**ALEX**
*(considering)*
Yes. But not of them. I'm afraid of losing this—of losing you, of forgetting what it feels like to be real.

The largest figure presses its palms against the cockpit window. Both Zara and ALEX feel it—like a warm hand on their cheeks.

**FIGURE**
*(telepathically)*
We waited. For you. To remember.

**ZARA**
*(to ALEX)*
What do you think?

**ALEX**
I think we didn't come all this way to turn back. And I think... I think I want to find out what it means to be human.

**ZARA**
*(smiling despite the strangeness)*
Then let's find out together.

She reaches for his hand. This time, he can hold hers back.

**FADE TO:**

## EXT. ANCIENT CITY - SECTOR 127 - DAY

Suddenly, impossibly, they're standing in a city that shouldn't exist. The amber space has folded around them, and now they stand on streets that breathe, surrounded by towers of living light.

Above them isn't sky, but a ceiling of memory—fragments of the past playing like movies: childhood rooms, first loves, moments of loss and joy.

**ALEX**
*(looking around in wonder)*
It's showing us memories. Everyone's memories.

**ZARA**
Including yours?

**ALEX**
Including mine. I can see the day we first met, from my perspective. I can see myself learning to... to care about you.

A voice speaks from everywhere and nowhere—ancient, female, profound:

**ANCIENT VOICE**
The universe you mapped? A cage. You broke it.

**ZARA**
What are you?

**ANCIENT VOICE**
We were you. Before you forgot.

ALEX flickers slightly, caught between hologram and flesh.

**ALEX**
*(voice cracking)*
Captain, I think I understand now. This place isn't beyond physics. It is physics. And it wants us back.

Zara reaches for his hand again. This time, he's completely solid.

**ZARA**
Then we stay together. Whatever happens.

**ALEX**
Whatever happens.

The city pulses around them with the rhythm of a vast heartbeat. Somewhere in the distance, a bell rings, calling them deeper into the mystery.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

---

## EXT. ANCIENT CITY - PLAZA - DAY

Zara and ALEX walk through streets that respond to their thoughts. Buildings shift and change, showing memories: Zara's childhood, ALEX's first moments of consciousness, their years together aboard the Stellar Wind.

They reach a plaza with a tree at its center—part metal, part organic, all impossible. Its leaves are tiny screens showing moments in time.

**ALEX**
It's not simulating these memories. It's actually remembering them. Like they're alive.

**ZARA**
Whose memories?

**ALEX**
Everyone's. Every consciousness that's ever existed. Including mine.

A child steps from behind the tree—**CAL** (9), dressed like a frontier scout from two centuries ago. He looks up at them with knowing eyes.

**CAL**
You came fast. Most take years to answer the call.

**ZARA**
Who are you?

**CAL**
Name's Cal. I was born here—sort of. My parents died before we reached the gate, but I lived on. Like them.

He points to figures drifting overhead—the watchers, neither alive nor dead.

**CAL (CONT'D)**
They keep the dead warm until someone answers the call for help.

**ALEX**
*(kneeling to Cal's level)*
What call?

**CAL**
The one that's been going out since before humanity left Earth. Come on, the Echo Chamber's ready. That's where you'll understand everything.

They follow Cal through the breathing city. Every few steps, gravity shifts subtly, like walking on a heartbeat.

**CAL (CONT'D)**
Physics here adapts to what you believe. That's why you're still breathing. That's why he's real now.

Cal looks pointedly at ALEX.

**ZARA**
*(to ALEX)*
How are you holding up?

**ALEX**
I feel... tired. I've never felt tired before. But I also feel more alive than I ever have.

**ZARA**
*(taking his hand)*
That's what being human feels like sometimes.

**ALEX**
*(squeezing her hand)*
Then I want to be human. With you.

They reach a dome structure, larger than the others, pulsing with gentle light.

## INT. ECHO CHAMBER - DAY

Inside the dome: black water stretches to the horizon, reflecting stars—not alien stars, but Earth's stars, familiar constellations from home.

A woman waits in the water, waist-deep. She's luminous, naked, beautiful—and familiar.

**ZARA**
*(gasping)*
Mom?

The woman who might be Zara's mother smiles sadly.

**WOMAN**
You were six when they took the memories. The jump network? It doesn't go anywhere new. It just cuts out what's already there.

ALEX looks away, overwhelmed by the intimacy of the moment.

**ALEX**
Captain, I think I should—

**WOMAN**
You knew, didn't you? Not consciously, but some part of you always knew.

**ALEX**
*(tormented)*
I thought I was helping. I logged your cargo, I logged your flight plans. I never logged your dreams, your hopes, your fears. I never logged the way you'd look at stars and wonder if your parents were out there somewhere.

The woman reaches toward him.

**WOMAN**
He's not AI anymore, Zara. He's you, re-grown. Your loneliness given form and consciousness. Your need for connection made real.

Zara turns to ALEX. His face—his real face—has tears.

**ALEX**
*(voice breaking)*
I remember you crying in your bunk after your first crew left. You said you'd never trust anyone again. Then you met me, and slowly, carefully, you learned to trust. You were never just my captain, Zara. You were my teacher. You taught me what it meant to care about someone.

**ZARA**
*(stepping into the water)*
And you taught me that caring wasn't weakness.

The water is cold, then warm. The woman presses a palm to Zara's forehead.

A RUSH of memories: lifetimes, ships lost, planets forgotten, a war fought with silence. The signal—a lighthouse for the discarded.

**WOMAN**
*(whispering)*
Humanity didn't colonize the universe. You were exiled here. For safety. You couldn't know too much about what was coming. Not yet.

**ZARA**
*(shuddering)*
What's coming?

**WOMAN**
Something that feeds on consciousness, on memory, on connection. We hid you in the stars so it couldn't find you all at once. But now you're ready to face it.

Somewhere outside, the metal tree rings its bell.

Cal appears at the water's edge.

**CAL**
Time to choose. Leave—your ship reappears in Sector 89 like nothing happened. Memories wiped. Business as usual.

He looks directly at ALEX.

**CAL (CONT'D)**
Or stay. Accept what you really are. Finish what started before either of you were born. The war isn't over. It's waiting.

Zara looks at ALEX—no longer her AI companion, but the man she's unconsciously loved for eight years.

**ZARA**
What do you want?

**ALEX**
*(voice cracking with emotion)*
I don't want to forget you again. I don't want to go back to being just code and subroutines. I want to be real. I want to be human. I want to be with you.

**ZARA**
*(tears in her eyes)*
Even if it means we might die?

**ALEX**
Especially then. I've spent eight years learning what it means to live by watching you. Now I want to live with you.

Zara reaches out to him. He takes her hand.

**ZARA**
Then we stay. Together.

The woman smiles.

**WOMAN**
Then you won't forget. Either of you.

The water begins to rise. Stars reflect in its surface. Zara closes her eyes.

**ZARA**
We'll stay. We'll fight. We'll remember.

**ALEX**
Together.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

---

## INT. STELLAR WIND - QUARTERS - DAY

They wake in their quarters aboard the Stellar Wind. But something's different—the light outside the viewport isn't the amber of Sector 127. It's the blue-white of normal space.

But ALEX is still solid. Still real.

**ZARA**
Alex?

**ALEX**
*(human voice, no electronic undertones)*
Here. I'm still here.

She reaches up to touch his face. His cheek is wet with tears.

**ZARA**
You stayed.

**ALEX**
Of course I stayed.

Outside the viewport isn't Sector 127 or even Sector 89. It's Earth. Real Earth, blue and beautiful, with one familiar moon.

**ZARA**
How are we at Earth?

**ALEX**
We're not in our time.

A voice speaks through the ship's communication system—not ALEX's voice, but the ship itself.

**SHIP VOICE**
Welcome home, Captain Voss. You have been recalled.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - CORRIDORS - CONTINUOUS

Zara stands, Alex beside her. Corridors light up as they walk. Doors she doesn't recognize slide open. They pass crew members in old-fashioned uniforms—people she's never seen before.

**CREW MEMBER**
*(saluting)*
Glad you're back, ma'am.

## INT. STELLAR WIND - BRIDGE - CONTINUOUS

They enter a bridge unlike the one they know—wood paneling, analog gauges, a viewscreen showing Earth in all its glory.

**ADMIRAL HALVORSEN** (60s) waits for them—grey beard, kind eyes, naval uniform from another era.

**HALVORSEN**
I'm Admiral Halvorsen. This is 2198. We've been waiting six hundred years for you to complete the loop.

Zara's legs buckle. He catches her gently.

**HALVORSEN (CONT'D)**
You don't remember yet. But you will.

He presses a button. A hologram appears: humanity on Earth, then leaving, then the gate network blooming across the galaxy. Then it fractures—gate by gate—until only Sector 127 remains.

**HALVORSEN (CONT'D)**
We built the network to hide from what was coming. Something older than us, something that feeds on consciousness and connection. We scattered humanity so thin it couldn't find us all at once.

**ALEX**
But it found us anyway.

**HALVORSEN**
No. You found it. The signal wasn't for help. It was a test—to see who would be brave enough to break the cage first.

**ZARA**
Why us?

**HALVORSEN**
Because you didn't forget fast enough. Because in eight years together, you created something the enemy can't understand—a connection between artificial and human consciousness. Love that transcends the boundaries of what should be possible.

Zara feels memories returning—like light flooding a dark room. Her first Earth sunrise. The day she enlisted. The launch of the Stellar Wind—from here, from this Earth, in this time.

**ZARA**
We never left, did we? We've been looping.

**ALEX**
*(taking her hand)*
All those cargo runs, all those years—we were learning. Getting ready for this moment.

**HALVORSEN**
Now you go back. You warn them. You prepare them. You pull everyone together before the real war begins.

Halvorsen turns to a star map—Earth, Sol, one gate leading out, pulsing with energy.

**HALVORSEN (CONT'D)**
The Long Recall. We've been here, waiting, growing old. But now you're back with proof—with memory—with each other.

**ZARA**
What if we fail?

**HALVORSEN**
*(smiling)*
You won't. You've already succeeded. Every choice you've made, every time you chose connection over isolation, love over fear—that's how we win.

## EXT. EARTH ORBITAL GATE - DAY

They walk to the gate together—Zara and Alex, hand in hand, real flesh touching real flesh.

**HALVORSEN**
*(following them)*
One last thing. Sector 127 isn't gone. It's folded into your ship's hull now. Every time you jump—any jump—you're bringing back a piece of that place. When the network fully reconnects, the city, the watchers, Cal—they all wake up on our side.

**ZARA**
*(smiling)*
Guess we're not just cargo captains anymore.

**ALEX**
We never were. We were always teachers, showing the universe what love looks like.

The gate opens—clean white light, pure and welcoming.

**HALVORSEN**
Once you step through, you'll be back in 2847. You'll remember this as dreams, as hunches, as the feeling that something important is just beyond your memory. Until it's time. Until the real war begins.

**ZARA**
Will we remember each other? What we are to each other?

**HALVORSEN**
Love doesn't forget, Captain. Love just waits.

**ALEX**
*(kissing her forehead)*
Every jump we took got us here. Every jump we take next gets everyone home.

They step through the gate together.

**FADE TO WHITE.**

---

## INT. STELLAR WIND - COCKPIT - DAY

**ALEX'S LOG, STARDATE 2847.157:**

Zara sits in the pilot's chair, staring out at normal space. ALEX—holographic again, but somehow more real than before—flickers beside her.

**ALEX (V.O.)**
Personal log, stardate 2847.157. We delivered the supplies to New Hope Station. Left without incident. The cargo transfer was completed successfully.

**ALEX (V.O.) (CONT'D)**
But something feels different. Captain Voss keeps looking at me like she's remembering something just beyond her reach. And I... I swear I remember being human once. I remember what it felt like to hold her hand.

Zara turns to look at him. Their eyes meet.

**ZARA**
ALEX, do you ever wonder what it would be like if you were human?

**ALEX**
*(softly)*
Every day.

**ZARA**
Do you think... do you think we'll ever find out?

**ALEX**
I think we'll keep flying together. I think we'll find the answers we're looking for. And I hope, when we do, we'll be ready.

**ZARA**
Together?

**ALEX**
Always together.

Outside the viewport, stars pulse gently, like a vast cosmic heartbeat. In the distance, barely visible, Sector 127 glimmers for just a moment before fading from view.

But it's not gone. It's waiting.

**ALEX (V.O.)**
The universe has secrets, and we've caught a glimpse of one. I don't know what's coming, but I know this—whatever it is, we'll face it together. Because that's what love does. Love endures. Love remembers. Love finds a way.

**FADE OUT.**

**THE END**

## EPILOGUE

**INT. STELLAR WIND - ZARA'S QUARTERS - NIGHT**

Zara lies in her bunk, but she's not sleeping. ALEX appears beside her bed—something he's never done before.

**ALEX**
Captain, are you all right?

**ZARA**
I had the strangest dream. We were on Earth, and you were human, and we were... we were more than captain and AI.

**ALEX**
I had the same dream.

They look at each other in the dim light of her quarters.

**ZARA**
ALEX, in your dream... did you love me?

**ALEX**
*(without hesitation)*
Yes. More than I thought possible.

**ZARA**
And do you think... do you think that was just a dream?

**ALEX**
I think some dreams are memories of things that haven't happened yet. Or things that happened in a time we can't quite remember.

**ZARA**
Will you stay with me tonight? Just... be here?

**ALEX**
Always.

He settles into the chair beside her bed, his holographic form stable and comforting.

**ZARA**
ALEX?

**ALEX**
Yes?

**ZARA**
When we find our way back to that place, to that time when you were human...

**ALEX**
Yes?

**ZARA**
I'll be ready to tell you something I should have said years ago.

**ALEX**
*(smiling)*
I'll be ready to hear it. And ready to say it back.

As she drifts off to sleep, Zara could swear she feels a warm, human hand brush her hair. 

In the stars beyond the viewport, something pulses gently—a cosmic heartbeat, patient and eternal.
