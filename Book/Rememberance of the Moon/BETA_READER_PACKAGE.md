## **📖 About the Book**

**Title**: Remembrance of the Moon
**Genre**: Science Fiction / Space Opera
**Word Count**: 18,000+ words
**Estimated Reading Time**: 75-90 minutes
**Target Audience**: Science fiction enthusiasts, philosophical readers, fans of space opera

### **Synopsis**

Captain Zara Voss discovers a mysterious signal from Sector 127—a sector that shouldn't exist. What begins as a routine cargo run becomes a journey that will change the universe forever. Along with her AI companion ALEX, she answers the call and finds herself in a place where the laws of physics don't apply, where ancient beings test humanity's worthiness, and where the fate of all existence hangs in the balance.

## **📋 Beta Reader Guidelines**

### **What We're Looking For**

**Story & Plot**

- Does the story flow well?
- Are there any plot holes or inconsistencies?
- Is the pacing appropriate?
- Are the plot twists surprising but logical?

**Characters**

- Are the characters believable and engaging?
- Do they have clear motivations and goals?
- Do they grow and change throughout the story?
- Are their relationships authentic?

**World-Building**

- Is the world consistent and believable?
- Are the sci-fi elements well-explained?
- Does the technology make sense?
- Is the setting immersive?

**Writing Quality**

- Is the prose clear and engaging?
- Are there any awkward sentences or phrases?
- Is the dialogue natural and character-appropriate?
- Are there any grammatical or spelling errors?

**Themes**

- Are the themes clear and well-developed?
- Do they resonate with you?
- Are they relevant and meaningful?
- Do they enhance the story?

### **What We're NOT Looking For**

- **Line editing** (grammar, spelling, punctuation)
- **Major plot changes** (unless there are significant issues)
- **Personal preferences** that don't affect the story quality
- **Comparisons to other books** (unless relevant to quality)
