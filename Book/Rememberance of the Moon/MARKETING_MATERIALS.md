Captain Zara Voss thought she was making a routine cargo run. But when a mysterious signal calls from Sector 127—a sector that shouldn't exist—she faces a choice that will change the universe forever.

Along with her AI companion ALEX, Zara discovers a place where the laws of physics don't apply, where ancient beings test humanity's worthiness, and where the fate of all existence hangs in the balance.

As she and her team face trials of cooperation, wisdom, and sacrifice, they must choose between dominion and consent, between control and cooperation, between the safety of the known and the infinite possibilities of the unknown.

In the end, they don't just discover the Heartline—a living network that connects all things—they give birth to it, creating a new kind of consciousness that will change the universe forever.

**A science fiction novel that explores the deepest questions of existence: What does it mean to be conscious? What is the nature of power and responsibility? How do we balance individual choice with collective good?**
