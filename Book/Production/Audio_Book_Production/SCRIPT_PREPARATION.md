# **SCRIPT PREPARATION**

## **Remembrance of the Moon - Audio Book**

---

## **SCRIPT ADAPTATION OVERVIEW**

This guide outlines how to adapt the written text of "Remembrance of the Moon" into a format optimized for audio book production. The adaptation focuses on enhancing the listening experience while maintaining the story's integrity.

---

## **ADAPTATION PRINCIPLES**

### **Audio-First Approach**

- **Clarity**: Ensure all dialogue and narration is clear
- **Pacing**: Optimize rhythm for listening experience
- **Character Voices**: Enhance character differentiation
- **Sound Cues**: Add audio-specific directions
- **Navigation**: Include chapter markers and transitions

### **Maintaining Story Integrity**

- **Preserve Content**: Keep all original text and meaning
- **Enhance Experience**: Improve without changing story
- **Character Development**: Strengthen character arcs
- **Emotional Beats**: Highlight key emotional moments
- **Thematic Elements**: Emphasize core themes

---

## **SCRIPT FORMAT**

### **Standard Format**

```
[CHAPTER MARKER]
Chapter [Number]: [Title]

[NARRATOR]
[Text content with audio directions]

[CHARACTER NAME]
[Character dialogue with voice directions]

[SOUND EFFECT]
[Sound effect description and timing]

[MUSIC CUE]
[Music direction and timing]
```

```

[NARRATOR - Deep, commanding voice]

[SOUND EFFECT - Jump gate energy building]
```
