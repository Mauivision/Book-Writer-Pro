## **PLATFORM SELECTION**

### **Primary Platforms**

#### **Audible (Amazon)**

- **Market Share**: 60% of audio book market
- **Revenue Split**: 25% to author (exclusive), 40% (non-exclusive)
- **Requirements**: MP3 format, 64kbps (mono) or 128kbps (stereo)
- **Benefits**: Largest audience, professional platform
- **Strategy**: Launch exclusive for 90 days, then expand

#### **iTunes/Apple Books**

- **Market Share**: 15% of audio book market
- **Revenue Split**: 70% to author
- **Requirements**: AAC format, 128kbps or higher
- **Benefits**: High-quality platform, Apple ecosystem
- **Strategy**: Launch simultaneously with Audible

#### **Spotify**

- **Market Share**: Growing rapidly
- **Revenue Split**: Variable based on streams
- **Requirements**: MP3 or AAC, 128kbps or higher
- **Benefits**: Music streaming audience, discovery features
- **Strategy**: Launch after exclusive period

### **Secondary Platforms**

#### **Google Play Books**

- **Market Share**: 5% of audio book market
- **Revenue Split**: 70% to author
- **Requirements**: MP3 or AAC format
- **Benefits**: Android ecosystem, Google integration
- **Strategy**: Launch with secondary platforms

#### **Kobo**

- **Market Share**: 3% of audio book market
- **Revenue Split**: 70% to author
- **Requirements**: MP3 or AAC format
- **Benefits**: International reach, indie-friendly
- **Strategy**: Launch with secondary platforms

#### **Libro.fm**

- **Market Share**: 2% of audio book market
- **Revenue Split**: 50% to author, 50% to local bookstores
- **Requirements**: MP3 or AAC format
- **Benefits**: Supports local bookstores, indie-friendly
- **Strategy**: Launch with secondary platforms
