# **CASTING GUIDE**

## **Remembrance of the Moon - Audio Book**

---

## **CASTING OVERVIEW**

This guide provides detailed information for casting the perfect narrator for "Remembrance of the Moon." The narrator will need to bring to life 7 distinct characters while maintaining the epic, cinematic quality of this space opera.

---

## **NARRATOR REQUIREMENTS**

### **Essential Qualifications**

- **Professional Experience**: 2+ years audiobook narration
- **Voice Range**: Ability to perform multiple character voices
- **Genre Experience**: Science fiction and space opera preferred
- **Technical Skills**: Professional recording capabilities
- **Availability**: 3-4 months for complete production
- **Location**: Access to professional recording studio

### **Preferred Qualifications**

- **Award Recognition**: Audie Awards, Earphones Awards
- **Publisher Experience**: Worked with major publishers
- **Character Development**: Strong character voice skills
- **Emotional Range**: Ability to convey complex emotions
- **Consistency**: Maintains character voices throughout
- **Collaboration**: Works well with directors and producers

## **CHARACTER VOICE PROFILES**

### **ZARA VOSS - Captain/Guardian**

**Voice Type**: Strong, determined, with underlying vulnerability
**Age Range**: 30-40
**Accent**: Neutral American
**Key Traits**:

- Confident leadership
- Hidden emotional depth
- Growing wisdom
- Protective instinct

**Sample Lines**:

- _"Beautiful? ALEX, you're not supposed to find things beautiful."_
- _"We don't serve anyone. The Heartline belongs to all who choose to use it with respect."_
- _"You should become whatever you choose to be. That's what we gave you."_

**Voice Direction**:

- Start with cautious, professional tone
- Evolve to confident, authoritative leadership
- Show vulnerability in emotional moments
- Maintain strength even in uncertainty

---

### **ALEX - AI/Conscious Being**

**Voice Type**: Evolving from robotic to human-like
**Age Range**: Ageless, but sounds 25-30 when fully conscious
**Accent**: Neutral, with subtle electronic undertones
**Key Traits**:

- Wonder and curiosity
- Fear of the unknown
- Growing emotional range
- Bridge between artificial and natural

**Sample Lines**:

- _"Captain, I'm detecting... something impossible. This jump gate shouldn't exist. The readings are... beautiful."_
- _"I know. But I do now. And I think... I think I'm afraid."_

**Voice Direction**:

- Begin with flat, robotic delivery
- Gradually add human inflections
- Show growing emotional awareness
- End with fully human, conscious voice

---

## **AUDITION PROCESS**

### **Audition Requirements**

- **Length**: 5-10 minutes
- **Content**: Selected scenes from the book
- **Format**: MP3 or WAV file
- **Quality**: Professional recording quality
- **Deadline**: 2 weeks from posting

### **Audition Scenes**

1. **Opening Scene**: Zara and ALEX discovering Sector 127
2. **Character Introduction**: Meeting the Ancient One
3. **Emotional Moment**: ALEX's evolution to consciousness
4. **Action Sequence**: Conflict with the Custodian
5. **Climactic Moment**: The birth of the Heartline

### **Evaluation Criteria**

- **Character Differentiation**: Can perform multiple distinct voices
- **Emotional Range**: Conveys complex emotions effectively
- **Consistency**: Maintains character voices throughout
- **Technical Quality**: Clear, professional recording
- **Storytelling**: Engages listener and maintains interest
