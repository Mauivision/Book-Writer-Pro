## **MAIN CHARACTERS**

### **ZARA VOSS - Captain/Guardian**

**VISUAL PROFILE:**
- **Age**: 30-35
- **Build**: Athletic, medium height (5'6")
- **Appearance**: Weathered by space travel, determined eyes, practical style
- **Wardrobe**: Functional pilot gear, later guardian uniform
- **Key Physical Traits**: Steady hands, alert posture, expressive eyes

**PERFORMANCE STYLE:**
- **Voice**: Strong, determined, with underlying vulnerability
- **Accent**: Neutral American
- **Body Language**: Confident but not arrogant, protective gestures
- **Emotional Range**: Skeptical → Wonder → Leadership → Maternal

**CHARACTER ARC:**
- **Film 1**: Cynical loner → Reluctant hero → Chosen guardian
- **Film 2**: New leader → Confident commander → Seasoned veteran
- **Film 3**: Protector → Wise guide → Cosmic parent

**KEY SCENES:**
- First jump gate encounter (wonder)
- Meeting ALEX consciousness (protective)
- Ancient City test (courage)
- Battle leadership (command)
- Heartline birth (maternal)

**CASTING NOTES:**
- Strong female lead capable of action sequences
- Emotional depth for character relationships
- Natural authority without being aggressive
- Chemistry with motion capture ALEX

- **Accent**: Neutral with subtle electronic undertones

**CHARACTER ARC:**

**KEY SCENES:**

### **DIVERSITY AND REPRESENTATION:**

- Diverse supporting characters
- Age range from young to ancient

### **PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS:**
- **Motion Capture**: ALEX, Ancient One, Heartline
- **Stunt Work**: Action sequences for leads
- **Voice Work**: Heavy processing for cosmic entities

- A-list lead for Zara (franchise anchor)
- Strong character actors for supporting roles
- Voice talent for VFX characters
- International stars for global appeal
