## **RECORDING SPECIFICATIONS**

### **Studio Requirements**:

- **Acoustic Treatment**: Professional soundproof booth
- **Microphone**: Large diaphragm condenser (Neumann U87 or equivalent)
- **Audio Interface**: Professional grade (Apogee, RME, or equivalent)
- **Monitoring**: High-quality studio monitors
- **Software**: Pro Tools, Logic Pro, or equivalent

### **Recording Standards**:

- **Sample Rate**: 48kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 24-bit
- **File Format**: WAV
- **Room Tone**: Record 30 seconds of silence for each session
- **Backup**: Multiple backup copies of all recordings
