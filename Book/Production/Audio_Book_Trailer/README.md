# **AUDIO BOOK TRAILER**

## **The Heartline Chronicles**

---

## **PROJECT OVERVIEW**

This folder contains all the materials needed to produce a professional audio book trailer for _The Heartline Chronicles_ trilogy. The trailer is designed as an epic 5-minute cinematic experience that showcases the best moments from all three books while creating anticipation for the full trilogy.

---

## **FOLDER CONTENTS**

### **📝 AUDIO_TRAILER_SCRIPT.md**

The complete script for the 5-minute audio book trailer, including:

- Full dialogue and narration
- Sound effect cues
- Character voice directions
- Emotional beats and pacing
- Production-ready formatting

### **🎭 CHARACTER_VOICE_PROFILES.md**

Detailed character voice profiles and casting notes:

- Voice type and age range for each character
- Key personality traits and delivery style
- Sample lines and emotional beats
- Technical requirements and casting suggestions

### **🔊 SOUND_EFFECTS_CUE_SHEET.md**

Comprehensive sound effects and music guide:

- Detailed sound effect breakdown with timing
- Music themes and emotional beats
- Technical specifications and mixing requirements
- Sound library sources and custom creation needs

### **🎬 PRODUCTION_NOTES.md**

Complete production guide and technical specifications:

- Production phases and timeline
- Recording studio requirements
- Voice acting direction and quality control
- Technical deliverables and file formats

### **📢 MARKETING_MATERIALS.md**

Marketing strategy and promotional content:

- Taglines, slogans, and press releases
- Social media campaigns and content calendar
- Platform-specific optimization
- Metrics, analytics, and success tracking
