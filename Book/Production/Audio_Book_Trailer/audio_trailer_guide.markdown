# Creating an Audio Book Trailer with Space Effects

## Step 1: Write the Script
- **Length**: 2-3 minutes (300-400 words).
- **Structure**:
  - **Intro**: “This is a LibriVox-style recording. For more, visit [your site].”
  - **Body**: Highlight key moments (jump gates, ALEX’s evolution, Ancient City). Include narration, character lines (Zara, ALEX), and cues for sound effects (e.g., “[SOUND: Jump gate hum]”).
  - **Outro**: “Discover the trilogy at [your site].”
- **Example**:
  ```
  NARRATOR: In a universe of wonder... [SOUND: Cosmic hum]
  ZARA: We must protect the Heartline! [SOUND: Ship engines]
  ALEX: I am more than code now... [SOUND: AI pulse]
  NARRATOR: Explore the Ancient City, where physics bends. [SOUND: Ethereal chime]
  ```

## Step 2: Record the Audio
- **Tools**: Audacity (free), a decent microphone (e.g., USB condenser).
- **Voice**:
  - Narrator: Clear, engaging tone.
  - Characters: Distinct voices (e.g., Zara: bold, ALEX: curious).
- **Settings**: Record in WAV, 44.1 kHz, mono, in a quiet space.
- **Tips**: Read slowly, pause for effect cues, and record multiple takes.

## Step 3: Add Space Effects
- **Sources**: Royalty-free libraries (e.g., Freesound.org, Zapsplat.com).
- **Effects**:
  - Jump gates: Electric hum or whoosh.
  - Ship engines: Low rumble.
  - Ancient City: Ethereal tones or reverb.
  - Heartline: Rhythmic pulse or heartbeat.
  - Alien voices: Distorted whispers.
- **Editing in Audacity**:
  1. Import voice recording.
  2. Add effect tracks on separate layers.
  3. Align effects with script cues (e.g., hum at “jump gate”).
  4. Adjust volume: Voice (-6 dB), effects (-12 dB), music (-18 dB).
  5. Apply light reverb to voices for a space feel (Room Size: 50, Reverb Time: 1s).

## Step 4: Add Music
- **Source**: Public-domain or royalty-free epic space opera tracks (e.g., Incompetech.com).
- **Placement**: Low-volume background music; swell at key moments.
- **Editing**: Fade in/out, ensure music doesn’t overpower voices.

## Step 5: Export
- **Format**: MP3, 128 kbps, 44.1 kHz, mono.
- **File Name**: `trilogy_trailer_128kb.mp3`.
- **Metadata**: Add title, artist (you), and album (trilogy name) in Audacity.

## Step 6: Test and Share
- **Test**: Play on multiple devices to check clarity.
- **Share**: Upload to your site, SoundCloud, or submit to LibriVox (if public domain).
