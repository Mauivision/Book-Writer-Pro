# WEIGHTLESS LOVE - 7-DAY FREE ACTION PLAN

## 🎯 **START FINISHING YOUR BOOK PROJECT TODAY**

### **📅 DAY 1: SOCIAL MEDIA SETUP (2-3 hours)**

#### **🎯 Morning: Account Creation**
1. **Create Author Accounts**:
   - Instagram: @WeightlessLoveAuthor
   - Twitter/X: @WeightlessLoveSciFi
   - Facebook Page: "Weightless Love Author"
   - TikTok: @WeightlessLoveBooks

2. **Profile Optimization**:
   - Use your book cover or author photo
   - Bio: "Sci-fi romance author | Zero gravity love stories | Debut novel coming soon! 🚀💫"
   - Link: Point to a free Linktree or Carrd page

#### **🌐 Afternoon: Free Website**
1. **WordPress.com Setup**:
   - Choose free "Author" theme
   - Basic pages: Home, About, Books, Contact
   - Add email signup form (Mailchimp free)

2. **Content Upload**:
   - Upload your cover art
   - Add book description
   - Create "Coming Soon" page

#### **📧 Evening: Email List**
1. **Mailchimp Setup** (free tier):
   - Create account
   - Design signup form
   - Embed on website and social media

**🎉 Day 1 Goal**: Professional online presence established

---

### **📅 DAY 2: CONTENT CREATION (3-4 hours)**

#### **📝 Morning: Social Media Posts**
1. **Create 14 Posts** (2 per platform x 7 days):
   - Character introductions
   - Setting descriptions
   - Writing process updates
   - Teaser quotes
   - Behind-the-scenes facts

2. **Visual Content** (Canva free):
   - Quote graphics
   - Character mood boards
   - Simple book teasers

#### **🎥 Afternoon: Video Content**
1. **TikTok/Instagram Reels**:
   - 15-second book pitch
   - Character voiceovers
   - Writing desk tour
   - "Day in the life" snippets

#### **📚 Evening: Blog Content**
1. **WordPress Posts**:
   - "How I Created Zero Gravity Romance"
   - "Alien Biology in Sci-Fi"
   - "My Writing Journey"
   - "Book Cover Design Process"

**📊 Day 2 Goal**: 2 weeks of social media content ready

---

### **📅 DAY 3: COMMUNITY OUTREACH (2-3 hours)**

#### **🌐 Morning: Writing Communities**
1. **Reddit Join & Post**:
   - Join r/BetaReaders, r/SelfPublishing, r/SciFi
   - Introduce yourself: "Sci-fi romance author seeking beta readers"
   - Share your blurb and request feedback

2. **Facebook Groups**:
   - Join 5-10 writing and reading groups
   - Introduce your project
   - Ask for beta reader volunteers

#### **📧 Afternoon: Beta Reader Search**
1. **Direct Outreach**:
   - Message 10-15 people from writing groups
   - Share your blurb and sample chapter
   - Ask for honest feedback

2. **Goodreads Groups**:
   - Join relevant groups
   - Post about your book
   - Network with potential reviewers

#### **🤝 Evening: Author Connections**
1. **Twitter Outreach**:
   - Follow 50 sci-fi romance authors
   - Like and retweet their content
   - Send 5-10 genuine messages

**👥 Day 3 Goal**: 10+ potential beta readers contacted

---

### **📅 DAY 4: SELF-EDITING SESSION (4-5 hours)**

#### **📖 Morning: Read-Through**
1. **Full Manuscript Review**:
   - Read start to finish
   - Mark inconsistencies
   - Note pacing issues
   - Check character development

#### **✏️ Afternoon: Specific Edits**
1. **Focus Areas**:
   - Romantic tension consistency
   - Scientific accuracy
   - Character voice consistency
   - Plot hole identification

#### **🔧 Evening: Technical Polish**
1. **Tools to Use**:
   - Grammarly (free) for grammar
   - Hemingway App for readability
   - Manual fact-checking

**✨ Day 4 Goal**: Complete first round of self-editing

---

### **📅 DAY 5: PROMOTIONAL CONTENT (3-4 hours)**

#### **📢 Morning: Launch Materials**
1. **Canva Designs** (free tier):

2. **Social Media Templates**:
   - "Cover coming soon" posts
   - "Beta reader needed" graphics
   - "Follow for updates" content

#### **🤝 Afternoon: Cross-Promotion**
1. **Author Collaborations**:
   - Message 5 similar authors
   - Propose shoutout exchange
   - Suggest newsletter swap

#### **📈 Evening: Analytics Setup**
1. **Free Tracking**:
   - Google Analytics on website
   - Instagram Insights activation
   - Twitter Analytics setup

**📊 Day 5 Goal**: Professional promotional materials created

---

### **📅 DAY 6: BETA READER MANAGEMENT (2-3 hours)**

#### **📤 Morning: Distribution**
1. **PDF Creation**:
   - Format manuscript in Google Docs
   - Export as PDF
   - Create feedback form (Google Forms free)

2. **Beta Reader Package**:
   - Send to 5-10 volunteers
   - Include timeline (2-3 weeks)
   - Provide feedback questions

#### **📊 Afternoon: Progress Tracking**
1. **Organization**:
   - Create spreadsheet of beta readers
   - Note feedback deadlines
   - Track responses

#### **🔄 Evening: Engagement**
1. **Community Follow-up**:
   - Thank everyone who responded
   - Share teaser content
   - Build relationships

**📋 Day 6 Goal**: Beta reader process initiated
