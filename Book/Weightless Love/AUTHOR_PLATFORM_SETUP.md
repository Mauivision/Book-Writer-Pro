## 🌐 **WEBSITE SETUP**

### **🎯 Domain & Hosting**
**Recommended Domain**: [yourname].com or weightlesslovebook.com
**Hosting Options**:
- **WordPress.com**: Free basic, $4-25/month for premium
- **Bluehost**: $2.95/month (recommended for authors)
- **SiteGround**: $3.95/month, excellent support

### **📄 Essential Pages**
1. **Home**: Featured book, newsletter signup, social links
2. **About**: Author bio, writing journey, contact info
3. **Books**: Weightless Love details, buy links, reviews
4. **Blog**: Writing tips, behind-the-scenes, sci-fi discussions
5. **Contact**: Contact form, media inquiries
6. **Newsletter**: Dedicated signup page

### **🎨 Design Requirements**
- **Theme**: Professional, sci-fi romance aesthetic
- **Colors**: Deep space blues, silvers, warm golds
- **Mobile Responsive**: Essential for reader access
- **Loading Speed**: Optimized images and code

### **💰 Website Budget**
- **Domain**: $10-15/year
- **Hosting**: $35-100/year
- **Theme**: $0-50 (free options available)
- **Total**: $45-165 first year

- **Frequency**: Bi-weekly during launch, monthly ongoing
