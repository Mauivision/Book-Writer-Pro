**Genre**: Science Fiction Romance / Space Opera

**Heat Level**: Steamy (mature content)

## 📖 **STORY SYNOPSIS**

In the year 2387, Dr. Elena Vasquez is a brilliant xenobiologist stationed on Echo Reef—a remote research station floating in the weightless depths between galaxies. When a damaged alien vessel crashes into their sector, she encounters Commander Kai Torven, a mysterious warrior from a distant world who challenges everything she believes about love, duty, and what it means to find home among the stars.

As Elena works to heal Kai's injuries and understand his alien biology, the zero gravity environment of Echo Reef creates an intimacy unlike anything on solid ground. Every touch is electric, every moment suspended in time and space. But Kai harbors secrets about his mission and his people—secrets that could destroy both their worlds.

When Elena's research reveals a cosmic threat that could annihilate entire star systems, she and Kai must choose between their growing love and their duties to their respective peoples. In the weightless void between worlds, they discover that sometimes the strongest bonds are forged when you have nothing to hold onto but each other.

**Tagline**: *"In the space between worlds, love defies gravity."*
