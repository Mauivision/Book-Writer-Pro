## 📡 **TECHNICAL PRODUCTION SPECS**

### **🎙️ Recording Setup**

#### **Studio Requirements**
- **Microphones**: Condenser mics (AKG C414, Neumann U87)
- **Recording Space**: Sound-treated room with acoustic panels
- **Headphones**: Open-back for natural monitoring
- **Interface**: Multi-channel audio interface (Focusrite, Apogee)

#### **Session Structure**
- **Individual Recording**: Each actor records separately for best control
- **Group Sessions**: Intimate scenes recorded together for chemistry
- **ADR Sessions**: Dialogue replacement for technical issues
- **Music Sessions**: Live recording for organic feel

### **🎛️ Audio Engineering**

#### **Mixing Guidelines**
- **Dynamic Range**: Intimate whispers (-40dB) to dramatic peaks (-12dB)
- **EQ Profile**: Warm midrange for voices, crisp highs for effects
- **Compression**: Gentle compression (2:1 ratio) for consistency
- **Reverb**: Minimal for intimacy, increased for space scenes

#### **Mastering Standards**
- **Loudness**: -16 LUFS (podcast standard)
- **Frequency Response**: Full range with natural rolloff
- **Stereo Imaging**: Wide soundstage for immersive experience
- **Final Format**: 44.1kHz, 320kbps MP3 with metadata
