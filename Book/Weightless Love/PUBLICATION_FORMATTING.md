## 🎨 **INTERIOR FORMATTING SPECIFICATIONS**

### **📝 Text Formatting**
- **Font**: Garamond or similar serif (body text)
- **Size**: 11-12pt for body, 14-18pt for chapter titles
- **Line Spacing**: 1.15-1.2 for readability
- **Margins**: 0.75" all sides (print), 0.5" for ebook
- **Paragraph Indents**: 0.25" first line

### **📖 Chapter Structure**
```
Chapter X: [Chapter Title]

[Chapter Content]

---

*End of Chapter X*

**Word Count**: X,XXX words
**Status**: Complete
**Next**: Chapter X - "[Next Chapter Title]"
```

### **🎭 Special Elements**
- **Scene Breaks**: Centered *** or elegant divider
- **Internal Thoughts**: *Italicized text*
- **Technical Terms**: First mention in italics
- **Dialogue**: Standard quotation marks

### **📊 Page Elements**
- **Headers**: Book title (left), Chapter title (right)
- **Footers**: Page numbers (centered)
- **Chapter Starts**: New page, half-page blank space
