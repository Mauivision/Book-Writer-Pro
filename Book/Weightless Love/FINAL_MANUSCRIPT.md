## BOOK DESCRIPTION

In the weightless void between galaxies, Dr. Elena Vasquez discovers an alien warrior whose touch creates electric sparks and whose heart merges with hers across species boundaries. Commander Kai Torven brings not just advanced technology and ancient threats—but an inexplicable quantum connection that defies physics.

As they fight ancient void creatures threatening multiple worlds, their growing love becomes the most powerful weapon of all. But when cultural differences and interstellar politics threaten to tear them apart, Elena and Kai must choose between their worlds and the love that defies gravity itself.

A steamy science fiction romance where alien biology meets human passion, and love becomes the most powerful force in the universe.
