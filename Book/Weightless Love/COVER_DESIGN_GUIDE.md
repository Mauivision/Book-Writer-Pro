## 🎭 **COVER CONCEPT**

### **📸 Primary Visual Elements**

#### **Main Characters**
- **Elena**: Human scientist in fitted jumpsuit, floating gracefully
- **Kai**: Alien warrior with silver-blue skin, muscular build, protective stance
- **Pose**: Embracing in zero gravity, bodies intertwined, faces close
- **Expression**: Tender, passionate, eyes locked in connection

#### **Setting Elements**
- **Background**: Deep space with stars and nebula
- **Space Station**: Partial view of Echo Reef in background
- **Zero Gravity Effects**: Floating particles, energy fields, weightless movement
- **Lighting**: Soft, romantic lighting with bioluminescent glow

#### **Color Palette**
- **Primary**: Deep space blues (#0B0F2A, #1A1F3A)
- **Accent**: Silver metallics (#C0C0C0, #E8E8E8)
- **Warm**: Golden yellows (#FFD700, #FFA500)
- **Romance**: Soft purples (#9370DB, #DDA0DD)

### **✨ Special Effects**
- **Energy Connection**: Visible sparks or energy field between characters
- **Bioluminescence**: Kai's skin glowing with shifting colors
- **Zero Gravity Particles**: Floating dust motes or energy particles
- **Star Field**: Realistic star patterns with nebula effects

## 📐 **TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS**

### **📏 Dimensions & Formats**

#### **Paperback Cover (6x9)**
- **Trim Size**: 6" x 9" (152mm x 229mm)
- **Bleed**: 0.125" on all sides
- **Spine Width**: ~0.5" (based on 200 pages)
- **Total Size**: 6.25" x 9.25" with bleed

#### **Ebook Cover**
- **Primary**: 1600x2400 pixels (optimal for all platforms)
- **Square**: 1080x1080 pixels (social media)
- **Banner**: 1600x800 pixels (website headers)

#### **Audiobook Cover**
- **Square**: 1400x1400 pixels (Audible requirement)
- **Alternative**: Use ebook cover design

### **🖼️ File Requirements**
- **Format**: High-resolution PDF, PSD, or AI
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI minimum
- **Color Mode**: CMYK for print, RGB for digital
- **Layers**: Organized, editable source files
