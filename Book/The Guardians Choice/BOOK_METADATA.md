### **Tagline**
"Five years after creating the Heartline, the Guardians face their greatest test: a species that wants to destroy everything they've built."

### **Short Description**
Captain Zara Voss and her team have become the first Guardians of the Heartline, a revolutionary network that connects consciousness across the galaxy. But when a mysterious species called the Architects claims to have built the original jump gate network and demands its return, the Guardians must choose between protecting their creation and preserving peace in the universe.
