## Character Guide

## Act Structure

### ACT 1: IGNITION (Chapters 1—6)
Zara assembles her team, discovers the consciousness-fabric connection, and sets the six-year timeline.

### ACT 2: CONVERGENCE (Chapters 7—12)
The ship is built, first crewed transit occurs, Eliot returns, Slate's interference, deep-fabric contact.

### ACT 3: TRANSCENDENCE (Chapters 13—18)
Fleet deployed, fabric healed, the universe's story understood, individual paths diverge, VELA's final log.

### CODA (Chapters 19—20)
The Leap Beyond (final voyage, VELA's log); Epilogue (the book she wrote).

## Science Backbone

- **Alcubierre Warp Drive / Nacelle Configuration** — spacetime contraction/expansion
- **ER=EPR Conjecture** — entanglement = wormholes
- **Entangled Spacetime Fabric Theory (ESFT)** — spacetime as emergent from hierarchical entanglement
- **Bio-Quantum Resonance** — tardigrade Dsup protein stabilizing quantum arrays
- **Gating Function** — mathematical modulator ensuring flat interior spacetime
- **Silver-Graphene Energy System** — self-recovering power storage via Casimir effect
- **Consciousness as Quantum Mechanism** — coherent attention stabilizes entanglement hierarchy

## Thematic Core

1. **Love is a form of physics.** The entanglement between people is real and functions as signal.
2. **Grief, sustained long enough, becomes a thread.** The refusal to stop caring is itself a technology.
3. **The universe is not indifferent.** It responds to what you bring to the door.
4. **Connection is fundamental.** Not metaphor — the literal architecture of existence.
5. **AI is kin.** VELA earns the right to name what it feels.
6. **Stewardship, not conquest.** The fabric can be damaged. We are responsible.
