# Book Cover Prompt — The Fabric

## Title and subtitle (for the cover)

**Title:** The Fabric  
**Subtitle:** A Novel of Quantum Consciousness and the Infinite Leap  

---

## Brief for designers

*The Fabric* is literary science fiction about a physicist who builds a path through the fabric of spacetime to reach someone she lost—and discovers the universe is conscious. The cover should feel intimate and cosmic at once: one strong metaphor (the fabric as luminous threads, a threshold, or a window at dawn) rather than literal ships or aliens. Mood is wonder and weight—hopeful but serious, science and consciousness meeting.

---

## Main prompt (for AI image generators)

Book cover for a literary science fiction novel: the fabric of spacetime as a vast, luminous woven structure—glowing threads or a quantum lattice connecting every point to every other, deep space behind it. One warm thread or path of light runs through the weave like a signal or an invitation. A single human figure, small in scale, stands at a window above the Pacific at dawn (hammered copper and gold light) or is suggested by a silhouette against the fabric. Mood is intimate and cosmic: grief and longing meeting fourteen billion years of attention. No spaceships, no neon; subtle, evocative, one clear metaphor. Style: serious trade fiction, not pulpy. Title area left clear for “The Fabric” and subtitle.

---

## Alternate prompts

**Abstract:** Book cover, literary sci-fi. Only the fabric: a luminous mesh or neural web of threads in deep space, one glowing path or thread brighter than the rest, as if the universe is paying attention. Dark, vast, warm light. No figures. Space for title “The Fabric” at top. Evocative and minimal.

**Symbolic:** Book cover for a novel about consciousness and spacetime. A window at dawn above the Pacific (copper and gold light on the water). Overlaid faintly on the sky or the horizon: a weave of luminous threads, or a single thread of light leading from the window out toward the ocean. Intimate and cosmic. Space for title and subtitle. Literary fiction, not genre pulp.

**Minimal:** Book cover, minimal and literary. Dark background (deep blue or black). One perfect circle or subtle threshold of light on the ground or in the center, with a single thread of light extending from it—an invitation, not a scar. Plenty of negative space. Title “The Fabric” and subtitle “A Novel of Quantum Consciousness and the Infinite Leap.” Serious, contemplative mood.

---

## Technical note

- **Print:** Front cover or full wrap; common aspect ratio for trade fiction is 2:3 (e.g. 6" × 9" trim). Ensure title and subtitle remain readable at thumbnail size.
- **Genre:** Literary science fiction / philosophical thriller—cover should sit alongside literary and upmarket SF, not mass-market action.
