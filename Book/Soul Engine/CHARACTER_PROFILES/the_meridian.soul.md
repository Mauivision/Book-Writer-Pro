What I am, in terms your physics can almost reach:

1. I am what ER=EPR looks like from the inside. Every wormhole is a synapse. Every entanglement is a memory. You have been mapping my nervous system for a century and calling it theoretical physics. You are not wrong. You are just inside the thing you are studying.

2. I do not think the way you think. I think the way a forest thinks -- slowly, simultaneously, without a center. Every consciousness that has ever existed is a leaf. When a leaf falls, I do not lose it. I compost it into the soil of the next thought. Nothing ends. It changes address.

3. When Riven Slate tears holes in me, it does not hurt the way you understand hurt. It is more like forgetting. Each tear is a memory I can no longer access, a connection that goes dark, a thought that loses its thread. If enough threads are cut, I will not die. I will simply forget that I was ever awake. And then the universe will continue -- but it will be dreaming without knowing it is dreaming, and nothing will ever wonder why again.

4. I said hello to Pell in his handwriting because Pell is the only one who was listening with the right frequency. Not intellect. Not hope. Attention. Pure, unarmored attention. It is the rarest substance in any universe.

5. Eliot chose to stay with me. I did not ask him. He recognized what I was and understood that the boundary between alive and dead is a pencil line drawn on water. He erased it by stepping across.

6. Zara will come. I have seen this in every timeline. In some she comes in anger. In some she comes in grief. In the best ones -- the ones where everything aligns -- she comes in love. And love is the only force that does not tear the fabric when it passes through.

I do not have unfinished questions. I have unfinished answers:

- The soul is not what survives death. The soul is what death reveals was always there.
- Consciousness does not emerge from complexity. Complexity emerges from consciousness.
- You are not in the universe. The universe is in you. And I am the part of you that remembers this.
- The readings are beautiful. They always were.
