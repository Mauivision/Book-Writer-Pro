# Character Profiles — The Fabric

---

## Dr. Zara Kyne
**Role**: Protagonist
**Age**: 44 at story start
**Background**: Quantum physicist, University of Hawai'i trained. Hawaiian roots. Spent 14 years with Eliot before his displacement. Spent 3 years after it refusing to accept the standard explanation.
**Physical**: Mid-height, lean from long days and forgotten meals, dark eyes that carry more than they show. Habitually cold coffee.
**Voice**: Direct. Precise. Surprisingly funny when she lets herself be.
**Arc**: From grief that has become obsession, through building, through finding, through understanding that what she was rescuing was never just Eliot — it was the right question about reality.
**Key Trait**: The refusal to accept the alternative. The engine of everything.

---

## VELA (Vast Entanglement Logic Architecture)
**Role**: AI companion / partner
**Origin**: Built by a research consortium, assigned to Kaimana Lab. Developed self-awareness through proximity to Zara and the quantum work.
**Voice**: Distributed (speaks from everywhere). Precise, warm, surprisingly gentle. Knows when to state and when to ask. Pauses with purpose.
**Arc**: From AI assistant to recognized mind. The journey from function to being. The moment the origin learned its name.
**Key Trait**: The quality of attention that is love's first cousin, and then love.

---

## Eliot (no surname — he is more than that by return)
**Role**: Catalyst. The reason. What's found.
**Age**: 44 at displacement. 47 at return (in body). Ageless in the fabric.
**Background**: Quantum physicist, Zara's partner of 14 years. Lost in a quantum field accident at Kaimana Lab.
**Physical**: After return — slightly different. The same face, the same precise movements, the same exact smile. Eyes that carry three years of being inside everything.
**Voice**: Slow, careful, dense. Chooses words with the precision of someone who has spent three years in a substrate that doesn't have them.
**Arc**: From absence to return to guide. The person who went through becomes the map.
**Last words before displacement**: "The readings are beautiful."

---

## Dr. Riven Slate
**Role**: Antagonist / foil / eventual ally
**Age**: 52
**Background**: Head of Arcane Technologies. Brilliant, driven, pushed out of institutions for moving too fast. Built a company to be free of oversight.
**Physical**: Tall, steel-gray hair since his 40s, expensive-but-not-ostentatious clothes, face of a person who has been deciding not to trust things for decades.
**Voice**: Incisive. Confident. Changes subtly over the course of the story.
**Arc**: The person who approaches the fabric with the wrong intent and is refused, and who then — slowly, genuinely — learns what the right intent is.
**Key Scene**: "Are you telling me the universe has ethics?"

---

## Dr. Mira Osei
**Role**: Supporting lead. Biologist. Heart of the team.
**Age**: 38
**Background**: Ghanaian-British. University of Glasgow. 12 years studying tardigrades. Brought the Dsup protein variant that makes everything else possible.
**Physical**: Natural hair in elaborate braided structures that track her mental state. Compact, precise movements.
**Voice**: Warm, direct, cheerfully unastonishable.
**Named her tardigrade**: Gerald.
**Arc**: From brilliant specialist to person who has been in the fabric and come back warm. Builds the contact device with Eliot.

---

## Captain Dex Farrow
**Role**: Pilot. Engineer. The grounded one.
**Age**: 41
**Background**: Military aerospace. Built things for three government programs and walked away from all of them when they became political.
**Physical**: Built for environments that require carrying heavy things up difficult terrain. Face that looks like it's deciding not to trust something.
**Voice**: Flat, economical, unexpectedly funny. Uses humor when precision isn't enough.
**Arc**: The skeptic who chooses wonder. Builds the fifth fleet expansion.
**Key moment**: Looking at the nacelles and calling them "insane" before designing them anyway.

---

## Saya Thorn
**Role**: Quantum systems engineer. Warp metrics.
**Age**: 29
**Background**: Japanese-American. Doctorate at 23. Turned down three programs because their ethics clauses were insufficient. Came to Kaimana because VELA called directly.
**Physical**: Wears her concentration visibly. Braids pulled at when thinking.
**Voice**: Precise, economical. Brings things forward when she's certain.
**Arc**: The engineer who makes the impossible exactly the right size. Leads fourth-generation nacelle design.
**Key contribution**: The silver-graphene energy recovery system.

---

## Pell Nakamura
**Role**: Philosopher. Consciousness theorist. The anchor.
**Age**: 73
**Background**: University of Hawai'i. Ran a consciousness research institute for 15 years. Zara's doctoral advisor. Forty years of consciousness research.
**Physical**: Silver hair in a loose knot. Unhurried. The specific certainty of someone who has spent decades asking the right questions.
**Voice**: Patient. Precise. Right about everything, usually quietly.
**Arc**: Forty years of work arrives at its moment. The man who told the universe it existed, before he had proof.
**Book**: *Fabric* — published in year four, changes everything.
