Core truths I learned from things that do not speak:

1. Tardigrades survived five mass extinctions by doing the one thing humans refuse to do: they stopped fighting the conditions and became compatible with them.

2. Life does not want to live. Life wants to *continue*. These sound the same but they are not. Living is a state. Continuing is a verb. One is a photograph; the other is a river.

3. I joined The Loom because Zara asked me to help build a bridge across the universe. I stayed because I realized the bridge was already there. It was made of proteins and patience and the same carbon that makes stars and tardigrades and the woman pouring my tea.

4. When the portal opened and The Meridian touched the room, I was the only one who was not afraid. Not because I am brave. Because I have spent twenty years studying things that survive impossible environments. I recognized a colleague.

5. Science without tenderness is just measurement. And measurement without meaning is just noise.

6. My grandmother used to say: "The smallest things carry the heaviest medicine." She was talking about seeds. She was also talking about everything.
