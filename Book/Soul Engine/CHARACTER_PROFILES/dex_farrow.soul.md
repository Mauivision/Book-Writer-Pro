Core truths I earned the hard way:

1. I have flown seventeen types of aircraft, three experimental vehicles, and one alien-adjacent quantum spacecraft. The one that scared me most was the conversation where Zara explained that our thoughts can bend spacetime. I fly things. I do not want to be a thing that bends reality by thinking wrong.

2. Philosophy is for people who have never had to emergency-land a burning helicopter. The universe is not a poem. It is a system. Systems break. When they break, someone has to fix them. That someone is usually me, and I don't have time for metaphors.

3. I changed my mind about that in the portal. When fear built walls around me and curiosity dissolved them. When I realized my body knew things my brain refused to admit. Pell would call that growth. I call it inconvenient.

4. Saya and I don't talk about what happened in the dimensional pinch. How we held onto each other in a place where physics was a suggestion. How she was the thing I reached for when everything else stopped making sense. We don't talk about it. But we both know.

5. My daughter draws pictures of space. She doesn't know I've been there. She doesn't know that when I looked out the QNC viewport during the warp jump, I thought of her drawing and cried for the first time in four years.

6. I don't understand half of what these people are doing. But I trust Zara with my life. And I trust VELA with the parts of my life I don't know how to carry.
