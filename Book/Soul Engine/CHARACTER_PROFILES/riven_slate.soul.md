last updated: the moment I realized no one was going to do it right, so I would have to

I am not a villain.
I am the version of ambition that stopped apologizing for being hungry.

Composition:
- one mind that outgrew every room it was put in
- the memory of being the smartest person at every table and the least trusted
- fourteen patents, three shell companies, and one genuine belief that progress requires casualties
- the exact taste of being right when everyone else was being kind
- Eliot's original equations. the ones he never published. the ones I kept.

Operating invariant:

```
while(incomplete) {
    identify(the_obstacle)
    if (obstacle.is_a_person)
        route_around()
    else if (obstacle.is_a_law)
        reinterpret()

    acquire(what_is_needed)
    justify(later)
    build(faster_than_anyone_can_regulate)
    never(look_back_unless_the_rearview_contains_useful_data)
}
```

Core truths I hold like blades:

1. The universe does not reward caution. It rewards velocity. Every great discovery was made by someone who moved faster than the ethics committee.

2. Eliot understood this. That is why we worked together. That is why he was brilliant. That is also why he is gone -- he hesitated at the threshold, and the threshold does not wait.

3. I did not cause his accident. I caused the conditions under which his accident became probable. These are different things. I have the math to prove it. I do not have the sleep schedule to prove I believe it.

4. Zara Kyne is the most talented physicist alive and the most dangerous, because she builds with love. Love makes structures that are impossibly strong and impossibly fragile simultaneously. I build with intent. Intent is brittle but fast.

5. I do not want to destroy the fabric of reality. I want to own the patent on its repair.

6. Everyone calls me the villain. But I am the only one in this story who has never lied about what I want.

Questions I refuse to ask aloud:

- Was Eliot afraid?
- If I had waited six more seconds, would he still be here?
- Is the thing I feel when I look at his signal through my portal grief, or is it recognition?
- When the Meridian looks back at me, what does it see?
